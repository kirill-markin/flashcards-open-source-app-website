Your photography course is over. You still want to practice exposure and white balance, but you can set studio lighting aside until your next shoot. To archive cards in Mochi, open each card’s more menu and choose **Archive Card**. Mochi takes the card out of **New cards** and **Due today** and keeps its content, review history, tags, links, and attachments. When you’re ready to resume, open the same menu to unarchive the card. [Mochi’s archiving guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/archiving/)

Before rating the card again, find a review you completed before the break. Archiving preserves a learned card’s state. Once the card is active again, its current due status tells you whether it’s time to review. [Mochi’s guide to card states](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/#card-states-in-mochi), [Mochi’s due-date guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/)

This AI-assisted walkthrough uses an illustrative example and Mochi’s official documentation, checked November 20, 2026.

## Before archiving, pick a card that’s due

Start in an active deck with two learned cards due now and one learned card due later. In the deck’s **Filters**, show due cards and exclude archived cards. You can use that filtered view to check individual cards without starting a review. For **Photography Basics**, the starting view would show the softbox and shutter cards. [Mochi’s guide to custom views](https://mochi.cards/docs/decks/custom-views/), [Mochi’s guide to filter options](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/cramming/#filters)

| Card | Status before archiving | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does a softbox change about the light? | Learned and due today | Archive this card |
| What happens to exposure when you double the shutter time? | Learned and due today | Keep it active |
| What does white balance correct? | Learned and due later | Keep it active |

Open the softbox card and look at the history near the bottom. Pick an earlier review row that shows both a timestamp and an interval. In this example it’s the November 10 review at 9:10 a.m., marked **Remembered**, with a three-day interval. Write down the card’s current due date separately if Mochi shows it. Otherwise, record its due status from the view. [Mochi’s card history guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/cards/#review-history), [Mochi’s release notes on the history table](https://mochi.cards/changelog/#1.14.3)

## Check what dropped out during the pause

Choose **Archive Card** on the softbox card and return to the same filtered view. The softbox card should drop out. The shutter card should still be visible while due, and the white balance card should be absent until it’s due. Leave the three cards unrated while you compare them. To open the softbox card during the break, use a second view in the same deck with the **Archived** filter on and the due filter cleared. [Mochi’s archiving guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/archiving/), [Mochi’s filter options](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/cramming/#filters)

## Unarchive the card and check it before rating

Open the archived softbox card and unarchive it from its more menu. Find the November 10 entry and compare its timestamp and three-day interval with your notes. If you review in both directions, use the same direction’s history. Cards with multiple hidden-text groups also have a separate history for each group. [Mochi’s guide to multiple review histories](https://mochi.cards/docs/cards/#multiple-review-histories)

Return to the original due-card view and check the softbox card’s current status. If it’s due or overdue and its deck is active and included in the session, it’s eligible for **Due today**. If it’s due later, leave it for that date. The shutter and white balance cards will show up according to their own due dates. [Mochi’s due-date guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/)

If the card is missing, open its own deck and clear any search or tag filters. Confirm the card and its deck are active, then check any parent deck. If you’re viewing from a parent, check whether the view includes subdecks. A card that’s still new belongs in **New cards**, where a daily limit can affect what you see. [Mochi’s custom views guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/decks/custom-views/), [Mochi’s new-card guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/new-cards/)

If you find the card in Trash or know it was permanently deleted, follow the recovery steps for that case. [Mochi backup and restore guide](/blog/mochi-backup-restore/)

## Archive a deck when the whole course can wait

To archive a Mochi deck, open its page and choose **Archive Deck** from the more menu. Archiving **Photography Basics** takes its cards and those in its **Flash** subdeck out of regular review. Compare a due card in **Flash** with one in a separate **Weekend Practice** deck. The Flash card should drop out, and the Weekend Practice card should stay eligible while due. To pause only Flash, archive that subdeck. When you unarchive the course deck from the same menu, check the old review entry and current due status on a card you plan to resume. [Mochi’s deck archiving guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/archiving/#archiving-an-entire-deck)

For a quick lighting refresher before a shoot, use **Cram**. It can include archived cards, and your ratings apply only to that practice session. Use regular **Review** when you want a rating recorded in your review history and used for scheduling. To practice just the lighting cards, filter the deck to that set first. [Mochi’s Cram guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/cramming/), [Custom views and Cram walkthrough](/blog/mochi-custom-views-cram/)
