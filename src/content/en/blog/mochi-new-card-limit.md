To see fewer new cards after a big import, open **Settings → Review Settings → Limit new cards per day** in Mochi. Enter a number and click **Save Changes**. This caps how many cards Mochi shows in **New cards** each day. Cards already scheduled for review follow their due dates, which is why **Due today** can still look just as full. [Mochi’s new-card limit instructions](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/new-cards/#limiting-new-cards-per-day), [Mochi’s due-date guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/)

This AI-assisted walkthrough uses an illustrative example based on Mochi’s public documentation and forum, checked November 22, 2026.

## Where to look after you change the limit

**Inbox** combines **New cards**, **Due today**, and **Re-reviews**. To see the effect of the limit in one deck, open that deck’s **New cards** queue. If the top-right button says **Review**, use its dropdown to switch to **New cards**. Mochi supports both a global limit and a per-deck limit. [Mochi’s Inbox release notes](https://mochi.cards/changelog/#1.21.0), [Mochi’s new-card guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/new-cards/#learning-new-cards), [Mochi’s release notes on limits](https://mochi.cards/changelog/#1.11.16)

Cards you create or import start as new by default. The cap controls how many of those cards you see in the queue. The rest stay in your deck. You can still add a card to reviews manually. To do it from **New cards**, reveal the answer and choose the action that adds the card to your schedule. Mochi calls this **Remembered** in its docs. The first review typically comes up the same day, so leave time to see any cards you learn again. [Mochi’s maintainer on daily limits](https://forum.mochi.cards/posts/212/daily-review-limits), [Mochi’s new-card guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/new-cards/), [Mochi’s due-date guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/)

## Try it with four cards

Before the day you plan to compare the queues, set up an empty practice deck with no subdecks or deck-specific limit. Create *la gare* first and add it to reviews. Once it appears in the deck’s **Review** queue, leave it due and create three more cards, *le quai*, *le billet*, and *le départ*. Leave those three in **New cards**. [Mochi’s new-card guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/new-cards/#learning-new-cards), [Mochi’s deck review instructions](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/#reviewing-a-subset-of-cards)

On the day of the comparison, run both checks before adding any new cards to reviews in any deck. Leave all four cards unrated during the comparison.

1. Write down your current global limit. Use the current setting if it already shows all three new cards. Otherwise, set the limit high enough to show all three and save. In the practice deck’s **New cards** queue, use the arrow buttons to step through the queue and write down the three distinct prompts. Open that deck’s **Review** queue and check that the due prompt is *la gare*.
2. Set the global limit to **1** and save. Reopen **New cards** in the same deck and use the arrows again to count distinct prompts. Expect just one of the three new cards. Reopen **Review** and look for *la gare* again.
3. Go back to the deck’s card list and check for all four prompts. Restore the original limit and save.

## When the due list is the problem

A cap for cards already due is a separate feature request in Mochi’s forum. For today’s session, open the deck you most want to keep studying and choose **Review**. Set a timer for a block you can fit today, say 10 minutes. When you stop, the due cards you haven’t reviewed stay eligible for your next session. [Mochi’s due-date guide](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/due-today/), [Mochi’s forum request for a due-review cap](https://forum.mochi.cards/posts/412)

While you catch up, pause adding new cards to reviews. When due reviews fit your usual study time again, introduce a few cards and see how long the next several sessions take. If some material can wait for a future course or trip, you can archive those cards and keep their review history.
