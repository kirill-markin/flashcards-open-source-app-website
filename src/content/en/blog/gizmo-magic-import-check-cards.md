The flashcard says last check-in is 7:00 p.m. The notes for this fictional ceramics studio say 6:30. Keep your notes open after Gizmo Magic Import and you can catch a slip like that before you learn the wrong time.

## Start with one section

In a Gizmo deck, open **Add → Magic Import**. Choose **Notes** to paste text or **PDF** to upload a file. Gizmo generates cards and highlights words in green for **Memorise** quizzes. For longer documents, Gizmo recommends importing smaller sections. [Gizmo's Magic Import guide](https://help.gizmo.ai/en/articles/15647624-what-is-magic-import)

Keep one section of the source beside the cards. If it came from a PDF, jot down the page number. These are the notes for the studio example.

> The community ceramics studio takes drop-ins on Wednesdays. Last check-in for Wednesday drop-ins is 6:30 p.m. Bring a current membership card to get the member rate. Reserve a pottery wheel at least 24 hours ahead.

Imagine opening the deck and seeing these four mock cards. The bold words stand in for Gizmo's green highlights.

| Card | Front | Back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What day does the ceramics studio take drop-ins? | **Wednesday** |
| 2 | When is the studio's drop-in day? | **Wednesday** |
| 3 | What time is last check-in for Wednesday drop-ins? | **7:00 p.m.** |
| 4 | What do you need to bring to get the member rate? | Bring a current membership card to get the **member rate**. |

## Find a card for each fact you want to learn

Start from the notes. Point to the card that asks about each fact you've picked. The studio notes have four facts, and the deck has four cards. Cards 1 and 2 both ask about Wednesday, so the wheel reservation has no question.

Rewrite card 2 to ask how far ahead you need to reserve a wheel. If a fact is missing from your own deck and there’s no duplicate to reuse, add a card for it.

## Read each answer against the source

Go from the cards back to the notes. Card 3 needs **6:30 p.m.** Keep **last** in the question so it still asks about the cutoff for Wednesday drop-ins. Card 4 needs the word **current**, and the new wheel card needs **at least** because 24 hours is a minimum. Add the subject to any question that would be hard to place with the notes closed. [Guide to writing better flashcards](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/)

From the deck, open the three-dot menu on the card and choose **Edit** to change the front or back. If you've already started a Memorise quiz, return to the deck to make the edit. [Gizmo's card editing guide](https://help.gizmo.ai/en/articles/13761411-how-do-i-edit-or-manage-my-cards)

## Check what the green words ask you to recall

The back of card 4 already has the right information. Cover the old highlight on paper and you get “Bring a current membership card to get the ___.” The prompt gives you the thing you meant to remember.

In the editor, click the green words in **member rate** to clear their highlights. Then drag across **current membership card** to highlight the phrase. With the new highlight covered, the paper prompt reads “Bring a ___ to get the member rate.” Now it asks you to recall the current membership card. [Gizmo's highlighting guide](https://help.gizmo.ai/en/articles/13166301-how-does-highlighting-work)

Here's the edited set, with the intended highlights in bold.

| Card | Front | Back |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What day does the ceramics studio take drop-ins? | The ceramics studio takes drop-ins on **Wednesdays**. |
| 2 | How far ahead do you need to reserve a pottery wheel at the ceramics studio? | Reserve a pottery wheel **at least 24 hours** ahead. |
| 3 | What is the last check-in time for Wednesday drop-ins at the ceramics studio? | Last check-in for Wednesday drop-ins is **6:30 p.m.** |
| 4 | What do you need to bring to get the ceramics studio's member rate? | Bring a **current membership card** to get the member rate. |

## Take the checked cards into Memorise

Start reviewing once each fact you picked has a card, the answers match the source, and the green highlights cover what you want to recall. In a short Memorise quiz, look at what each prompt asks you to remember. Gizmo can blank out just some of the highlighted words when it first quizzes you on a new card. If card 4 doesn't come up or part of the phrase is still visible, leave that prompt check pending until a later quiz asks you for the whole phrase. [How Gizmo uses highlights in quizzes](https://help.gizmo.ai/en/articles/13166301-how-does-highlighting-work)

For your next import, put the page or section heading at the top of a note. Give each fact a line with its card number and any fix. The studio example would have a line that reads “Current membership card for the member rate. Card 4. Highlight moved to ‘current membership card.’ Quiz prompt pending.”
