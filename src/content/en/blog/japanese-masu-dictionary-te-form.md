おきます can be 起きます (“get up”) or 置きます (“put something down”). The first becomes **起きる (おきる)** and **起きて (おきて)**. The second becomes **置く (おく)** and **置いて (おいて)**. In kana, the polite forms look identical. Use the sentence to figure out which verb you have, then use its group to change the ending.

The **dictionary form** is the form you look up in a dictionary. The **て form** also includes forms ending in で, such as 読んで (よんで). You can use it with ください to make a request, as in 読んでください (“please read”). [Japan Foundation’s dictionary-form guide](https://www.kyozai.jpf.go.jp/kyozai/material/BTS00066/ja/render.do) · [Japan Foundation’s examples with てください](https://www.kyozai.jpf.go.jp/kyozai/material/BTS00149/ja/render.do)

## From ます to both forms

**Group 2 (ichidan).** Swap ます for る to get the dictionary form or て to get the て form. 教えます (おしえます, “teach”) gives 教える (おしえる) and 教えて (おしえて). 見ます (みます, “see”) is also Group 2. It has the same みます ending as 読みます (よみます, “read”), which is Group 1. Learn the group along with each verb.

**Group 1 (godan).** Drop ます, then replace the last kana using the chart. For 呼びます (よびます, “call someone over”), start with よび. Change び to ぶ for 呼ぶ (よぶ), or replace び with んで for 呼んで (よんで).

| Kana before ます | Dictionary ending | て-form ending |
| --- | --- | --- |
| い | う | って |
| ち | つ | って |
| り | る | って |
| に | ぬ | んで |
| び | ぶ | んで |
| み | む | んで |
| き | く | いて |
| ぎ | ぐ | いで |
| し | す | して |

**行きます (いきます, “go”)** follows Group 1 for the dictionary form, 行く (いく). Its て form is **行って (いって)**. Compare it with the regular 書きます → 書く → 書いて (かきます → かく → かいて). [Irodori conjugation chart](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/assets/data/elementary01/pdf/Y_L01.pdf#page=18)

Hold the *t* for an extra beat in いって. [Small っ reading and spelling practice](/blog/japanese-small-tsu/)

**Group 3 (irregular).** します (“do”) gives する and して. The same change works in 勉強します (べんきょうします, “study”), giving 勉強する and 勉強して. 来ます (きます, “come”) gives 来る (くる) and 来て (きて). Watch the reading of 来 as you switch forms. [Japan Foundation’s verb conjugation reference](https://www.kyozai.jpf.go.jp/kyozai/material/BTS00012/ja/render.do)
