## Xhumana nathi

Ukuze uthole usizo ngomkhiqizo, usizo nge-akhawunti, noma ngemibuzo emayelana nokuhlolwa kwe-App Store, thumela i-imeyili ku-[kirill+flashcards@kirill-markin.com](mailto:kirill+flashcards@kirill-markin.com).

## Ukususa i-akhawunti

Ohlelweni lwe-iOS, ungasusa i-akhawunti yakho esingathiwe ku-`Settings > Delete Account > Delete my account`. Uma udinga usizo ngaleyo nqubo, xhumana nosizo nge-imeyili.

## Umthombo ovulekile nemibiko yobuchwepheshe

I-Nibomo inomthombo ovulekile. Ngemibiko yamaphutha, izinkinga zokuzisingathela, noma izingxoxo zobuchwepheshe, ungasebenzisa futhi [i-repository ye-GitHub](https://github.com/kirill-markin/flashcards-open-source-app) nesilandeleli sayo sezinkinga.

## Ukuzisingathela

Uma ungathandi ukusebenzisa i-beta yefu esingathiwe, ungazisingathela isitaki esifanayo esinomthombo ovulekile bese uqhuba isevisi kwingqalasizinda yakho.

## Okulindelekile ngezimpendulo

Usizo lunikezwa ngokwenza okusemandleni ngesikhathi se-beta. Sizama ukuphendula ngokushesha okukhulu, kodwa izikhathi zokuphendula aziqinisekisiwe.
