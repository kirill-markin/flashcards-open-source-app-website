## Isevisi nomhlinzeki

I-Nibomo iyithuluzi le-spaced repetition namakhadi okufunda elinomthombo ovulekile. Le Migomo ilawula kuphela iwebhusayithi esingathiwe, izinhlelo zokusebenza, i-API, nesevisi ye-MCP ehlinzekwa yi-SAMO DANNI EOOD, inombolo yenkampani 207395566, i-VAT BG207395566, ikheli elibhalisiwe bulv. Maritza 154, entrance D, floor 6 #14, 4018, Plovdiv, Bulgaria. I-Nibomo yadalwa ngu-Kirill Markin.

Isevisi esingathiwe ihlinzekwa njengoba injalo futhi njengoba itholakala ngesikhathi se-beta.

## Iminyaka

Kufanele okungenani ube neminyaka engu-13 ukuze usebenzise isevisi esingathiwe. Uma umthetho wendawo ohlala kuyo udinga ukuthi ube mdala kunalokho, kusebenza iminyaka edingwa yilowo mthetho. Uma ungaphansi kweminyaka engu-18, kufanele ube nemvume yomzali noma yomgcini osemthethweni ukuze usebenzise isevisi.

## I-akhawunti yakho

Wena unesibopho sokufinyelela ku-akhawunti yakho ye-imeyili, kumakhodi okuqinisekisa, kumaseshini, nakumininingwane yokungena ye-API, kanye nomsebenzi owenziwa ngakho. Nikeza ulwazi olunembile, gcina imininingwane yokungena iyimfihlo, futhi usazise uma ukholwa ukuthi i-akhawunti yakho ingenwe ngabangagunyaziwe.

## Okuqukethwe kwakho nedatha

Phakathi kwakho nathi, wena ugcina amalungelo onawo kumakhadi, amafayela, imibono, nokunye okuqukethwe okuthumelayo. Usinika imvume elinganiselwe edingekayo ukuze sisingathe, sicubungule, sidlulise, senze ikhophi yokulondoloza, futhi sibonise lokho okuqukethwe kuwe nakubantu owabelana nabo ngendawo yokusebenza, ukuze sikwazi ukuqhuba isevisi.

Ukucubungula kwethu idatha yomuntu siqu kuchazwe kuNqubomgomo Yobumfihlo. Uma ususa i-akhawunti yakho, okuqukethwe endaweni yokusebenza okwabelwana ngayo kungase kuqhubeke kutholakala kwamanye amalungu ayo.

## Ukusetshenziswa okwamukelekile

Ungasebenzisi isevisi emisebenzini engekho emthethweni; ungephuli amalungelo omunye umuntu; ungazami ukufinyelela ku-akhawunti noma kudatha yomunye umsebenzisi; ungaphazamisi, ungahloli, noma ungalayishi ngokweqile isevisi; ungagwemi imikhawulo yenani lezicelo noma izilawuli zokuphepha; ungasabalalisi i-malware; noma ungathumeli okuqukethwe okungekho emthethweni, okulimazayo, noma okuhlukumezayo ngezici ze-AI noma zokwabelana.

Singamisa okwesikhashana noma sinqamule ukufinyelela lapho kudingeka ngokunengqondo ukuze sivikele abasebenzisi noma isevisi, siphenye ukwephulwa kwemigomo, noma sithobele umthetho.

## Izici ze-AI

Izici ze-AI ezisingathiwe ungazisebenzisa uma uthanda, futhi zisebenzisa i-OpenAI. I-telemetry ye-AI ehlobene nazo icutshungulwa nge-Langfuse ukuze kulungiswe amaphutha futhi kuhlaziywe izinga lesevisi. Okukhishwa yi-AI kungase kungabi nembile, kungaphelele, noma kungafaneleki. Wena unesibopho sokuhlola okukhishwayo ngaphambi kokuthembela kukho, ukwabelana ngakho, noma ukusebenzisa izinguquko.

Uma ugunyaza iklayenti le-AI langaphandle ukuthi lisebenzise isevisi ye-MCP noma i-Agent API, lelo klayenti nomhlinzeki walo wemodeli bacubungula idatha oyicelayo ngaphansi kwemigomo yabo. Asinasibopho ngokusebenza noma ngokukhishwa yiklayenti langaphandle.

## Ukubhalisela nge-Stripe

Lapho ukukhokha nge-Stripe kutholakala, i-Nibomo Premium inikezwa ngentengo eyisisekelo ye-USD 6.99 ngenyanga, kuhlanganise nentela esebenzayo. I-Stripe Adaptive Pricing ingabonisa intengo ngohlobo lwemali lwasendaweni; i-Stripe ibonisa inani lokugcina nohlobo lwemali yokukhokha ngaphambi kokuthi uqinisekise. Ukuze uthenge, kufanele ungene ku-akhawunti enekheli le-imeyili; izivakashi kufanele ziqale zixhumanise i-imeyili.

Amakhasimende e-Stripe afanelekayo athola isikhathi sokuzama samahhala sezinsuku eziyisikhombisa, esidinga ukuthi ufake indlela yokukhokha. Isikhathi sokuzama sitholakala kanye kuphela ekhasimendeni ngalinye le-Stripe; izikhathi zokuzama zangaphambilini ze-Apple noma ze-Google azithinti ukufaneleka. Uma ungakhanseli ngaphambi kokuba isikhathi sokuzama siphele, indlela yakho yokukhokha izokhokhiswa imali yokubhalisela kwanyanga zonke. Ngemva kwalokho ukubhalisela kuzivuselela ngokuzenzakalela inyanga ngayinye kuze kukhanselwe. Izikhumbuzo zesikhathi sokuzama zisho intengo eyisisekelo yanyanga zonke; i-Stripe ibonisa inkokhelo yokugcina ngohlobo lwemali yokukhokha.

Sebenzisa [Izilungiselelo zokubhalisela](https://app.nibomo.com/settings/subscription) ukuze uvule iphothali yezinkokhelo ye-Stripe futhi uphathe noma ukhansele ukubhalisela kwakho. Ukukhansela okujwayelekile kuqala ukusebenza ekupheleni kwesikhathi sokuzama samanje noma senkathi ekhokhelwe yamanje; ukufinyelela kuqhubeka kuze kube yileso sikhathi. Ukuthenga nge-Stripe akukhanseli noma yikuphi ukubhalisela okuhlukile kwe-Apple noma kwe-Google futhi akuthathi indawo yokufinyelela kwempilo yonke osunakho.

Uma ususa i-akhawunti yakho ye-Nibomo kunoma yiliphi iklayenti elisekelwayo, ngaphambi kokusulwa kwedatha kukhanselwa ukuvuselelwa kwesikhathi esizayo kwakho konke ukubhalisela kwe-Nibomo nge-Stripe okuxhunyaniswe naleyo akhawunti, futhi kuvalwe amaseshini okukhokha e-Nibomo avulekile. Uma ukukhansela kungeke kuqinisekiswe, ukususwa ngeke kuqedwe; zama futhi noma uxhumane nosizo. Ukususwa kwe-akhawunti akukhanseli ukubhalisela kwe-Apple noma kwe-Google. Ukukhansela nokususwa kwe-akhawunti akukhiphi ngokuzenzakalela imali ebuyiselwayo noma ikhredithi ehambisana nesikhathi esisele. Akukho kule Migomo okunciphisa amalungelo abathengi aphoqelekile, kuhlanganise nanoma yimaphi amalungelo asebenzayo okuhoxa noma okubuyiselwa kwemali.

## Ukutholakala nezinguquko

I-beta esingathiwe ihlinzekwa ngokwenza okusemandleni futhi ingashintsha, ime okwesikhashana, noma iyekwe ngaphandle kwesaziso. Asiqinisekisi ukutholakala okungaphazanyiswa, ukulondolozwa kwazo zonke izikhophi zasendaweni noma ezisingathiwe, noma ukuthi zonke izici zizohlala zitholakala. Gcina ikhophi ezimele yokuqukethwe ongeke ukwazi ukulahlekelwa yikho.

## Ukuyeka ukusebenzisa

Ungayeka ukusebenzisa isevisi esingathiwe futhi ususe i-akhawunti yakho noma nini. Imibandela okufanele, ngenxa yohlobo lwayo, iqhubeke ngemva kokunqanyulwa, kuhlanganise naleyo ephathelene namalungelo, isibopho, nokuthobela umthetho, izoqhubeka isebenza.

## Izinhlelo ezizisingathelwe

Ikhodi yomthombo itholakala ngaphansi kwelayisensi ye-MIT. Ukufakwa okuzisingathelwe kulawulwa yilowo okuqhubayo, hhayi yi-SAMO DANNI EOOD, futhi akulawulwa yile Migomo. Oqhuba lokho kufakwa nguyena onesibopho sokutholakala nokuphepha kwalokho kufakwa, ukuphathwa kwedatha kukho, kanye nemigomo esebenza kukho.
