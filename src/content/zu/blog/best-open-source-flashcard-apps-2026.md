I-Anki isewuhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olunekhodi evulekile olufanele abantu abaningi ngo-2026. Umbuzo uba mnandi uma ikhodi evulekile ingeyona yodwa imfuneko ongazimisele ukuyekethisa kuyo.

Mhlawumbe udinga uhlelo lwesiphequluli kuseva yakho. Noma inqwaba yamakhadi ongayifunda njenge-Markdown nje. Noma uhlelo lwamanothi oluyimfihlo olwakha amakhadi okufunda. Lezo zidingo zikuholela emikhiqizweni ehlukene; ukuba nekhodi etholakala ku-GitHub akwanele ukwenza isinqumo.

Uhlelo lwedeskithophu olunekhodi evulekile lungahambisana nolwe-iPhone olunekhodi engashicilelwe. I-container ye-Docker ingasingatha isixhumi sesiphequluli ngaphandle kokuvumelanisa nezinhlelo ezifakiwe kumadivayisi. Ukungenisa kungabuyisa amagama kodwa kulahle izifanekiso, imidiya neminyaka yomlando wokubuyekeza eyenza iqoqo libe wusizo.

Amaphrojekthi ayisithupha aphumelele kulokhu kubuyekeza. Ngiqhathanise ikhodi yawo namalayisensi, inguqulo yakamuva ezinzile, idatha yasendaweni, uhlelo lwezikhathi zokubuyekeza, ukuvumelanisa, ukuthutha kusuka ku-Anki, ukukhipha idatha nezingxenye ongazisingathela zona ngempela. Lowo mkhawulo wokugcina ubaluleke ngaphezu kwalokho okuvezwa izinhlu eziningi zezici.

> **Ubudlelwano bami nomkhiqizo:** NginguKirill Markin, futhi ngithuthukisa i-[Nibomo](https://nibomo.com/), enye yezinhlelo eziyisithupha ezingezansi. Ikhosombe layo elinelayisensi ye-MIT lihlanganisa uhlelo lwewebhu, izinhlelo ezifakwa kumadivayisi, i-backend, ukuvumelanisa nengqalasizinda. Angiyibekanga endaweni yokuqala. I-Anki iyisinqumo sokuqala esiphephe kakhudlwana, i-Mnemosyne inendlela yokuthutha kusuka ku-Anki esenesikhathi eside ikhona, kanti ezinye izinketho lapha zilula kakhulu ukuzisebenzisa nokuzinakekela.

**Amaqiniso ahlolwe:** 5 Septhemba 2026. Izinguqulo ezinzile zihlukaniswe nomsebenzi osekhona kuphela egatsheni eliyinhloko lekhodi.

## Yini ebaliwe njengohlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olunekhodi evulekile

Ngisebenzise imibandela emine:

1. **Umsebenzi oyinhloko wokufunda unekhodi eshicilelwe nelayisensi ecacile yekhodi evulekile.** Uhlu lwezihlanganisi ezizungeze uhlelo oluyinhloko olungashicilelwe alubalwa.
2. **I-spaced repetition isebenza manje.** Ukuyibeka ohlelweni lwekusasa noma ukuba nemodi evamile yemibuzo akwanele.
3. **Kukhona inguqulo ekhishiwe noma indlela esemthethweni yokufaka uhlelo echazwe ngokucacile.** Ama-commit amasha ewodwa awenzi uhlelo lokuhlola lufanele ukunconywa ngokuphepha.
4. **Imithombo esemthethweni ichaza ngokwanele ukuthi idatha iphathwa kanjani ukuze lokho kuhlolwe.** Bengidinga izimpendulo eziqondile mayelana nokugcina ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ukuvumelanisa, ukungenisa/ukukhipha noma ukusingatha—hhayi isithembiso esingacacile sokuthi abasebenzisi “bangabanikazi bedatha yabo.”

Izinkanyezi ze-GitHub bezingewona umbandela. Zivuza ubudala nokwaziwa kwephrojekthi ngendlela efanayo nokufaneleka komkhiqizo. Kodwa ukuvuthwa kwephrojekthi kusabalulekile. I-Anki, i-Mnemosyne ne-SiYuan zinezinguqulo nezindlela zokusebenza ezisunguliwe. I-Recall ne-Essentialist zifakwe ngenxa yokufaneleka kwazo ezidingweni ezithile ngoba ukusebenza kwezinguqulo zazo ezikhishiwe kuchazwe ngokwanele ukuze kunconywe ukusetshenziswa okuthile.

Ukuthi “isanakekelwa” nakho kudinga ukuhlolwa okubili. Inguqulo enethegi ikutshela lokho abasebenzisi abangakufaka; igatsha eliyinhloko likutshela ukuthi iphrojekthi iya kuphi. I-Essentialist iyisibonelo esicacile. Inguqulo yayo ezinzile ichaza i-SM-2, kanti igatsha layo lamanje lichaza i-FSRS. Ithebula elingezansi libhala i-SM-2.

## 1. I-Anki iyisinqumo sokuqala esiphephe kakhulu

I-Anki ihamba phambili ezintweni ezingakhangi kangako kodwa ezibalulekile. Ingamela izinhlobo zamanothi eziyinkimbinkimbi, ikhiqize amakhadi ahlobene enothini elilodwa ngezifanekiso, igcine imidiya neqoqo, futhi ithwale iminyaka yedatha yezikhathi zokubuyekeza. Inguqulo ezinzile yedeskithophu yalokhu kuhlola ingu-[26.08.1](https://github.com/ankitects/anki/releases/tag/26.08.1). Inguqulo entsha engu-26.09b2 imakwe njenge-beta, ngakho akuyona okuyisisekelo lapha.

Ukutholakala kwekhodi evulekile kuyahlukahluka. [Ikhosombe ledeskithophu lisebenzisa i-AGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/ankitects/anki/blob/26.08.1/LICENSE), ngaphandle kwezingxenye ezithile ezifakiwe ezibaliwe. [I-AnkiDroid](https://github.com/ankidroid/Anki-Android) iyiphrojekthi ehlukile ye-Android enekhodi evulekile. I-AnkiMobile ne-AnkiWeb ziyimikhiqizo esemthethweni, kodwa ikhodi yazo ayikho kulawo makhosombe. Incazelo ebanzi iku-[Ingabe i-Anki inekhodi evulekile?](/blog/is-anki-open-source/).

Izinhlelo ezifakiwe zigcina amaqoqo kudivayisi, ngakho ukubuyekeza okuvamile kusebenza ngaphandle kokuxhumeka. I-AnkiWeb isebenza ku-inthanethi. Uma ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuyikona okunqumayo, [Ingabe i-Anki isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi?](/blog/does-anki-work-offline/) ihlukanisa okuhlala kudivayisi nokulinda ukuvumelanisa.

I-Anki isekela [i-FSRS nohlelo lwayo lwakudala lwezikhathi](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html). Amafomethi ayo okukhipha idatha ayisiqalo esinamandla kakhulu sokuthutha kuleli qembu. [I-COLPKG iqukethe iqoqo lonke nezikhathi zokubuyekeza](https://docs.ankiweb.net/exporting.html), kanti okukhiphwa nge-APKG kungahlanganisa imininingwane yezikhathi nemidiya uma ukhetha lezo zinketho. I-Anki iphinde ingenise umbhalo, amaphakheji e-Anki nezizindalwazi ze-Mnemosyne 2.0.

Lelo phakheji elinemininingwane eminingi aliqinisekisi ukungenisa okuphelele kolunye uhlelo. Uhlelo olwamukelayo kusadingeka luqonde izifanekiso, imithetho yokukhiqiza amakhadi, izinkomba zemidiya nezinkambu zezikhathi ezingaphakathi. Lumane lunemininingwane eminingi yokusebenza nayo kunefayela le-CSV.

[Iseva esemthethweni ozisingathela yona](https://docs.ankiweb.net/sync-server.html) inomsebenzi omncane ngamabomu. Ivumelanisa izinhlelo ze-Anki ezihambisanayo; ayinikezi i-AnkiWeb, ukubuyekeza esipheqululini noma indawo yokuphatha ama-akhawunti. Ngokuzenzakalela ilalela nge-HTTP engabethelwe, futhi umhlahlandlela uncoma ukuyigcina kunethiwekhi yasendaweni noma ukufaka i-VPN noma i-HTTPS reverse proxy phambi kwayo. Izinguqulo zohlelo neseva nazo kufanele zihlale zihambisana.

Khetha i-Anki uma ukugcina iqoqo linembile, izifanekiso, izengezo noma ukusekelwa kwamadivayisi amaningi kuza kuqala. Bheka kwenye indawo kuphela uma umkhawulo othile—njengendawo yesiphequluli ozisingathela yona noma ikhodi yonke yezinhlelo zeselula eshicilelwe—ubaluleke kakhulu.

## 2. I-Mnemosyne igxile ekufundeni ngedatha esekhompyutheni

I-Mnemosyne yakhelwe ukufunda kukhompyutha yedeskithophu, futhi indlela esebenza ngayo iyakubonisa lokho. Ayilethi isizindalwazi solwazi noma ipulatifomu yefu kanye nayo. Uthola isizindalwazi sasendaweni, indlela evamile ye-spaced repetition, uhlelo oluhambisana nayo lwe-Android lokubuyekeza, neseva yokuvumelanisa engasebenza kudeskithophu noma emshinini ongenasixhumi esibonakalayo.

Inguqulo yayo yakamuva ezinzile isengu-[2.11 yangoNovemba 2023](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/releases/tag/2.11). Ikhosombe lithole izinguquko ngo-2026, kodwa lokho akwenzi lezo zinguquko zibe inguqulo ezinzile efakekayo. Hlola u-2.11 ezinhlelweni zokusebenza ohlela ukuzigcina eminyakeni embalwa ezayo.

Ilayisensi nayo idinga okungaphezu kwebheji elilodwa. [Incazelo yamalayisensi empandeni yekhosombe](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/blob/master/LICENSE) inikeza i-openSM2sync i-LGPL v3, bese ibeka eminye imigomo kwezinye izingxenye ze-Mnemosyne. [Ilayisensi yohlelo oluyinhloko](https://github.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/blob/master/mnemosyne/LICENSE) isebenzisa i-AGPL v3 kanye nombandela owengeziwe odinga ukuba igama elithi Mnemosyne lihlale libonakala ngokucacile emisebenzini esuselwe kuyo; indlela eliboniswa ngayo ixoxwa nabanakekeli. Funda lowo mbhalo ngaphambi kokusabalalisa inguqulo oyishintshile.

[Uhlelo lwe-Android luyabuyekeza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kodwa alukwazi ukuhlela amakhadi](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/help/android-client). Amanye amadivayisi angasebenzisa iseva yokubuyekeza esipheqululini eqalwa ohlelweni lwedeskithophu, kodwa ikhasi elisemthethweni lezici lixwayisa ngokuthi iseva ayinazo izici zokuphepha. Iyindawo ewusizo yokufinyelela nge-LAN, hhayi uhlelo lwewebhu olulungiselwe ukuvulelwa wonke umuntu.

Ukuthutha kuyisizathu esinamandla kakhulu se-Mnemosyne sokungamane uhlale ne-Anki. Ikhasi elisemthethweni lezici lichaza [ukungenisa kwe-Anki okuphelele, okuhlanganisa izinhlobo zamakhadi ezenziwe ngokwezifiso nedatha yokufunda](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/features). [Ukuvumelanisa kwayo okwakhelwe ngaphakathi](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/help/syncing) kuhlanganisa amakhadi nedatha yokufunda futhi kungasebenzisa umshini owulawulayo.

Umyalo ovamile wokukhipha idatha ungakukhohlisa uma uwusebenzisa njengesipele. Wenzelwe ukwabelana ngamakhadi akhethiwe futhi ushiya ngaphandle idatha yakho yokufunda. Ukuze uthuthe noma ubuyisele uhlelo lonke, [umhlahlandlela wamakhompyutha amaningi](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/help/mnemosyne-and-multiple-computers) uthi kopisha yonke ifolda yedatha.

I-Mnemosyne iyindlela enekhodi evulekile enamandla kakhulu lapha esikhundleni se-Anki, egxile ekufundeni. Okufanele ukwamukele ukuthi izinguqulo ezinzile azikhishwa njalo, ukuhlela okulinganiselwe kuselula, nendawo yesiphequluli edinga umngcele wenethiwekhi ocacile.

## 3. I-SiYuan ifanele uma amanothi eyisisekelo sohlelo lwakho

I-SiYuan iwuhlelo lokuphatha ulwazi olubeka ubumfihlo kuqala, olunamakhadi okufunda ngaphakathi kwesakhiwo esifanayo samabhulokhi nemibhalo. Lokho kuwusizo uma amanothi akho ekhiqiza okufanele ukubuyekeze. Kodwa ingaba yinkimbinkimbi ngokungadingekile uma ufuna umugqa wamakhadi kuphela.

[Ikhosombe le-AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/siyuan-note/siyuan) lixhumanisa isixhumi esibonakalayo, i-kernel, izinhlelo zeselula, ungqimba lwedatha nengxenye ye-FSRS. Inguqulo [v3.8.2](https://github.com/siyuan-note/siyuan/releases/tag/v3.8.2) iyona ezinzile ehloliwe lapha. Izinhlelo zedeskithophu nezeselula zigcina indawo yokusebenza kudivayisi futhi ziyaqhubeka nokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.

Ukuvumelanisa akuyona ingxenye yohlelo lwamahhala lokugcina idatha kudivayisi. [Ikhasi elisemthethweni lamanani](https://b3log.org/siyuan/en/pricing.html) linikeza ukuvumelanisa okusemthethweni okubethelwe kusukela komunye umkhawulo kuya komunye ngokubhalisela ukukhokha, kanti izici ze-Pro ezikhokhelwayo zengeza ukuhlanganisa nendawo yakho yokugcina ye-S3 noma ye-WebDAV. Iphrojekthi iphinde ixwayise ngokufaka indawo yokusebenza esetshenziswayo kufolda evamile yokuvumelanisa amafayela, ngoba ukuhlela ngesikhathi esisodwa kungonakalisa noma kubhale phezu kwedatha.

I-Docker isebenzisa uhlelo lwesiphequluli lwangempela, kodwa ayiphenduki iseva yokuvumelanisa yezinhlelo ezifakiwe. [Imibhalo ye-Docker ye-v3.8.2](https://github.com/siyuan-note/siyuan/blob/v3.8.2/README.md#docker-hosting) ithi izinhlelo zedeskithophu nezeselula azikwazi ukuxhuma kuyo. I-Docker iphinde isuse ukungenisa kwe-Markdown nokukhipha i-PDF, i-HTML ne-Word. Leyo miyalo ikhona ohlelweni olufakiwe olubanzi, ngakho ukukopisha uhlu lwezici olujwayelekile ohlelweni lokufaka i-Docker kungadukisa.

Angitholanga singenisi esisemthethweni se-APKG. I-SiYuan ingathutha i-Markdown namafomethi ayo edatha, kodwa iqoqo le-Anki lidinga ukwakhiwa kabusha okuhleliwe.

Khetha i-SiYuan uma isizindalwazi solwazi siwumkhiqizo oyinhloko, namakhadi kufanele abe ngaphakathi kwaso. Uma ufuna okuthatha indawo ye-Anki ngokuqondile, i-Mnemosyne ne-Anki zinemikhawulo yokuthutha ecace kakhudlwana.

## 4. I-Nibomo ivula izingxenye eziningi zohlelo—futhi kudingeka uziphathe

I-Nibomo ishicilela izingxenye eziningi kakhulu zomkhiqizo kulokhu kuqhathanisa. Ikhosombe elilodwa le-MIT lihlanganisa uhlelo lwewebhu, izinhlelo ze-iOS ne-Android, i-backend, isevisi yokungena, ukuvumelanisa, uhlelo lokuphatha, izinguquko zesakhiwo sesizindalwazi nengqalasizinda ye-AWS. Inguqulo ezinzile esetshenziswe lapha ingu-[v1.23.0](https://github.com/kirill-markin/flashcards-open-source-app/releases/tag/v1.23.0). Umsebenzi olandelayo egatsheni eliyinhloko awubalwa njengokusebenza osekukhishiwe.

[Isakhiwo](/docs/architecture/) sibeka ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuqala, kodwa ukuthi “ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi” kusho okuhluke kancane ohlelweni ngalunye. Uhlelo lwewebhu lugcina idatha yalo eyisisekelo ku-IndexedDB. I-iOS isebenzisa i-SQLite, kanti i-Android isebenzisa i-Room phezu kwe-SQLite. Izinguquko zibhalwa kudivayisi bese zifakwa emgqeni wokuthumela ngaphambi kokuvumelanisa. Lokho kubhekana nokunqamuka kokuxhumeka; akwenzi indawo yesiphequluli yokugcina idatha ihlale unomphela noma kususe isidingo sokuhlola ukuvula uhlelo lusuka esimweni sokuvalwa ngokuphelele kudivayisi ngayinye.

Iphakheji le-ZIP le-Nibomo liyifomethi yokuthutha okuqukethwe, hhayi isipele se-akhawunti. Ku-v1.23.0, [isakhiwo sephakheji](https://github.com/kirill-markin/flashcards-open-source-app/blob/v1.23.0/apps/backend/src/workspacePackages/types.ts) sithwala okuqukethwe kwengaphambili nengemuva, amathegi, uhlobo lwekhadi, imethadatha yomthombo nemethadatha yephakheji; imidiya ebhekiselwe kuyo ifakwa eceleni. Asithwali ukwakheka kwezinqwaba, umlando wokubuyekeza, isimo se-FSRS, izilungiselelo zendawo yokusebenza noma ama-akhawunti.

Akukho singenisi se-APKG ku-v1.23.0. [Indlela echaziwe yokuthutha i-Anki nge-TXT/CSV](/blog/migrate-from-anki-txt-export-open-source-flashcards/) isebenzisa umbhalo okhishiwe ukwakha amakhadi kabusha futhi idinga ukuhlolwa ngumuntu. Izifanekiso, isimo sezikhathi, ukwakheka kwezinqwaba nemidiya efakiwe akudluli ngaleyo ndlela ngokuzenzakalela. Iyindlela enengqondo yenqwaba elula yombhalo kodwa ayifanele iqoqo elenziwe kakhulu ngokwezifiso.

[Umhlahlandlela wokuzisingathela](/docs/self-hosting/) nawo ucacile. Ekusetshenzisweni kwangempela kusetshenziswa uhlelo lwe-AWS CDK oluhlanganisa i-RDS, i-Cognito, i-API Gateway ne-Lambda, i-S3 ne-CloudFront, izimfihlo, izexwayiso namakhophi ayisipele. Ukulungiswa kwe-Cloudflare DNS, i-imeyili ye-Resend ne-Sentry kungaphandle kwe-AWS. I-Docker Compose isebenzisa indawo yokuthuthukisa yasendaweni; ayilona iphakheji elisekelwayo lokusetshenziswa kwangempela. Abaphathi abafuna izinhlelo eziyimfihlo ze-iOS noma ze-Android bazakha futhi bazisabalalise ngokuhlukile.

Khetha i-Nibomo uma ukuba nekhodi yonke yewebhu, yamadivayisi ne-backend kukufanele umsebenzi wokuyiphatha. Khetha i-Anki noma i-Mnemosyne uma ukugcina iqoqo elikhona kuyimfuneko enzima kakhulu.

## 5. I-Recall ibukeka ingeyesimanje, kodwa funda ngesingenisi sayo ngokucophelela

I-Recall iyiphrojekthi entsha kunazo zonke ezinconyiwe ohlwini oluyinhloko. Ifakwe ngoba [i-v1.3.0](https://github.com/Madlezz/Recall/releases/tag/v1.3.0) inezinguqulo zedeskithophu ezinenombolo, i-PWA efakekayo, ukugcinwa kwedatha yasendaweni okucacile, i-FSRS, ukukhipha idatha nendlela echaziwe yokuvumelanisa ozisingathela yona.

Uhlelo lwedeskithophu olunelayisensi ye-MIT lusebenzisa i-SQLite; i-PWA isebenzisa i-IndexedDB. Akukho kulokhu okudinga i-akhawunti, futhi iphrojekthi ithi ukuthumela idatha yokusetshenziswa kuvaliwe ngokuzenzakalela. Izinguqulo zedeskithophu zitholakala ku-Windows, macOS ne-Linux.

Isingenisi se-APKG siwusizo, kodwa amazwi e-README athi “umlando wokubuyekeza” athembisa okuningi kunalokho okwenziwa yikhodi yenguqulo enethegi. [Ikhodi yesingenisi ye-v1.3.0](https://github.com/Madlezz/Recall/blob/v1.3.0/src-tauri/src/anki_import.rs) ayifundi ilogi yokubuyekeza ye-Anki. Ifunda isimo samanje sekhadi, isikhawu, inani lokuphindaphinda nokukhohlwa, kanye nokuzinza nobunzima be-FSRS lapho i-Anki ikugcinile. Kumakhadi amadala angenazo lezo zinkambu ze-FSRS, i-Recall iyazilinganisela ngamanani e-SM-2.

Ukuguqula okuqukethwe nakho kunemikhawulo ebalulekile. Isingenisi sisebenzisa izinkambu ezimbili zokuqala zenothi njengengaphambili nengemuva, esikhundleni sokuphinda sakhe izinhlobo zamanothi nezifanekiso ze-Anki. Sigcina amagama ezinqwaba namathegi. Sikhipha amafomethi avamile ezithombe futhi sibhale kabusha izinkomba zazo, kodwa seqe umsindo neminye imidiya. Njengoba isingenisi singumyalo we-Tauri, ukuthutha nge-APKG ngokuqondile kuyisici sedeskithophu, hhayi ese-PWA yesiphequluli.

Lokho kungcono kakhulu kunokwakha kabusha ngombhalo nje, kodwa akuligcini iqoqo linembile ngokuphelele. Hlola ama-cloze anezikhala zokugcwalisa, amakhadi asuka enothini elilodwa, izinkambu ezengeziwe, i-HTML/CSS, izithombe, umsindo, izinsuku zokubuyekeza namanothi aphindaphindiwe ngaphambi kokwethemba ukuthutha okukhulu.

I-Recall inezindlela ezimbili zokuvumelanisa. Uhlelo lwedeskithophu lungabhala ikhophi yesimo sangaleso sikhathi kufolda ephethwe yi-Dropbox, i-Drive noma elinye ithuluzi lokuvumelanisa amafayela. Iseva yokudlulisa ongayikhetha isebenzisa i-Cloudflare Worker ne-R2 bucket. Ngokwe-[ncazelo yokuvumelanisa yenguqulo enethegi](https://github.com/Madlezz/Recall/blob/v1.3.0/docs/SYNC.md), izinhlelo zibethela la makhophi esimo nge-AES-GCM ngaphambi kokuwalayisha; iseva ibona umbhalo obethelwe, hhayi idatha yamakhadi noma ukhiye. Izibuyekezo zisebenzisa i-optimistic concurrency futhi ziphinde zizame kanye uma kuba nokungqubuzana. Nokho, zihlanganisa amakhophi aphelele esimo esikhundleni sokuhlanganisa izinkambu ngazinye. Ayikho iseva yomphakathi ekhokhelwa abanakekeli—uyifaka ngokwakho bese ufaka i-URL yayo.

Ukukhipha i-JSON nezingobo ze-Recall kukunika indlela yokuphuma. Buyisela okukodwa kuphrofayela engenalutho ngaphambi kokukubiza ngesipele.

Khetha i-Recall uma ufuna uhlelo lwesimanje lwedeskithophu/lwe-PWA olugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala futhi ungamukela iphrojekthi entsha nesingenisi esigcina isimo esiwusizo, kunokuba sigcine lonke uhlelo lwe-Anki.

## Ukuzisingathela kuphelela kuphela ngemva kokuhlola ukubuyisela

Le mikhiqizo engenhla isebenzisa elithi “ukuzisingathela” ezintweni ezihluke kakhulu:

- I-Anki ne-Mnemosyne zisebenzisa **izinsizakalo zokuvumelanisa**, kanti izinhlelo ezifakiwe zihlala ziyindawo yokufunda.
- I-SiYuan Docker isebenzisa **uhlelo lwesiphequluli** izinhlelo ezifakiwe ezingakwazi ukulusebenzisa njengeseva yazo yokuvumelanisa.
- I-Recall isebenzisa **iseva yokudlulisa amakhophi esimo abethelwe**, hhayi i-PWA uqobo.
- I-Nibomo ifaka **uhlelo oluphelele lwewebhu ne-backend**, kanti izinhlelo zamadivayisi zakhiwa ngokuhlukile.
- I-Essentialist **ayinaseva**; okulawulayo ngamafayela asendaweni.

Uma lokho sekucacile, hlola ingxenye abaphathi abavame ukuyihlehlisa:

1. Yakha amakhadi, faka imidiya, qedela ukubuyekeza bese uvumelanisa usuka ezinhlelweni ezimbili.
2. Gcina zonke izizindalwazi ezichaziwe, ama-bucket okugcina izinto, amafayela asendaweni, izimfihlo namanani okulungisa uhlelo.
3. Buyisela ku-akhawunti engenalutho, emshinini ongenalutho noma endaweni efakwe ngokuzimela.
4. Qhathanisa isibalo samakhadi, imidiya, umlando wokubuyekeza, isimo sokuthi yini okufanele ibuyekezwe, ukungena nokuvumelanisa kwezinhlelo.
5. Thuthukisa ikhophi ebuyiselwe ibe inguqulo entsha bese uqedela omunye umjikelezo wokubuyekeza.

Uma ukwakha kabusha kusancike emshinini omdala, unesevisi esebenzayo. Awukabi nesipele esiqinisekisiwe.

## Imibuzo evame ukubuzwa

### Iluphi uhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olunekhodi evulekile olungcono kakhulu ngo-2026?

I-Anki iyisinqumo sokuqala esingcono kakhulu kubafundi abaningi. Ihlanganisa ukwakheka kweqoqo okuthuthukiswe isikhathi eside, i-FSRS, ukusekelwa kwamadivayisi amaningi namafomethi ayo anemininingwane eminingi kakhulu yokwenza isipele nokukhipha idatha. Okufanele ukukhumbule ukuthi izinhlelo zayo ezisemthethweni ze-iOS nezewebhu azihlanganisiwe ekhosombeni lekhodi evulekile yedeskithophu, futhi iseva yayo ozisingathela yona inikeza ukuvumelanisa kunokufunda esipheqululini.

### Iyiphi indlela enekhodi evulekile engcono kakhulu esikhundleni se-Anki?

I-Mnemosyne iyindlela egxile ekufundeni esenesikhathi eside isunguliwe, futhi ichaza ngokusemthethweni ukungenisa izinhlobo zamakhadi ezenziwe ngokwezifiso nedatha yokufunda ye-Anki. I-Recall ibukeka ingeyesimanje futhi ingenisa amafayela e-APKG ngokuqondile kudeskithophu, kodwa iguqula izinkambu ezimbili zokuqala zenothi, igcine isimo samanje kuphela sezikhathi, ingenise izithombe kodwa hhayi umsindo, futhi ayithwali yonke ilogi yokubuyekeza.

### Ngingazisingathela i-Anki?

Yebo, ungasebenzisa iseva esemthethweni yokuvumelanisa ye-Anki ezinhlelweni ezihambisanayo. Kodwa ayithathi indawo ye-AnkiWeb ngokuzisingathela: ayinayo indawo yokufunda esipheqululini.

### Ingabe ikhodi evulekile isho ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi?

Cha. Ikhodi evulekile ichaza ilayisensi nokufinyelela kukhodi. Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuncike ekutheni uhlelo luyigcina kuphi idatha nokuthi yiziphi izenzo ezidinga isevisi. Okuphambene nakho kuyiqiniso: uhlelo lungagcina idatha kudivayisi ngaphandle kokushicilela ikhodi yalo eyinhloko.

### Ingabe ukuzisingathela kuqinisekisa ukuthi idatha iyathutheka?

Cha. Ukuzisingathela kulawula ukuthi isevisi isebenza kuphi. Ukuthutheka kuncike ekukhipheni idatha, emakhophini ayisipele aphelele nasekubuyiseleni okuhlolile ngempela. Isizindalwazi esikuseva yakho sisengaba nzima ukusithutha, futhi inqwaba ye-Markdown efundekayo isengashiya ngaphandle isimo sokubuyekeza esigcinwe eceleni kwayo.

## Engikuncomayo

Gcina noma khetha **i-Anki** ngaphandle uma omunye wemikhawulo yayo udala inkinga yangempela. Khetha **i-Recall** ukuze uthole uhlelo lwesimanje olugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala, ngemva kokuhlola imikhawulo yalo yokuguqula idatha. Khetha **i-Essentialist** uma i-Markdown elula nokungafinyeleli nhlobo kunethiwekhi kubaluleke ngaphezu kokuvumelanisa.

Uhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olunekhodi evulekile olungcono kakhulu akulona ikhosombe elinohlu olude kakhulu lwezici. Yilolo elinikeza ikhodi, ukugcina idatha ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ukuthutha, ukuvumelanisa, ukusingatha nokubuyisela ngendlela ehambisana nohlelo ozimisele ukuluphatha ngempela.
