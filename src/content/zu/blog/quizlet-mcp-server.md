Ngomhla ka-12 Septhemba 2026, imibhalo yomphakathi ye-Quizlet ayichazi iseva ye-MCP noma i-API yomphakathi yonjiniyela abangazibhalisela ukuyisebenzisa. Uma ubufuna i-URL ye-Quizlet MCP ongayinamathisela ku-Claude, ku-Codex, noma kwelinye iklayenti le-MCP, okwamanje ayikho indlela yokuyisetha echazwe emibhalweni yomphakathi ye-Quizlet.

I-Quizlet inalo uhlelo olusemthethweni ku-ChatGPT. Lungaguqula ingxoxo ye-ChatGPT ibe isethi entsha ye-Quizlet, kodwa leyo ndlela ethile yokusebenza ayiyona iseva ye-Quizlet MCP engasetshenziswa ngokuphindaphindiwe. Uma udinga ukuthi i-ejenti ifinyelele njalo kumakhadi akhona, indlela esebenzayo iwukuthekelisa umbhalo kusethi oyidalile, ugcine ikhophi yokuqala ingashintshiwe, uhlole okudluliswayo, bese usebenzisa isistimu yamakhadi okufunda esekela ngokucacile ukufunda nokubhala ngama-ejenti.

## Uhlelo lwe-ChatGPT, ukuthekelisa, ne-MCP kwenza imisebenzi ehlukahlukene

I-[Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/docs/getting-started/intro) iyindinganiso evulekile yokuxhumanisa izinhlelo ze-AI nezinhlelo zangaphandle. Iseva ye-MCP ivamise ukunikeza amaklayenti ahambisanayo ikheli lokuxhumana elaziwayo kanye nesethi echaziwe yamathuluzi angawabiza.

[Isimemezelo esisemthethweni se-Quizlet nge-ChatGPT](https://quizlet.com/blog/quizlet-comes-to-chat-gpt) sichaza indlela ehlukile. Ufaka uhlelo lwe-Quizlet ku-ChatGPT, uqale umyalo ngo-`@Quizlet`, ubuke kuqala amakhadi okufunda akhiqiziwe ngaphakathi kwe-ChatGPT, bese uvula isethi ku-Quizlet ukuze uyilungise ngokwakho futhi ufunde ngayo.

Lokho kuyasiza uma umsebenzi uwukuthi “guqula le ngxoxo ye-ChatGPT ibe isethi ye-Quizlet.” Isimemezelo asishicileli ikheli elijwayelekile le-Quizlet MCP, asinikezi i-Claude noma i-Codex ukufinyelela eqoqweni lakho le-Quizlet, futhi asinikezi imininingwane yokuqinisekisa ukufinyelela yonjiniyela engasetshenziswa ngokuphindaphindiwe.

Lokhu akusho lutho ngengqalasizinda yangasese noma yangaphakathi ye-Quizlet. Kuchaza kuphela indlela yokuxhumana i-Quizlet eyenza itholakale emibhalweni yayo yomphakathi. Umkhawulo obanzi wokufinyelela konjiniyela uchazwe kwesithi [Ingabe i-Quizlet Inayo i-API Yomphakathi ngo-2026?](/zu/blog/quizlet-api/).

Uhlelo lwe-Quizlet ku-ChatGPT lusingatha inqubo eyodwa yokwakha. Ukuthekelisa kusingatha ukudlulisa okunomkhawulo. I-MCP isingatha ukufinyelela okuphindaphindekayo kusistimu enikeza amathuluzi ngamabomu. Lezi zindlela azikwazi ukuthatha indawo yezinye.

## Khetha indlela emfushane kunazo zonke esekelwayo

Uma uhlelo olusemthethweni lwe-ChatGPT seluwufanele umsebenzi, lusebenzise. Asikho isizathu sokwengeza isistimu yesibili ukuze nje wakhe isethi eyodwa ye-Quizlet ngengxoxo eyodwa.

Uma udinga kuphela ikhophi eyisipele noma ukuhambisa kanye, sebenzisa ukuthekelisa kwe-Quizlet. Uma udinga ukuthi i-ejenti ibuye kusasa, ithole iqoqo lamakhadi, yengeze amakhadi, futhi iwalungise, udinga indlela yokufunda nokubhala echazwe emibhalweni kusistimu owathuthela kuyo. Kuze kube yilapho i-Quizlet ishicilela leyo ndlela, leyo nqubo ephindaphindekayo ayikwazi ukuba ukuxhumana okuqondile okusekelwayo ku-akhawunti yakho ye-Quizlet.

Ngesofthiwe eyenziwe ngokukhethekile ukuze isebenzise idatha ye-Quizlet, yima kuwo lowo mkhawulo. Isicelo esithunyelwa yisiphequluli, umtapo omdala owenza kube lula ukusebenzisa uhlelo, noma ukuhlanganiswa umuntu akwakhe ngokuhlaziya indlela uhlelo olusebenza ngayo akuyona into ethatha indawo yemibhalo yamanje yonjiniyela.

## Dlulisa isethi oyidalile ngendlela ongabuyela emuva kuyo

[Imiyalelo esemthethweni yokuthekelisa ye-Quizlet](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034345672-Exporting-your-sets) ithi umdali kuphela ongathekelisa isethi, futhi angakwenza kuwebhusayithi kuphela. Inqubo ithi **More → Export → Copy text** (Okwengeziwe → Thekelisa → Kopisha umbhalo). Ihlanganisa amatemu nezincazelo; i-Quizlet ithi izithombe azikwazi ukuthekeliswa futhi amasethi akopishiwe awafaneleki ukuthekeliswa.

Ngesethi oyidalile, ukudlulisa ngokucophelela kuyinqubo emfushane:

1. Sebenzisa ukuthekelisa kuwebhusayithi ye-Quizlet bese unamathisela umphumela efayeleni lombhalo ongenakho ukufometha.
2. Gcina ikhophi ye-UTF-8 engathintwanga ngaphambi kokushintsha izihlukanisi, ukunqamuka kwemigqa, noma amagama.
3. Hlela enye ikhophi, hhayi umbhalo wokuqala othekelisiwe.
4. Hlola isampula esuka ekuqaleni, phakathi, nasekupheleni, kanye nezincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi nezinhlamvu ezingezona ezesiLatini.
5. Ngenisa noma unamathisele ikhophi osebenza ngayo, ubuke kuqala izinhlangothi zangaphambili nezingemuva zamakhadi ambalwa, bese ugcina amakhadi ngemva kwalokho kuphela.
6. Gcina isethi ye-Quizlet nefayela lokuqala kuze kube yilapho iqoqo lamakhadi lapho uwathuthele khona selivuliwe futhi lahlolwa.

Ukuhlola okuphelele kwezihlukanisi, i-CSV, namafayela kuchazwe kwesithi [Indlela Yokuthekelisa Amasethi e-Quizlet ngo-2026](/zu/blog/how-to-export-quizlet-sets-and-turn-them-into-fsrs-flashcards/). Kulula ukugeja umkhawulo obalulekile: ukuthekelisa kudlulisa umbhalo wamakhadi, hhayi izithombe ze-Quizlet, amafolda, umlando wokufunda, izinga lokuwazi kahle amakhadi, noma uhlelo lokuwabuyekeza.

Yingakho kubalulekile ukudlulisa ngendlela ongabuyela emuva kuyo. Uma incazelo enemigqa eminingi iba amakhadi amabili noma isihlukanisi sigudluza izinkambu, ifayela lokuqala likunikeza umbhalo ongashintshiwe ongaqhathanisa nawo.

## Okungasetshenziswa yi-Claude ne-Codex ngemva kokuthekelisa

I-Nibomo ishicilela ikheli lokuxhumana le-MCP elikuseva ekude lapha:

`https://mcp.nibomo.com/mcp`

> [Xhuma ku-Claude](https://claude.ai/directory/nibomo) · [Imibhalo](/zu/docs/mcp-connector/)

[Imibhalo yesixhumi sayo se-MCP](/zu/docs/mcp-connector/) ichaza amathuluzi ayisishiyagalombili: `list_workspaces`, `sql_query` yokufunda, `sql_execute` yokubhala, `get_guide` yeziqondiso eziyinkomba, kanye ne-`next_review_card`, `reveal_answer`, ne-`submit_review` zokubuyekeza. Amaklayenti e-MCP asebenzisana nomsebenzisi angathola imvume yokufinyelela nge-OAuth. Ama-ejenti asetshenziswa kutheminali wona angaqala [emibhalweni ye-Agent API](/zu/docs/api/) bese esebenzisa inqubo ye-HTTP eshicilelwe.

`get_usage_limits` — ifunda kuphela imininingwane yohlelo lwe-akhawunti, imikhawulo, nokusetshenziswa kwamanje kwe-AI kwanyanga zonke; ayifundi futhi ayishintshi amakhadi.

Lokhu akuguquli i-Nibomo ibe isixhumi se-Quizlet. Inqubo esekelwayo yile:

1. thekelisa umbhalo kusethi ye-Quizlet oyidalile;
2. gcina ifayela lokuqala bese uhlola ikhophi osebenza ngayo;
3. hlola ukuthi umbhalo ubekwe ezinkambini ezifanele zamakhadi ngaphambi kokuwagcina ku-Nibomo; bese
4. uxhuma indawo yokusebenzela ye-Nibomo evelayo kuklayenti elihambisanayo ukuze lifunde futhi libhale esikhathini esizayo.

Ku-Claude, izinyathelo zesixhumi zichazwe kwesithi [Indlela Yokuxhuma i-Nibomo ku-Claude nge-MCP](/blog/how-to-connect-flashcards-to-claude-with-mcp/). I-Codex namanye ama-ejenti angasebenzisa indlela ye-MCP noma ye-Agent API echazwe emibhalweni futhi esekelwa indawo asebenza kuyo.

I-Nibomo ayikwazi ukufunda i-URL ye-Quizlet, ukwenza ikhophi efana ne-akhawunti ye-Quizlet, ukuvumelanisa izinguquko ezenziwa kamuva ku-Quizlet, noma ukubuyisa izithombe nomlando wokufunda okushiywe ngaphandle ekuthekeliseni. Yakha amakhadi ayo nesimo esisha sohlelo lokuwabuyekeza isebenzisa izinto okhetha ukuziletha.

Uma unquma ukuthi lokho okuzuzayo nokulahlekelayo kuyakufanelekela yini nangale kokufinyelela kwama-ejenti, bheka [ukuqhathaniswa kwe-Quizlet nenye indlela enekhodi yomthombo evulekile](/blog/quizlet-alternative/).

## Gcina imvume yokufinyelela incane kunobubanzi bomsebenzi

Isivumelwano sokuxhumana esishicilelwe emphakathini sisusa okunye ukuqagela ekuhlanganiseni izinhlelo, kodwa asenzi zonke izenzo ze-MCP ziphephe. I-OAuth iqinisekisa ubuqiniso bokuxhumana. Ayiqinisekisi ukuthi ikhadi elikhiqizwe yi-AI lilungile, ayigcini idatha yamakhadi ebuyisiwe ingaphakathi kwesevisi yamakhadi okufunda, futhi ayinqumi ukuthi ukuhlela kuyahambisana yini nenhloso yakho.

I-Nibomo ihlukanisa imisebenzi yokufunda namathuluzi ayo okubhala: `sql_execute` yamakhadi namaqoqo amakhadi, ne-`submit_review` yokubuyekeza. Imininingwane yokuqinisekisa ukufinyelela ye-OAuth isahlanganisa yonke imisebenzi yesixhumi, ngakho ukuvinjwa kwamathuluzi nendlela yokucela imvume kuncike kuklayenti. Qala ngendawo yokusebenzela encane engenayo idatha ebucayi. Vumela i-ejenti ifunde kuphela idatha edingekayo emsebenzini, wenze ukuthi ukubhala ngakunye kudinge imvume uma iklayenti likusekela lokho, futhi uhlole indawo yokusebenzela noshintsho oluhlongozwayo ngaphambi kokulwamukela.

Ungaxhumi izinto zakho zokufunda zangasese uze uhlole imigomo yeklayenti le-AI yokugcinwa nokuphathwa kwedatha. Ekudluliseni kokuqala, umthetho olula wanele: gcina umbhalo wokuqala othekelisiwe, ubuke kuqala amakhadi ambalwa, uvumele ukubhala okulinganiselwe, bese uqinisekisa umphumela ohlelweni owathuthela kulo ngaphambi kokwenza okwengeziwe.
