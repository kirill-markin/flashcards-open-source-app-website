I-Mochi iqala ngombhalo we-Markdown, hhayi ngefomu elivamile lengaphambili nengemuva. Faka umugqa onodwi abathathu bese umbhalo uba nezinhlangothi ongazibuyekeza. Ungawushiya uyinothi, uwuxhumanise nelinye ikhadi, noma uwugcine kungobo yomlando ukuze usatholakale lapho usesha ngaphandle kokungena emgqeni wokubuyekeza.

Leso sehlukanisi esincane sichaza ukuthi **amakhadi okufunda e-Mochi** afanele bani. I-Mochi ifanele umuntu ofuna amanothi ne-spaced repetition ohlelweni olulodwa olugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala, ikakhulukazi uma i-Markdown, izixhumanisi ezibuyela emanothini axhumanisayo, nokubuyekeza okulula ngokuthi Ngikhumbulile/Ngikhohliwe kuyindlela ayijwayele. Ayikhangi kangako kumuntu osenesikhathi esebenzisa i-Anki, oneqoqo elincike ezinhlotsheni zamakhadi ezikhiqizwa ngokuzenzakalela, ku-HTML/CSS eyenziwe ngokwezifiso, ku-JavaScript, ezingezeni noma ezilawulini ezinemininingwane zezikhathi zokubuyekeza.

Uma usebenzisa idivayisi eyodwa, uhlelo lwamahhala lungaphezu nje kwesivivinyo: akudingeki ubhalise, futhi imibhalo ye-Mochi ithi ungayisebenzisa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngaphandle komkhawulo. Okufanele ukukhumbule ukuthi ukuvumelanisa phakathi kwamadivayisi kudinga **uhlelo lwe-Pro olungu-US$5 ngenyanga**. Kumsebenzisi we-Anki, izindleko ezinzima kakhulu yilokho okulahleka lapho ethutha. I-Mochi ingangenisa iphakheji ye-Anki nomlando wayo wokubuyekeza, kodwa ayikwazi ukugcina zonke izifanekiso, izitayela, izikripthi, izilungiselelo zezikhathi noma indlela izengezo ezisebenza ngayo.

## Ukubuyekeza kafushane

- **Khetha i-Mochi** uma ufuna amanothi e-Markdown namakhadi ndawonye, ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti kudivayisi eyodwa, izixhumanisi ezibuyela emanothini axhumanisayo, nesinqumo esinezinketho ezimbili ekubuyekezeni.
- **Khetha i-Anki** uma udinga izinhlobo zamanothi ezithuthukiswe isikhathi eside, izifanekiso ze-HTML/CSS, izengezo, ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala okusingathiwe, izilinganiso ezine zokubuyekeza noma izilawuli ezibanzi ze-FSRS.
- **Ungakashintshi** uma usuvele ubuyekeza njalo futhi ungakwazi ukusho ukuthi iyiphi inkinga endleleni yakho yokusebenza ezoxazululwa yi-Mochi. Isixhumi esibonakalayo esisha asisona isizathu esanele sokufaka engozini iminyaka yedatha yezikhathi zokubuyekeza namakhadi enziwe ngokwezifiso.
- **Hlola ngaphambi kokuthutha** uma iqoqo lakho lesikhathi eside seliku-Anki. I-Mochi yamukela amafayela e-`.apkg` futhi ingangenisa umlando wokubuyekeza, kodwa iguqula i-HTML ibe yi-Markdown bese isusa i-CSS ne-JavaScript.

## Okuyisisekelo sendlela isixhumi esibonakalayo se-Mochi esisebenza ngayo

Isixhumi esibonakalayo se-Mochi siqondakala kangcono uma ulandela ikhadi elilodwa endleleni elisebenza ngayo.

Ikhadi ngalinye lingelenqwaba yamakhadi. Chofoza **New Card** (Ikhadi elisha) bese uthola indawo yokubhala i-Markdown, esikhundleni samabhokisi angaguquki ombuzo nempendulo. Ikhadi elilodwa lingaba nezihloko, izinhlu, ikhodi, izithombe, izinkambu ezihlelekile nezixhumanisi. Faka `---` phakathi kwezingxenye ukuze wakhe izinhlangothi ezimbili noma ngaphezulu zokubuyekeza. Faka `[[double brackets]]` ukuze ubhekisele kwelinye ikhadi; i-Mochi yakha ngokuzenzakalela isixhumanisi esibuyela ekhadini elixhumanisayo. [Incazelo esemthethweni yamakhadi](https://mochi.cards/docs/cards/) iphinde ichaze izifanekiso ezinezibambindawo ezifakwa amanani ezinkambu ezihlelekile lapho ikhadi liboniswa.

Amakhadi angenza imisebenzi emibili ngaphandle kokuhlala ezinhlelweni ezihlukene:

- ikhadi lokubuyekeza lisebenzisa izinhlangothi bese lingena ku-spaced repetition;
- inothi lokubhekisela kulo lingahlala eqoqweni elifanayo bese ligcinwa kungobo yomlando, okulikhipha emigqeni yamakhadi amasha nawasefike isikhathi sokubuyekezwa ngaphandle kokususa okuqukethwe, amathegi, izixhumanisi noma umlando walo.

[Izindlela zokubuka iqoqo](https://mochi.cards/docs/decks/custom-views/) ziyinhlanganisela egciniwe yezihlungi, ukuhlelwa nokubekwa kwezinto esikrinini. Ungagcina igridi yokuphequlula okuvamile, bese wakhe enye indlela yokubuka esekelwe kuthegi, ekutheni isikhathi sokubuyekeza sesifikile yini, ezingeni eliphansi lokukhumbula noma ekubuyekezeni kwakamuva. I-Mochi ikuvumela nokuba usebenzise indlela yokubuka ukuze uphindaphinde amakhadi esimisweni esisodwa sokufunda ngokushesha, ngaphandle kokuguqula izikhathi ezijwayelekile noma umlando wokubuyekeza. Lokhu kuchaza okuningi kunokubiza isixhumi esibonakalayo ngokuthi “sicocekile”: iqoqo elifanayo lingasebenza njengencwajana yamanothi, isizindalwazi esihlungiwe nomugqa wokufunda.

[Ukubuyekeza kwansuku zonke](https://mochi.cards/docs/getting-started/reviewing-cards/) kunezigaba ezimbili. Ku-**New cards** (Amakhadi amasha), ufaka ikhadi ohlelweni lwezikhathi zokubuyekeza noma ukhetha u-Again (Phinda) ukuze ulibone futhi maduze. Uma selifundiwe, ikhadi eselifike isikhathi salo liveza uhlangothi olulandelayo bese licela ukuba ukhethe **Forgot** (Ngikhohliwe) noma **Remembered** (Ngikhumbulile). Uma ulikhohliwe, i-Mochi isebenzisa umugqa wokuphinda ubuyekeze ngaphambi kokubuyisela inqubekela phambili ekuqaleni. Lokhu kugcina isinqumo okufanele usenze lapho ubuyekeza sinezinketho ezimbalwa ngamabomu.

## Ingabe i-Mochi imahhala, futhi yini esebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi?

Yebo, kodwa ukuthi “imahhala” nokuthi “isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi” kusho izinto ezihlukene kuye ngokuthi uyisebenzisaphi. [Ikhasi lamanani le-Mochi](https://mochi.cards/) libala lokhu:

- **Mahhala:** US$0 unomphela, akudingeki ubhalise, nokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi akunamkhawulo.
- **Pro:** US$5 ngenyanga, yengeza ukuvumelanisa phakathi kwamadivayisi, ukushicilela amaqoqo, izinkambu eziguqukayo, ukuhlanganisa ne-AI nosizo nge-imeyili.

I-Mochi isebenza ku-macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android nakuwebhu. [Umhlahlandlela wayo wokulanda nokufaka](https://mochi.cards/docs/getting-started/download-and-install/) uchaza umngcele wokusetshenziswa:

| Indawo yokusebenzisa | Okushiwo ukusebenzisa mahhala nangaphandle kwe-inthanethi |
|---|---|
| Uhlelo olufakiwe lwedeskithophu noma lweselula | Ungasebenzisa i-Mochi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti. Idatha igcinwa kudivayisi, ngakho uhlelo olulodwa olufakiwe lunganele ukwenza wonke umsebenzi mahhala. |
| Uhlelo lwewebhu ngaphandle kwe-Pro | Okuqukethwe kugcinwa endaweni yesiphequluli yokugcina idatha ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. I-Mochi ixwayisa ngokuthi isiphequluli singayisula le datha ngaphandle kwesexwayiso. |
| Iqoqo elifanayo kumadivayisi amaningi | Ukuvumelanisa ngokuzenzakalela phakathi kwamadivayisi kuyisici se-Pro, noma uhlelo ngalunye olufakiwe lungasebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. |

Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nokuvumelanisa kuyizithembiso ezihlukene. Awudingi i-Pro ukuze nje wakhe noma ubuyekeze amakhadi ohlelweni olulandiwe. Uyayidinga uma iqoqo elifanayo elibuyekeziwe kufanele likulandele ngokuzenzakalela lisuka kukhompyutha ephathekayo liye ocingweni. Ngedatha ebalulekile ohlelweni lwamahhala, gcina ikhophi eyisipele ngefomethi yohlelo kunokuba uthembele kudivayisi—ikakhulukazi endaweni yesiphequluli yokugcina idatha—njengekhophi yakho kuphela.

Uma ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuyikona okunqumayo, qhathanisa indlela yokusebenza kudivayisi yakho kokuthi [Ingabe i-Anki isebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi?](/blog/does-anki-work-offline/) nakulo [mhlahlandlela obanzi wezinhlelo zamakhadi okufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](/blog/best-offline-flashcards-app/).

## Amakhadi okufunda e-Markdown ayisizathu sangempela sokukhetha i-Mochi

Inzuzo yangempela ye-Mochi yindlela i-Markdown eguqula ngayo umbhalo oyisisekelo owubhalayo futhi uwugcine uhambisana nezidingo zakho.

Ikhadi le-Mochi lihlala lifundeka njengombhalo. Umbhalo ofanayo ungaqukatha incazelo emfushane, ibhulokhi yekhodi, izixhumanisi eziya emibonweni ehlobene nezihlukanisi phakathi kwezinhlangothi zokubuyekeza. Amakhadi angasebenzisa nezinkambu nezifanekiso uma kubalulekile ukuphindaphinda ukwakheka okufanayo. Uma kusetshenziswa isifanekiso, i-Mochi ibonisa i-Markdown yesifanekiso, ifake amanani ezinkambu ezibambindaweni zazo, ingayinaki i-Markdown yekhadi uqobo ngesikhathi sokubonisa kodwa ingayisusi.

I-Anki iqala ngendlela ehlukile. Inothi ligcina izinkambu, bese [izifanekiso zamakhadi](https://docs.ankiweb.net/templates/intro.html) zinquma ukuthi yiziphi izinkambu ezivelayo nokuthi yimaphi amakhadi akhiqizwayo. Izifanekiso zisebenzisa i-HTML, ne-CSS yokubeka isitayela. Ngakho inothi elilodwa legama lingakhiqiza ikhadi lokubona incazelo nelokuzikhumbulela igama, idatha eyisisekelo ihlale endaweni eyodwa.

Lokhu kwakheka kunikeza i-Anki amandla engeziwe okubonisa izinto ngezimo ezithile, okukhiqiza izinhlobo ezihlukene zamakhadi, okuthayipha izimpendulo, okwenza izitayela ngokwezifiso nawokwandisa indlela yokusebenza ngezingezo. Kusho nokuthi i-Anki ayilona uhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olusebenzisa i-Markdown ngokwemvelo. Indlela yokusebenza nge-Markdown ku-Anki idinga isinyathelo esengeziwe sokuguqula noma isengezo.

Umbuzo owusizo ulula: ufuna inothi elingaba yikhadi, noma uhlobo lwenothi oluhlelekile olungakhiqiza amakhadi amaningi? I-Mochi igxile endleleni yokuqala. I-Anki igxile kweyesibili.

## I-spaced repetition ye-Mochi manje ihlanganisa i-FSRS

Ukuqhathanisa okuthi i-Mochi ayinayo i-FSRS sekuphelelwe yisikhathi. I-Mochi yengeza inguqulo yokuhlola ye-FSRS ngo-2025 futhi iqhubekile nokukhipha ukulungiswa kohlelo lwezikhathi. Noma kunjalo, [i-algorithm ye-Mochi uqobo isalokhu iyona esetshenziswa ngokuzenzakalela](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/fsrs/).

I-algorithm esetshenziswa ngokuzenzakalela ishintsha izikhawu ngokuziphindaphinda ngezinombolo ezingaguquki ngemva kokubuyekeza ngakunye lapho ukhumbulile noma ukhohliwe. Shintshela ku-FSRS ku-Review Settings (Izilungiselelo zokubuyekeza), bese amakhadi osuwafundile edlulela ku-FSRS ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa umlando wawo. Ungasetha izinga lokukhumbula olihlosile, ufake amapharamitha akho bese ubuyela emuva kamuva.

I-Mochi igcina izilinganiso zayo ezimbili kunoma yiluphi uhlelo lwezikhathi:

- **Forgot** (Ngikhohliwe) ihambisana no-Again ku-FSRS.
- **Remembered** (Ngikhumbulile) ihambisana no-Good ku-FSRS.

Imibhalo ye-Mochi ithi izilinganiso ezimbili ziyasebenza ne-FSRS, kodwa kulahleka olunye ulwazi olunganikezwa u-Hard no-Easy. Yamukela amapharamitha enziwe ngokwezifiso futhi athuthukisiwe, kodwa ayinalo ithuluzi elakhelwe ngaphakathi lokuwalungisa ngokuzenzakalela; ukuze ukhiqize amapharamitha akho udinga ithuluzi langaphandle lokulungisa i-FSRS nomlando wokubuyekeza we-Mochi.

[Izilungiselelo ze-FSRS ku-Anki](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html#fsrs) zenza okwengeziwe. Izinga lokukhumbula elifiswayo namapharamitha kungahlanganiswa nezilungiselelo ezigciniwe, ithuluzi elakhelwe ngaphakathi lingalungisa amapharamitha ngokomlando wokubuyekeza, bese isilingisi silinganisela inani lokubuyekeza noma imizuzu yokufunda ngezilungiselelo ezihlukene. I-Anki iphinde irekhode imiphumela emine: Again, Hard, Good no-Easy.

Lezo zinkinobho ezengeziwe zisiza kuphela uma uzisebenzisa ngendlela engaguquguquki. Imanuwali ye-Anki ithatha u-Hard njengokukhumbula ngempumelelo. Ukucindezela u-Hard uma ukhohlwe impendulo kunikeza i-FSRS ulwazi olungalungile futhi kungadala izikhawu ezinde ngokweqile.

Khetha ukubuyekeza kwe-Mochi okunezinketho ezimbili uma ukukhetha phakathi kokukhumbula nokukhohlwa kwenza ukufunda kube lula. Khetha i-Anki uma ufuna ulwazi olwengeziwe oluvela ezilinganisweni futhi uzosebenzisa ithuluzi layo lokulungisa amapharamitha, izilawuli zokukhumbula, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe noma isilingisi somthwalo wokufunda. Ukuze uqhathanise izinhlelo zezikhathi kunokuba uqhathanise izinhlelo zokusebenza, bheka [i-FSRS uma iqhathaniswa ne-SM-2](/blog/fsrs-vs-sm-2/).

## Amanani e-Mochi nawe-Anki akhokhela izinto ezihlukene

Uma ufunda kukhompyutha eyodwa, zombili izinhlelo zingangakubizi lutho. Izindleko zivela ezindaweni ezihlukene uma ufaka amadivayisi engeziwe endleleni yokusebenza.

I-Mochi ikhokhisa **US$5 ngenyanga** ngokuvumelanisa kwe-Pro, okuhlanganiswa nokushicilela, izinkambu eziguqukayo, ukuhlanganisa ne-AI nosizo. Izinhlelo zedeskithophu ze-Anki zimahhala, futhi [isayithi esemthethweni ye-Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) ichaza ukuvumelanisa kwe-AnkiWeb njengokwamahhala. I-AnkiMobile iwuhlelo olusemthethweni olukhokhelwayo lwe-iPhone ne-iPad; i-AnkiDroid iwuhlelo lwamahhala lwe-Android oluthuthukiswa ngokuzimela.

Ngakho ukuthi “Yikuphi okushibhile?” kuncike kumadivayisi akho:

- ikhompyutha eyodwa: kokubili kungaba mahhala;
- amakhompyutha noma amadivayisi e-Android amaningi: ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala okusingathiwe kwe-Anki kugwema inkokhelo yokubhalisa;
- i-iPhone noma i-iPad: ku-Anki ukhokhela uhlelo kanye kuphela, kanti i-Mochi ibeka ukuvumelanisa phakathi kwamadivayisi ohlelweni lwayo lwe-Pro olukhokhwa njalo;
- abasebenzisi be-Mochi asebevele befuna ukushicilela, izinkambu eziguqukayo noma ukuhlanganisa kwayo ne-AI bangabheka ukuvumelanisa njengengxenye yephakheji kunokuba kube yikho konke abakukhokhelayo.

Hlola i-App Store yesifunda sakho ngaphambi kokuqhathanisa izindleko eziqondile ze-iOS. Lokhu kubuyekeza akubeki intengo yesitolo njengengaguquki, ngoba ingahluka ngokwemakethe.

## Ukusingatha i-Mochi akufani nokugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala

Amagama amathathu avame ukuhlanganiswa sengathi ayinto eyodwa:

- **Ukugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala** kusho ukuthi ikhophi osebenza ngayo ihlala kudivayisi yakho futhi uhlelo lungaqhubeka ngaphandle kwesevisi yalo yefu.
- **Ikhodi evulekile** kusho ukuthi ikhodi yomthombo iyatholakala ngaphansi kwelayisensi evumela ukuhlolwa nokuguqulwa.
- **Ukuzisingathela** kusho ukuthi imibhalo yomkhiqizo ichaza indlela esekelwayo yokuqhuba isevisi efanele kungqalasizinda yakho.

I-Mochi ichaza ngokucacile ukusebenza ngokugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala. Ayethuli uhlelo lwayo oluyinhloko njengolunekhodi evulekile: isixhumanisi esithi “Open source” ngaphansi kwesayithi yayo yomphakathi siya [eqoqweni lamathuluzi okuxhumanisa nezinye izinhlelo](https://github.com/mochi-cards/open-source), hhayi ohlelweni oluyinhloko. Isayithi yayo esemthethweni nayo ayichazi indlela esekelwayo yokuzisingathela isevisi ethatha indawo yokuvumelanisa kwe-Pro.

Uma usesha **ukusingatha i-Mochi** ngoba ufuna iseva yakho, nanku umngcele: ungagcina idatha kudivayisi namakhophi ayisipele ngefomethi yohlelo, kodwa indlela ebhaliwe yokusebenza phakathi kwamadivayisi yi-Mochi Pro. Ukugcina idatha kudivayisi kuqala kuwukulawula idatha okuwusizo; akukhona ukuzisingathela.

Ikhosombe eliyinhloko le-Anki [linelayisensi ye-AGPL inguqulo 3 noma eyakamuva](https://github.com/ankitects/anki/blob/main/LICENSE), ngaphandle kwezingxenye ezithile ezibaliwe. Imanuwali esemthethweni iphinde ichaze [iseva yokuvumelanisa ozisingathela yona](https://docs.ankiweb.net/sync-server.html) yabasebenzisi abanolwazi olunzulu. Leyo seva ithatha indawo yokuvumelanisa kwe-AnkiWeb ezinhlelweni ezihambisana nayo; ayiyona ikhophi yesayithi ye-AnkiWeb ozisingathela yona, futhi i-Anki ilindele ukuba oyisebenzisayo aphathe izinkinga zomugqa wemiyalo, zenethiwekhi, ze-firewall, zephrothokholi nezokuthuthukiselwa ezinguqulweni ezintsha.

## Lokho ukungenisa kusuka ku-Anki okukugcinayo nalokho okukuguqulayo

[Imibhalo yokungenisa ye-Mochi](https://mochi.cards/docs/import-and-export/importing/) ithi yamukela amafayela e-Anki e-`.apkg`, kuhlanganise nomlando wokubuyekeza. Kodwa ukuthi “kungenisiwe” nokuthi “kuyalingana nokwasekuqaleni” akuwona umphumela ofanayo.

Ngesikhathi sokungenisa, i-Mochi iguqula i-HTML ibe yi-Markdown bese isusa i-CSS ne-JavaScript. Lokho kuwukuguqula ifomethi phakathi kwezindlela ezimbili ezihlukene zokwakha amakhadi. Amakhadi anombhalo olula ngaphambili nangemuva yiwo aguquleka kalula kakhulu. Ikhadi elincike esitayeleni, emithethweni yesifanekiso, ekuthayipheni noma ku-JavaScript lidinga ukuhlolwa ngemva kokungeniswa.

Umlando wokubuyekeza nawo udinga ukuba ukhethe ngokucacile lapho ukhipha idatha. [Imanuwali yokukhipha idatha ye-Anki](https://docs.ankiweb.net/exporting.html) ithi **Include Scheduling Information** (Faka imininingwane yezikhathi zokubuyekeza) ilawula ukuthi umlando wokubuyekeza ufakwa yini ephakhejini. Uma ungayikhethi, i-Mochi ayikwazi ukubuyisa umlando i-`.apkg` engakaze iwuqukathe.

Ngisho noma umlando ufika, ungalindeli izinsuku ezifanayo ncamashi zokubuyekeza esikhathini esizayo. Lezi zinhlelo ezimbili zingasebenzisa izinhlelo zezikhathi, izilinganiso, amazinga okukhumbula ahlosiwe, amapharamitha, izinyathelo zokufunda nezilungiselelo zamaqoqo ezihlukene. Izehlakalo ezigciniwe zinikeza uhlelo olusha lwezikhathi ubufakazi; azenzi izinhlelo zifane ncamashi.

## Amakhophi ayisipele ngefomethi yohlelo nombhalo othuthekayo anemisebenzi ehlukene

Ngaphambi kokuthutha noma yini, gcina ikhophi eyisipele engabuyisela uhlelo lwasekuqaleni. Ifayela elikhishiwe elifundekayo liwusizo, kodwa alikwazi njalo ukubuyisela konke.

[Umhlahlandlela wamakhophi ayisipele we-Mochi](https://mochi.cards/docs/getting-started/backing-up/) uchaza izinketho ezimbili zokuphepha ngefomethi yohlelo:

- Ukukopisha yonke ifolda yomsebenzisi kugcina okuqukethwe, umlando wokubuyekeza, okunamathiselwe, izilungiselelo zohlelo nesimo sokungena ngemvume.
- Ukukhipha i-`.mochi` kugcina amaqoqo, amakhadi, izifanekiso nezinkambu, okunamathiselwe, amathegi nemethadatha, umlando wokubuyekeza, ukulandelana kwamakhadi nokwakheka kwamaqoqo.

[Ukukhipha kwe-Mochi nge-Markdown nange-CSV](https://mochi.cards/docs/import-and-export/exporting/) kungamafomethi okuthutha idatha. I-Markdown yakha ifayela elilodwa ngekhadi ngalinye namafolda amaqoqo angaphansi kwamanye, kodwa ilahla umlando wokubuyekeza, ukulandelana kwamakhadi, izifanekiso namathegi emethadatha ngaphandle uma amathegi eku-Markdown. I-CSV ingakhipha izinkambu zesifanekiso noma izinhlangothi zengaphambili nengemuva njengoba ziboniswa, kodwa ayigcini umlando wokubuyekeza, izifanekiso noma amathegi emethadatha ngaphandle uma amathegi efakwe kokuqukethwe.

I-Anki yenza umehluko ofanayo:

- I-`.colpkg` ikhipha lonke iqoqo kanye nezikhathi zokubuyekeza futhi ingafaka imidiya. Ukuyingenisa kuthatha indawo yamakhadi eqoqweni le-Anki elingeniselwa kulo.
- I-`.apkg` ikhipha inqwaba eyodwa yamakhadi nezinqwaba ezingaphansi kwayo, nezinketho zokufaka imininingwane yezikhathi, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe nemidiya.
- Amanothi ombhalo ongenafomethi asebenzisa izinkambu ezihlukaniswe ngamathebhu, kufakwe ukufometha kwe-HTML ngaphakathi. Agcina okuqukethwe okuhlelekayo, hhayi konke ukusebenza kweqoqo.

Ukusuka ku-Mochi ubuyele ku-Anki ngokuvamile kusho ukusebenzisa i-CSV. I-Anki ingakwazi [ukuhlanganisa amakholomu ombhalo nezinkambu zenothi](https://docs.ankiweb.net/importing/text-files.html), kodwa izixhumanisi ze-Mochi, ukusebenza kwezinhlangothi eziningi, izifanekiso nomlando wokubuyekeza akuphenduki izinto ezilinganayo ku-Anki ngalelo fayela. Gcina ifayela le-`.mochi` ngisho nangemva kokuba ikhophi ye-Anki isibukeka ilungile.

## Hlola ngenqwaba emele amakhadi akho, ugcine indlela yokubuyela emuva

Iwindi lokungenisa idatha libonisa ukuthi ifayela lamukelwe. Alifakazeli ukuthi iqoqo lakho langempela lisasebenza—noma ukuthi ungabuyisa okuqukethwe okusebenzisekayo. Hlola zombili izindlela, iphrofayela yakho evamile ye-Anki ingathintwa.

1. **Yenza ikhophi eyisipele yayo yonke i-Anki.** Khipha i-`.colpkg` enemidiya bese uyigcina ngaphandle kwephrofayela osebenza ngayo.
2. **Qinisekisa ukuthi ikhophi eyisipele iyavuleka.** Yakha iphrofayela ye-Anki yesikhashana engenalutho bese ungenisa i-`.colpkg` kuyo. Ukungenisa iphakheji yeqoqo kuthatha indawo yeqoqo elingeniselwa kulo, yingakho iphrofayela yesikhashana ibalulekile.
3. **Yakha inqwaba emele amakhadi akho kuleyo phrofayela yesikhashana.** Mayibe ncane ngokwanele ukuba uyihlole ikhadi ngekhadi, kodwa ifake ukusebenza othembele kukho: amakhadi ayisisekelo naphendukezelwe, ama-cloze anezikhala zokugcwalisa, izifanekiso ezenziwe ngokwezifiso, i-CSS, i-JavaScript, izithombe, umsindo, izibalo, amathegi, amaqoqo angaphakathi kwamanye nomlando wokubuyekeza.
4. **Khipha leyo nqwaba njenge-`.apkg`.** Faka imininingwane yezikhathi, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe nemidiya lapho kubalulekile. Lezo zinketho zifaka idatha ephakhejini ye-Anki; azithembisi ukuthi i-Mochi izophinda zonke izilungiselelo.
5. **Ngenisa enqwabeni entsha ye-Mochi.** Gcina iphrofayela yakho yansuku zonke ye-Anki nomugqa wayo wamakhadi asefike isikhathi sokubuyekezwa kungaguqukile.
6. **Hlola ngaphambi kokubuyekeza.** Qhathanisa okuqukethwe, ukufometha, izinkambu, imidiya, amathegi, ukwakheka kwamaqoqo nomlando. Naka kakhulu noma yini ebincike ku-HTML, ku-CSS, ku-JavaScript noma ezinhlotsheni zamakhadi ezikhiqizwa ngokuzenzakalela.
7. **Khetha uhlelo lwezikhathi ngamabomu.** I-Mochi iqala nge-algorithm yayo. Vula i-FSRS kuphela uma kuyiyona ohlose ukuyisebenzisa ngemva kokuhlola.
8. **Buyekeza ikhophi isonto elilodwa elijwayelekile.** Hlola indlela yokuhlela nge-Markdown, isigaba se-New cards, isinqumo se-Remembered/Forgot, ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, kanye—uma ukukhokhele—nokuvumelanisa kumadivayisi owaphatha ngempela.
9. **Hlola indlela yokubuyela emuva.** Khipha inqwaba yokuhlola ye-Mochi njenge-`.mochi` ukuze uthole ikhophi eyisipele ngefomethi yayo, nanjenge-CSV ye-Anki. Sebenzisa i-CSV yamanani ezinkambu uma kubalulekile ukuba izinkambu ziphinde zisetshenziswe; sebenzisa i-CSV yezinhlangothi eziboniswayo uma udinga kakhulu okuqukethwe okubonakalayo kwengaphambili nengemuva. Ngenisa leyo CSV kwenye iphrofayela ye-Anki engenalutho bese uqondanisa amakholomu ayo nezinkambu zohlobo olufanele lwenothi.
10. **Bhala konke ukulahleka okwamukelayo.** Hlola indlela yokuya neyokubuya ngokwehlukana. Isitayela esinembile, ukusebenza kwezinhlangothi eziningi, ukusebenza kwezingezo, izinhlobo ezikhiqizwayo, izilinganiso zokubuyekeza, umlando noma izinsuku ezizayo zokubuyekeza kungabaluleka kakhulu ekusetshenzisweni kwansuku zonke kunalokho obukucabanga ethebula lokuqhathanisa.

Ukubuya nge-CSV kuwukuthutha okuqukethwe, hhayi ukubuyisela i-Mochi ngokugcwele: ayithwali umlando wokubuyekeza, izifanekiso noma amathegi emethadatha ngaphandle uma lawo mathegi efakwe kokuqukethwe. Uma i-Mochi ingayixazululi inkinga oyishilo, susa inqwaba yokuhlola bese uqhubeka nephrofayela yokuqala ye-Anki. Uma iyixazulula, thutha inqwaba yangempela eyodwa ngesikhathi bese ugcina amakhophi ayisipele e-Anki e-`.colpkg` nawe-Mochi e-`.mochi` phakathi kwemijikelezo eminingana evamile yokubuyekeza.

## Ubani okufanele asebenzise amakhadi okufunda e-Mochi?

I-Mochi ifanele uma:

- i-Markdown isivele iyindlela obhala futhi ucabange ngayo;
- amanothi namakhadi okubuyekeza kufanele abe sendaweni eyodwa yokusebenza exhumene;
- ufuna isinqumo esilula se-Remembered/Forgot esikhundleni sezilinganiso ezine;
- ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kudivayisi eyodwa kuyanelisa izidingo zakho zamahhala, noma ukuvumelanisa kwe-Pro kuwufanele u-US$5 ngenyanga;
- iqoqo lakho lisha noma lilula ngokwanele ukuba ukuliguqula lisuka ku-Anki kube nengozi encane.

Hlala ne-Anki uma:

- izinhlobo zakho zamanothi zikhiqiza izinhlobo eziningana zamakhadi ezibalulekile;
- izifanekiso ze-HTML/CSS, i-JavaScript, izengezo noma amaqoqo okwabelwana ngawo kuyingxenye yohlelo lwakho;
- ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala phakathi kwamadivayisi kubaluleke ngaphezu kokubhala nge-Markdown;
- ufuna ithuluzi lokulungisa i-FSRS le-Anki, izilawuli zezilungiselelo ezigciniwe, izilinganiso ezine nesilingisi somthwalo wokufunda;
- iminyaka yedatha yokubuyekeza nokusebenza okwenziwe ngokwezifiso sekusebenza kahle.

Inketho enengqondo kakhulu esikhundleni se-Mochi incike ekutheni kungani kungekho kulokhu kokubili okukufanele. Ukuze uqale iqoqo elisha nelilula, [izici ze-Nibomo](/zu/features/) zihlanganisa ukubuyekeza nge-FSRS, ukufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nokuvumelanisa, ukuthutha amakhadi/amathegi/imidiya, ukufinyelela kwama-ejenti nendlela yokuzisingathela echazwe emibhalweni. Yimina engiyakhayo, futhi imikhawulo yayo ibalulekile: ayithathi indawo yencwajana yamanothi e-Markdown axhumene ye-Mochi noma uhlelo lwezifanekiso nezingezo oluthuthukiswe isikhathi eside lwe-Anki. [Umhlahlandlela wokuqalisa](/docs/getting-started/) ubonisa izindlela zamanje zohlelo olusingathiwe, ezeselula, zama-ejenti nezokuzisingathela.

## Isiphetho

I-Mochi ingaphezu kwesixhumi esibonakalayo se-Anki esibukeka kahle kakhudlwana. Umqondo wayo wangempela uwukuthi inothi le-Markdown, ulwazi oluxhumene nelinye, nekhadi le-spaced repetition kungaba yinto eyodwa. Uhlelo lwamahhala luhlanganisa ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti; i-Pro yengeza izici ezisingathiwe, kuhlanganise nokuvumelanisa phakathi kwamadivayisi.

Lokho kuyinzuzo efanele kumfundi oqala iqoqo elisha eligxile ku-Markdown. Kungase kube wusizo nokushintsha usuke ku-Anki uma ukuhlola ngenqwaba emele amakhadi akho kufakazela ukuthi ukusekelwa kwe-Markdown okwakhelwe ohlelweni nokubuyekeza ngezinketho ezimbili kususa ubunzima bangempela.

Kumuntu osenesikhathi esebenzisa i-Anki, ukushintsha yikho okudinga ubufakazi. Yenza ikhophi eyisipele yeqoqo, hlola amakhadi asebenzisa kakhulu ukusebenza okwenziwe ngokwezifiso, bese ugcina i-Anki ngaphandle uma i-Mochi ithuthukisa indlela osebenza ngayo isonto nesonto ngokwanele ukuba yamukeleke ukushiya emuva izici ezithile zokufometha, zezifanekiso, zezikhathi zokubuyekeza nezamathuluzi ayizungezile.
