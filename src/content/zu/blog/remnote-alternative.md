I-RemNote ibiza ukukhiphela kwayo idatha ku-Anki ngokuthi **Flashcards Only** (Amakhadi okufunda kuphela). Amaphuzu ohlu angenawo amakhadi ayeqiwa, futhi iphakheji ayiqukethe amanothi akho axhumene, ama-PDF noma indlela yakho yokusebenza nge-Reader. Olunye uhlelo lungamukela yonke imibuzo nezimpendulo kodwa lushiye emuva uhlelo olwalwenza lawo makhadi abe wusizo.

**Enye indlela efanele esikhundleni se-RemNote** yileyo exazulula isizathu sokuhamba kwakho ngaphandle kokususa buthule ingxenye ye-RemNote esakusebenzela. Kwabanye inkinga yintengo. Abanye bafuna amafayela avamile agcinwe kudivayisi, uhlelo lwamakhadi olunamathuluzi abanzi, noma ikhodi abangayisebenzisa ngokwabo.

> **Ubudlelwano bami nomkhiqizo:** NginguKirill Markin, futhi ngithuthukisa i-[Nibomo](/zu/), omunye wemikhiqizo eqhathaniswa lapha. I-Nibomo ayithathi indawo ye-RemNote ngokuphelele. Kulesi siqhathaniso i-RemNote ihlanganisa kahle kakhulu amanothi nama-PDF endleleni eyodwa yokusebenza, kuyilapho i-Anki inohlelo lwamakhadi namafomethi okuthutha idatha athuthukiswe isikhathi eside kakhulu.

**Amaqiniso namanani ahlolwe:** 31 Agasti 2026. Amanani asohlwini ngamanani asesidlangalaleni ase-US, futhi asebenzisa inkokhelo yonyaka lapho kushiwo khona; izintela, izifunda, izitolo zezinhlelo nemibandela ye-beta kungashintsha inani.

## Qala ngesizathu sokuthi kungani ufuna ukuhamba

- **Intengo:** Hlola ukuthi i-RemNote Free iyazifeza yini kakade izidingo zakho zokufunda. Ifaka amanothi, amakhadi okufunda namadivayisi avumelanisiwe angenamkhawulo, kodwa ibeka imikhawulo emibhalweni efakwe izichasiselo nakwezinye izici ezithuthukile.
- **Indlela yokusebenzisa amakhadi ezwakala iboshelwe kakhulu emanothini:** Zama i-Anki. Inikeza amakhadi, izifanekiso, ukungeniswa kwedatha ne-FSRS indawo enkulu yokuba yisisekelo sohlelo.
- **Amafayela avamile amanothi agcinwe kudivayisi:** Hlukanisa umsebenzi phakathi kwe-Obsidian yamanothi e-Markdown ne-Anki yokubuyekeza. Lokhu akuhlangene kakhulu, kodwa kucace kakhulu ukuthi iyiphi ingxenye olawula yona.
- **Amanothi axhumene anekhodi evulekile, nama-PDF namakhadi akhelwe ngaphakathi:** I-Logseq iyona esondela kakhulu lapha, kodwa kunomkhawulo obalulekile ngo-2026: inguqulo yayo entsha yesizindalwazi iku-beta, uhlelo lwayo olusha lwe-iOS nokuvumelanisa ngesikhathi sangempela kuku-alpha, kanti uhlelo lwayo olusha lwe-Android alukavulelwa ukuhlolwa.
- **Ikhodi nokuzisingathela uhlelo olugxile emakhadini:** Cabangela i-Nibomo uma amakhadi anengaphambili nengemuva anele futhi wamukela ukuqala kabusha izikhathi zokubuyekeza kanye nomsebenzi omkhulu wokuphatha i-AWS.
- **Ukufunda ama-PDF, ukugqamisa okuxhumene namakhadi endaweni eyodwa:** Hlala ne-RemNote. Ayikho enye yalezi zindlela ekhiqiza leyo ndlela yokusebenza kahle ngokuphelele.

Kulula ukungayinaki leyo mpendulo yokugcina. Ukushintsha akukuyisi phambili uma enye indlela yanelisa ukukhetha kwakho kwelayisensi kodwa iphazamisa ukufunda kwakusasa.

## Hlala ne-RemNote uma indlela yokusebenza exhumene iyona eyenza umkhiqizo ubaluleke

I-RemNote ihlanganisa izinyathelo ezinye izinhlelo eziningi ezizihlukanisayo. I-[Reader](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6690975-learning-from-pdfs-and-files-with-the-remnote-reader) yayo ingagcina i-PDF ivuliwe eceleni kwamanothi akho, inamathisele izikhombo ezibuyela ezingxenyeni ezithile ezigqanyisiwe, bese iguqula lawo manothi noma lokho okugqanyisiwe kube amakhadi okufunda. Uhlelo lwe-Free lukuvumela ukufaka izichasiselo emibhalweni emithathu; [ikhasi lamanani](https://www.remnote.com/pricing) lamanje libala imibhalo enezichasiselo engenamkhawulo ku-Pro.

Uhlelo lwayo lwezikhathi zokubuyekeza alusasona isizathu esisobala sokuhamba. I-RemNote manje ichaza [i-FSRS-6](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/9124137-the-fsrs-spaced-repetition-algorithm) njengenketho ye-beta oyivula ngokwakho. Ngemva kokubuyekeza okungenani izikhathi eziyi-1,000, ingaqeqesha amapharamitha ngomlando wakho. I-Anki isanezindlela eziningi zokulawula, kodwa umfundi othanda amanothi nama-PDF e-RemNote akudingeki awayeke ukuze nje asebenzise i-FSRS.

Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nakho kungcono kunokuthi “isebenza kuthebhu yesiphequluli evulekile.” [Izinhlelo ze-RemNote zedeskithophu neselula](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752029-offline-mode) zingahlela amanothi futhi zibuyekeze amakhadi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngemva kokufakwa nokungena ngemvume. Ideskithophu igcina ikhophi ephelele yezithombe nama-PDF kudivayisi. Iselula newebhu kungase kungabi nayo imidiya engakagcinwa kusilondolozi sesikhashana, futhi uhlelo lwewebhu alukwazi ukuqala kuthebhu evaliwe noma elayishwe kabusha ngaphandle kokuxhumeka.

Uma uqale lokhu kusesha ufuna **enye indlela yamahhala esikhundleni se-RemNote**, hlola uhlelo lwe-Free ngaphambi kokuthutha. Uma inkinga kuwukufinyelela ikhodi, imodi egcina idatha kudivayisi ayifani nekhodi evulekile noma ukuzisingathela. Umhlahlandlela ohlukile wokuthi [ingabe i-RemNote inekhodi evulekile](/blog/is-remnote-open-source/) uchaza lo mehluko ngokuningiliziwe.

## I-RemNote ne-Anki: khetha ukuthi yini eyisisekelo

Umehluko owusizo lapho **uqhathanisa i-RemNote ne-Anki** awukona ukuthi “amanothi noma awekho amanothi.” I-Anki nayo igcina amanothi, kodwa inothi le-Anki liyisethi yezinkambu [izifanekiso zamakhadi](https://docs.ankiweb.net/templates/intro.html) eziyiguqula ibe amakhadi okubuyekeza. I-RemNote iqala ngemibhalo namaphuzu ohlu axhumene angaba amakhadi. Enye iwuhlelo oluthuthukiswe isikhathi eside lokukhiqiza amakhadi; enye iyindawo yokufunda egxile emanothini nasemithonjeni.

Khetha i-Anki uma izinkambu ezenziwe ngokwezifiso, izinhlobo zamakhadi ezikhiqizwayo, izifanekiso ze-HTML/CSS, izengezo noma iminyaka yomlando wokubuyekeza kubalulekile. [Izilungiselelo zayo zamanje ze-FSRS](https://docs.ankiweb.net/deck-options.html#fsrs) zifaka ukulungiswa kwamapharamitha, izinga lokukhumbula elihlosiwe nokulingisa umthwalo wokufunda. [Ukukhipha kwayo idatha](https://docs.ankiweb.net/exporting.html) kungagcina lonke iqoqo ku-`.colpkg`, kuyilapho amaphakheji ezinqwaba ze-`.apkg` engafaka imininingwane yezikhathi zokubuyekeza, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe nemidiya.

I-RemNote inikeza indlela yokuphuma uye ku-Anki, kodwa igama layo libalulekile: [ukukhiphela ku-Anki kungu-“Flashcards Only”](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/7898019-exporting-notes). Amaphuzu ohlu angenawo amakhadi awafakwa. I-RemNote igcina umongo wamaphuzu angaphezulu emakhadini akhishiwe futhi iguqula amakhadi okukhetha impendulo phakathi kweziningana abe amakhadi avamile, kodwa okukhishiwe akusona isizinda sakho solwazi, umtapo wakho wama-PDF noma indlela yakho ephelele yokufunda. Ikhasi elisemthethweni lokukhipha idatha le-RemNote nalo alithembisi ukuthi zonke izingxenye zesimo sakho sezikhathi zokubuyekeza zizofika ku-Anki. Hlola ngaphambi kokucabanga ukuthi akukho okuzolahleka.

I-Anki iyinketho enamandla kakhulu lapha egxile emakhadini. Kodwa kunzima ukuyisebenzisa esikhundleni se-RemNote Reader ngaphandle kokushintsha indlela osebenza ngayo. Uma usafaka izichasiselo emaphepheni futhi ubhala amanothi axhumene, yihlanganise nethuluzi lamanothi esikhundleni sokuphoqa i-Anki ukuba ibe yilo. [Umhlahlandlela obanzi wezinye izinhlelo esikhundleni se-Anki](/zu/blog/best-anki-alternatives/) uhlanganisa ezinye izinketho ezigxile emakhadini.

## I-Obsidian ne-Anki: amafayela akudivayisi, nokuhlukanisa ngamabomu

Abanye abafuna ezinye izinhlelo esikhundleni se-RemNote abadingi olunye uhlelo oluhlanganisa konke. Bafuna amanothi ahlala engamafayela avamile nohlelo lokubuyekeza olungathuthuka ngokuzimela. I-Obsidian ne-Anki ziyindlela ecacile yalokho kuhlukanisa.

[I-Obsidian igcina amanothi](https://obsidian.md/help/Files%2Band%2Bfolders/How%2BObsidian%2Bstores%2Bdata) njengombhalo ojwayelekile ofomethwe nge-Markdown kufolda ekudivayisi. Uhlelo lumahhala ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti; [i-Obsidian Sync](https://obsidian.md/pricing) ongayikhetha iqala ku-US$4 ngenyanga uma ukhokha ngonyaka. I-Obsidian ayinayo ikhodi evulekile, kodwa amafayela amanothi afundeka ngokuqondile futhi angenzelwa amakhophi ayisipele ngamathuluzi avamile amafayela.

Sebenzisa ukukhipha kwe-RemNote nge-Markdown ngasohlangothini lwamanothi nangama-`.apkg` ngasohlangothini lwamakhadi. Lindela umsebenzi wokulungisa. Uhlaka olunamazinga angaphakathi kwamanye olukhishwe njenge-Markdown efundekayo alufani nezikhombo ezisebenzayo ze-RemNote, ama-portal, izifanekiso noma amaphini ama-PDF. Uma amanothi namakhadi esezinhlelweni ezimbili, izinguquko nazo azisadluliselwa phakathi kwazo ngokuzenzakalela.

Le ndlela iyasebenza uma ukulawula amafayela akudivayisi kubaluleke kakhulu kunomjikelezo oshelelayo wokuthi “gqamisa, xhumanisa, yakha ikhadi, buyekeza.” Ayifanele uma lowo mjikelezo uyisona sizathu owakhetha ngaso i-RemNote.

## I-Logseq: inketho enekhodi evulekile egxile emanothini isenguqukweni

I-Logseq ifanele ukufakwa ekuqhathaniseni **ezinye izinhlelo ezinekhodi evulekile esikhundleni se-RemNote** ngoba iqala ngamanothi ngempela. [Ikhosombe elisemthethweni elinelayisensi ye-AGPL](https://github.com/logseq/logseq) lichaza uhlelo lokuphatha ulwazi olunamabhulokhi axhumene nokufakwa kwezichasiselo kuma-PDF. [Imibhalo yamanje yenguqulo yesizindalwazi](https://github.com/logseq/docs/blob/master/db-version.md#cards) yengeza amakhadi akhelwe ngaphakathi: faka ithegi kubhulokhi, ubone ukuthi isikhathi sokuyibuyekeza sifika nini, bese uyibuyekeza ngezilinganiso ezine.

Isimo samanje sibaluleke ngaphezu kohlu lwezici. Ikhosombe le-Logseq uqobo lithi inguqulo yesizindalwazi iku-beta, kanti uhlelo lwayo olusha lwe-iOS nokuvumelanisa ngesikhathi sangempela kuku-alpha; imibhalo yamanje yenguqulo yesizindalwazi ithi uhlelo lwe-Android alukavulelwa ukuhlolwa kwe-alpha. I-Logseq ixwayisa ngokucacile ukuthi idatha ingalahleka futhi incoma igrafu yokuhlola engabalulekile kanye namakhophi ayisipele. [Amanothi ayo ezinguquko zenguqulo yesizindalwazi](https://github.com/logseq/docs/blob/master/db-version-changes.md#high-level-changes) athi futhi i-algorithm entsha yamakhadi ayingenisi izakhiwo noma idatha ye-SRS yamakhadi amadala e-Logseq.

Ukuthutheka kwedatha nakho kudinga ukuchazwa ngokucophelela. [Imibhalo yamanje yokukhipha idatha yenguqulo yesizindalwazi](https://github.com/logseq/docs/blob/master/db-version.md#export-and-import) inikeza i-SQLite namafayela ahambisana nayo, i-EDN ne-Markdown evamile. Ithi i-EDN iyona kuphela ifomethi ekhishekayo nehlelekayo egcina yonke idatha yegrafu, kodwa ayincomi ukusebenzisa i-EDN njengekhophi eyisipele okuwukuphela kwayo. I-Markdown evamile ayifaki izakhiwo nezitembu zesikhathi.

Ngakho i-Logseq iyindlela okufanele uyihlole uma ikhodi evulekile, amanothi axhumene, ama-PDF namakhadi akhelwe ngaphakathi konke kubalulekile. Akuyona indlela engingayisebenzisa ukuthutha ngosuku olulodwa isizinda solwazi esibalulekile sesikole sobudokotela ngo-Agasti 2026. Yisebenzise eceleni kwe-RemNote kuqala futhi uvumele lolu shintsho lwamanje luzinze kumadivayisi owasebenzisa ngempela.

## I-Nibomo: lonke uhlelo luvulekile, indlela yokufunda igxile kokumbalwa

I-Nibomo ikhetha cishe okuphambene ne-RemNote. [Izici zayo](/zu/features/) zigxile emakhadini e-Markdown anengaphambili nengemuva, ezinqwabeni zamakhadi, emathegini, emidiyeni, ekubuyekezeni nge-FSRS, ezinhlelweni ezisebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuqala nasekubhaleni amakhadi ngosizo lwe-AI. Ayinaso isizinda solwazi samanothi axhumene, isifundi sama-PDF, uhlelo olwakhelwe ideskithophu noma ukungenisa okuqondile kwe-RemNote.

Ikhodi etholakalayo ihlanganisa okuningi: ikhosombe elinelayisensi ye-MIT lifaka iwebhu, i-iOS, i-Android, ukuqinisekisa ubuwena, iseva, ukuvumelanisa nengqalasizinda. [Umhlahlandlela osekelwayo wokuzisingathela ekukhiqizeni](/docs/self-hosting/) usebenzisa i-AWS CDK. Akulona ithuluzi olisebenzisa kudivayisi ngomyalo owodwa. Abaliphethe banomthwalo wezindleko zefu, izimfihlo zokufinyelela, ukuthuthwa kwesizindalwazi, ukuqapha, amakhophi ayisipele, ukuhlola ukubuyisela nezinhlelo zeselula ezakhiwa ngokwehlukana.

Ukuthutha kuwumkhawulo omkhulu kumuntu osebenzisa i-RemNote. I-Nibomo ingenisa amaphakheji ayo e-`flashcards.zip`, hhayi i-Markdown ye-RemNote noma ama-`.apkg` e-Anki. Lawo maphakheji athwala amakhadi, amathegi nemidiya ebhekiselwe kuyo, kodwa awafaki umlando wokubuyekeza, isimo se-FSRS, izilungiselelo zendawo yokusebenza, ukwakheka okugcwele kwezinqwaba zamakhadi noma ama-akhawunti. Ingxoxo ye-AI ingaguqula umbhalo okhishiwe ube yimibhalo yokuqala yamakhadi ozoyihlola; lokho kuwukwakha kabusha okuqukethwe, hhayi ukuqhubeka neqoqo elidala. [Umhlahlandlela wokuthutha nge-TXT](/blog/migrate-from-anki-txt-export-open-source-flashcards/) ubonisa lo mkhawulo wokulahleka kwedatha isinyathelo ngesinyathelo.

## Yini engeke ithuthe kahle isuka ku-RemNote

I-RemNote inezindlela eziningana eziwusizo zokukhipha idatha, kodwa alikho ifayela elilodwa eliphinda wonke umkhiqizo kwenye indawo.

- **Ukukhipha okuphelele kwe-RemNote** kuyifomethi engcono kakhulu yokubuyisela ku-RemNote. Okwamanje akufaki izithombe nama-PDF.
- **Ukukhipha kwe-Anki nge-`.apkg`** kuqukethe amakhadi okufunda kuphela. Amaphuzu ohlu angenawo amakhadi ayanyamalala kuleyo ndlela, futhi umphumela awulona uhlelo lwakho lwamanothi axhumene.
- **I-Markdown, i-HTML, i-OPML nombhalo** kwenza okuqukethwe kufundeke kalula kwenye indawo. Akukwenzi olunye uhlelo luqonde bonke ubudlelwano noma indlela yokusebenza eqondene ne-RemNote.
- **Okugqanyisiwe kuma-PDF nemithombo** kudinga ukuhlolwa kwakho. I-RemNote Reader ingalanda i-PDF enokugqanyisiwe, kodwa ungacabangi ukuthi ukukhipha okuphelele kwesizinda solwazi kuqukethe lelo fayela.
- **Izilungiselelo, amatimu nama-plugin** akufakwa ekhophini eyisipele ye-RemNote eyenziwa ngesandla, ngokusho [kwemibhalo yamakhophi ayisipele](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6301627-remnote-backups).
- **Isimo sokubuyekeza** kufanele siqinisekiswe ikhadi ngekhadi ohlelweni olusha. Ukungenisa okugcina umbuzo nempendulo kusengaqalisa kabusha izikhathi zokubuyekeza.

Yingakho ukuthi “isekela i-Markdown” noma “ingenisa i-Anki” kunganele. Ukuthutheka kunezingqimba: amanothi afundekayo, imidiya esebenzisekayo, imithombo exhumene, ukwakheka kwamakhadi nomlando wokufunda.

## Hlola indlela yokuphuma ngaphambi kokukhansela

Yenza ukuthutha kube into ongakwazi ukuyibuyisela emuva. Ihora elilodwa elizolile manje libiza kancane kunokuthola i-PDF elahlekile ngesonto lezivivinyo.

1. Yenza ukukhipha okusha ngesandla kwe-**RemNote (Complete)** bese ukugcina kungaguquliwe.
2. Kudeskithophu, kopisha amakhophi ayisipele e-`.db.zip` nefolda ye-`files` akudivayisi. Landa wonke ama-PDF okuqala noma anezichasiselo ongeke ukwazi ukuwathola futhi.
3. Khetha isampula elincane elinezinto eziyinkimbinkimbi: amanothi angaphansi kwamanye, izikhombo, i-PDF eyodwa, izithombe, amakhadi anezikhala zokugcwalisa noma okukhetha phakathi kwezimpendulo, amathegi namakhadi anomlando obalulekile wokubuyekeza.
4. Khipha lelo sampula kuwo wonke amafomethi adingwa indlela oyicabangayo—ngokuvamile i-Markdown yamanothi ne-`.apkg` ye-Anki.
5. Ngenisa endaweni yokugcina amanothi, kugrafu, kuphrofayela noma endaweni yokusebenza ongayisusa ngemva kokuhlola. Vula i-RemNote eceleni kohlelo olusha, bese uqhathanisa inani lezinto, ukufometha, izixhumanisi, imidiya, ingaphambili nengemuva lamakhadi nokuthi yimaphi amakhadi adinga ukubuyekezwa.
6. Sebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuwo wonke amadivayisi ohlela ukuwasebenzisa. Bese uxhuma futhi uqinisekise ukuthi izinguquko nokubuyekeza kufika lapho kulindelekile.
7. Buyisela ikhophi eyisipele ephelele esizindeni solwazi sesikhashana se-RemNote esigcinwe kudivayisi. Ingobo oyilandile iba icebo lokubuyisela kuphela ngemva kokuba uyivule ngempumelelo.
8. Sebenzisa zombili izinhlelo okungenani ezikhathini ezimbalwa zokufunda kwansuku zonke. Khansela kuphela ngemva kokuba olunye uhlelo seluphumelele ekusetshenzisweni kwansuku zonke, ekukhipheni idatha nasekubuyiseleni.

Gcina amafayela owakhipha ohlelweni lokuqala ngisho nangemva kokuthutha. Ukungenisa okuphumelelayo kufakazela ukuhambisana nenguqulo yanamuhla yohlelo olusha, hhayi ukufinyelela unomphela kuzo zonke izingxenye zohlelo oludala.

## Izinketho okufanele uzicabangele

- **Hlala ne-RemNote** uma amanothi axhumene nokufunda ngama-PDF kuyizingxenye ezibalulekile. Uhlelo lwayo lwe-Free noma isizinda solwazi esigcinwe kudivayisi kuphela kungenzeka sesiyixazulula inkinga yakho.
- **Khetha i-Anki** uma amakhadi, izifanekiso, izilawuli ze-FSRS nokugcina idatha ngesikhathi sokuthutha kubaluleke kakhulu.
- **Khetha i-Obsidian ne-Anki** uma amafayela avamile amanothi akudivayisi enza kube wusizo ukusebenzisa amathuluzi amabili.
- **Hlola i-Logseq** uma udinga amanothi axhumene anekhodi evulekile namakhadi akhelwe ngaphakathi, kodwa ungayihloli ngedatha ebalulekile njengoba uhlelo lwayo lwamanje lwesizindalwazi nokuvumelanisa kusese-beta nase-alpha.
- **Khetha i-Nibomo** uma uhlelo olusha olulula lwamakhadi nokufinyelela ikhodi yalo lonke uhlelo kubaluleke ngaphezu kwamanothi, ama-PDF noma ukuqhubeka nezikhathi zokubuyekeza ezikhona.

Ngithuthukisa i-Nibomo, futhi bengisazogcina i-RemNote ukuze ngibe nencwajana yamanothi axhumene ethembele kakhulu kuma-PDF, noma ngikhethe i-Anki yeqoqo eliyinkimbinkimbi eselinesikhathi eside likhona. I-Nibomo iyinketho egxile kokumbalwa: amakhadi anengaphambili nengemuva, uhlelo oluvulekile nezikhathi ezintsha zokubuyekeza.

Uma usuwazi umkhawulo ongawamukela, hlola leyo ndlela kuphela. Uma i-Nibomo ikufanele, [umhlahlandlela wokuqalisa](/docs/getting-started/) ubonisa izindlela zokungena ohlelweni olusingathiwe noluzisingathela lona. Uma ingakufanele, ukuqhubeka ne-RemNote nakho kuyisinqumo esifanele.
