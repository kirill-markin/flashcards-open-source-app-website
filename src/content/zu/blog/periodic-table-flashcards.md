## Inombolo ye-athomu ayiyona imasi ye-athomu

Ibhokisi le-elementi lingaba nezinombolo eziningana, kodwa ziphendula imibuzo ehlukene.

- **Inombolo ye-athomu** ikhomba i-elementi futhi ilingana nenani lama-proton ayo. I-sodium ihlale inenombolo ye-athomu engu-11.
- **Uphawu lwe-elementi** luyisifinyezo esinohlamvu olulodwa noma izinhlamvu ezimbili. Ubukhulu bezinhlamvu buyingxenye yencazelo: i-sodium ibhalwa `Na`, hhayi `NA` noma `na`.
- **Imasi ye-athomu nesisindo se-athomu** akuyona inombolo ye-athomu. Amathebula asetshenziswa ekilasini angase abhale elinye lala magama eduze kwenombolo enkulu enedesimali, kodwa la magama awasho into efanayo ncamashi; landela ilebula, ukunemba nenqubo esetshenziswa yithebula.
- **Inombolo yemasi** iyisamba esiyinombolo ephelele sama-proton nama-neutron ku-nuclide eyodwa.

Inombolo ephelele efakwe kubakaki eduze kwe-elementi ekhipha imisebe kwamanye amathebula ivame ukuba yinombolo yemasi—hhayi enye inombolo ye-athomu noma isisindo se-athomu esijwayelekile.

Yingakho ithebula elingenhla linikeza igama, uphawu nenombolo ye-athomu kuphela. Le mininingwane emithathu ikunikeza okufunayo ngokucacile. [Umlando wezinguqulo zethebula le-periodic we-NIST](https://www.nist.gov/pml/periodic-table-version-history) uphinde ubonise ukuthi kungani idekhi equkethe izisindo ze-athomu ezinembile idinga umthombo nosuku: imininingwane eyisisekelo ingabuyekezwa. Uma izifundo zakho zihlola izisindo ze-athomu, kopisha ngokuqondile isici esidingekayo, inguqulo yethebula nomthetho wokusondeza izinombolo okunikeziwe.

## Indlela yokukhumbula ithebula le-periodic: qala ngezifundo zakho

Ukubona imigqa engu-118 kungakwenza uzinike umsebenzi ongadingeki ezifundweni zakho: “Kusho ukuthi kufanele ngikukhumbule konke lokhu.” Okuvame ukudingeka kuncane kunalokho. Bheka uhlelo lwezifundo, imiyalelo kathisha, ishidi lokubheka elivunyelwe kanye nemibuzo yakamuva ngaphambi kokwenza idekhi.

| Uma umsebenzi wakho udinga ukuba… | Okusiza ukukukhumbula | Okufanele kungangeni kule dekhi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ubone ama-elementi avamile kumafomula | uphawu → igama | ukubhala amafomula aphelele nemibuzo yokuqamba amagama |
| ubhale izimpawu usuka emagameni ama-elementi | igama → uphawu olunobukhulu bezinhlamvu obufanele | amashaji nezinkomba eziphansi kumakhompawundi |
| wazi ama-elementi okuqala angu-20 | indlela yokubuza edingekayo kuma-elementi 1–20 | amanye angu-98 kuze kufike isikhathi sawo ezifundweni |
| wakhe kabusha ithebula | ukukhomba ama-elementi akhethiwe nezindawo ezikusiza ukhumbule amaqembu nezinkathi | ukuzilolonga ngokuphindaphinda ukugcwalisa ithebula elingenalutho |
| uqhathanise ukuthambekela kwezakhiwo kuthebula | imithetho embalwa yezinkomba kanye nokuhlukile okuqanjwe ngamagama | izincazelo nokuqhathanisa izinto ongazijwayele |
| ubhale ukuhleleka kwama-electron | ukuhleleka nokuhlukile okudingwa yizifundo | umsebenzi wokugcwalisa ama-orbital osetshenziselwa ukukuthola |
| ukhumbule ukuthi yiliphi ngalinye lama-elementi angu-118 | izindlela ezihlolwayo zamagama, izimpawu nezinombolo ze-athomu | imasi, ukuthambekela nokuhleleka kwama-electron ngaphandle uma kudingwa ngokwehlukile |

Othisha bangasusa ukungaqondi okuningi ngokusho ukuthi iyiphi indlela yokubuza ezohlolwa. “Yazi ama-elementi” kungasho ukufunda uphawu, ukubhala uphawu, ukugcwalisa ithebula elingenalutho noma ukukhumbula izinombolo ze-athomu. Le misebenzi idinga izibalo zamakhadi ezihluke kakhulu.

## Yenza amakhadi aqondiswe ephutheni, hhayi kulo lonke ibhokisi

`Co` yi-cobalt. `CO` iyifomula equkethe izimpawu ezimbili ezihlukene, `C` no-`O`. Uhlamvu olulodwa oluncane lushintsha incazelo. Ikhadi eliwusizo lokulungisa iphutha liqondiswa kulelo phutha ngqo:

```text
Ngaphambili: Iyiphi i-elementi enophawu oluthi Co?
Ngemuva: I-cobalt.

Ngaphambili: Yiziphi izimpawu zama-elementi ezimbili eziku-CO?
Ngemuva: U-C wekhabhoni no-O womoyampilo.
```

Umbuzo wesibili uwukuzilolonga ngokufunda ifomula, awulona elinye ikhadi le-cobalt. Ezinye izimpawu ezifanayo zihlanganisa `S` no-`Si`, `N` no-`Ni`, `C` no-`Ca`, kanye no-`P` no-`Pb`. Uhlamvu lwesibili lophawu olunezinhlamvu ezimbili kufanele lube luncane.

Ezinye izimpawu zinzima ngesinye isizathu: igama lesiNgisi alinazo izinhlamvu obungalindela ukuzibona ophawini. Lezi zilungele amakhadi okuqala aqondiswe emaphutheni uma zidingeka ezifundweni zakho:

## Iqiniso elilodwa ekhadini lenza ukuzihlola kuthembeke

“Ngitshele konke nge-sodium” kubukeka kuwusizo ngoba kungena ekhadini elilodwa. Kodwa ngesikhathi sokubuyekeza kuba nzima: mhlawumbe ukhumbule `Na` no-11 kodwa wayibeka enkathini engafanele yethebula. Kunzima ukunikeza impendulo elungile ngokwengxenye isilinganiso esithembekile.

Kunalokho, hlukanisa amaqiniso azohlolwa:

```text
Ngaphambili: Bhala uphawu lwe-sodium. Gcina ubukhulu bezinhlamvu obufanele.
Ngemuva: Na.

Ngaphambili: Iyiphi i-elementi enophawu oluthi Na?
Ngemuva: I-sodium.

Ngaphambili: Iyiphi i-elementi enenombolo ye-athomu engu-11?
Ngemuva: I-sodium.
```

Lezi izindlela ezintathu zokubuza, hhayi amakhadi amathathu ayimpoqo. Qala ngendlela esetshenziswa emsebenzini wakho. Faka indlela ebuyela emuva kuphela uma kudingeka ukwazi ukuyiphendula noma ukuzilolonga kuveza igebe. Ukuguqula ngokuzenzakalelayo wonke amaqiniso ukuze abuzwe nhlangothi zombili kungaguqula ama-elementi angu-118 abe amakhulu amakhadi okufanele uwabuyekeze ngaphandle kokuthuthukisa ikhono elisetshenziswa esivivinyweni sakho.

## Uhlelo olusebenzisekayo lokukhumbula iqoqo olidingayo

Qala ngobufakazi, ngisho noma kuyisivivinyo semizuzu emihlanu kuphela.

1. **Bhala ngokuqondile okudingekayo.** “Ama-elementi 1–20, igama ↔ uphawu” kuyasebenziseka. “Funda ithebula le-periodic” akucacile ngokwanele.
2. **Zihlole kafushane ungawabheki amanothi.** Sebenzisa ithebula elingenalutho, isivivinyo sikathisha noma umbuzo wamakhemikhali enisebenza ngawo. Maka iphutha ngalinye ngokuqondile.
3. **Yenza ikhadi elilodwa ngendlela ngayinye yokubuza owehlulekile kuyo.** Ungenzi amakhadi enombolo ye-athomu, iqembu, imasi nokuthambekela ngaphandle uma lawo maqiniso edingeka.
4. **Funda iqoqo elilodwa elinomkhawulo ocacile.** Ingxenye yezifundo, umndeni wama-elementi noma ama-elementi 1–20 ikunikeza isiphetho esibonakalayo. Uma kudingeka wonke angu-118, sebenzisa amaqoqo amancane ngokwezinombolo kunokuwafaka wonke ngosuku lokuqala.
5. **Xuba izinto ezididanayo ngemva kokuzijwayela ngayinye.** Faka `Co`, `C` no-`O` esikhathini esisodwa sokuzilolonga; xuba izinombolo ze-athomu ezisondelene ovame ukuziphambanisa. I-Nibomo ayikwenzi lokhu ngokuzenzakalelayo, ngakho zikhethele ukuhleleka kwedekhi noma amathegi.
6. **Zijwayeze ukwakheka kwethebula ngaphandle kwedekhi.** Gcwalisa ithebula elingenalutho bese usho ngokuzwakalayo izindawo ezikusiza ukhumbule amaqembu noma izinkathi. Amakhadi ahlukene awafundisi ukuhleleka kwezindawo ngokwawo.
7. **Buyela emsebenzini wamakhemikhali.** Funda amafomula, chaza ukuthambekela, bhala ukuhleleka kwama-electron, uxazulule nemibuzo ongayijwayele. Iphutha ngalinye eliphindekayo lingakhiqiza ikhadi elincane lokulilungisa.

## Imibuzo evamile ngethebula le-periodic

### Mangaki ama-elementi kuthebula le-periodic ngo-2026?

Kunama-elementi aziwayo angu-118. Ihayidrojini iyinombolo 1 kanti i-oganesson iyinombolo 118. Amathebula amanje e-ACS, NIST nawe-IUPAC wonke agcina ku-elementi 118.

### Iyiphi i-elementi engu-118?

I-oganesson, enophawu oluthi `Og`, yi-elementi engu-118. I-IUPAC yagunyaza igama nophawu lwayo lwamanje ngoNovemba 2016 kanye ne-nihonium (`Nh`), i-moscovium (`Mc`) ne-tennessine (`Ts`).

### Ingabe abafundi kufanele bakhumbule wonke ama-elementi angu-118?

Kufanele bawakhumbule wonke kuphela uma lokho kudingeka ezifundweni noma esivivinyweni. Abafundi abaningi badinga iqoqo elincane, njengama-elementi 1–20, ama-elementi avamile kumafomula, imindeni ekhethiwe noma ingxenye yezifundo abafunda yona manje. Qinisekisa nendlela yokubuza: ukubona `Na` nokuyibhala usuka egameni elithi “i-sodium” kungamakhono ahlukene.

### Ingabe inombolo ye-athomu iyafana nemasi ye-athomu?

Cha. Inombolo ye-athomu iyinani lama-proton futhi ikhomba i-elementi. Inombolo yemasi ibala ama-proton nama-neutron ku-nuclide eyodwa; imasi ye-athomu nesisindo se-athomu kuchaza amanani ahlukene ahlobene nemasi. Sebenzisa igama nomthombo okushiwo ezifundweni zakho ngokuqondile.

### Iyiphi indlela eshesha kakhulu yokufunda amagama nezimpawu zama-elementi?

Chaza iqoqo elincane kunawo wonke elidingekayo, uzihlole ngaphandle kwamanothi, bese wenza amakhadi ngezindlela zokubuza owehlulekile kuzo kuphela. Ngemva kwalokho, hlola ukukhumbula kwakho ngethebula elingenalutho nemibuzo yangempela yamakhemikhali.

## Sebenzisa ithebula, bese ulivala

Sebenzisa ithebula elinemigqa engu-118 uma udinga ukubheka imininingwane ethembekile. Ukuze uzijwayeze ukukhumbula, lifihle, uzihlole ngqo ngokudingwa yizifundo zakho, bese wenza amakhadi amancane ngamaphutha akho. Buyela-ke ethebuleni elingenalutho, kufomula, encazelweni yokuthambekela noma ezibalweni—lapho ulwazi kufanele lusebenze khona.
