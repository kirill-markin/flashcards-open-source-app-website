Umzuliswano we-Flashcards ku-Duolingo uqala ngamakhadi amahlanu. Ikhadi ngalinye libonisa igama ngolimi osulwaziyo, bese kufanele usho ukuhunyushwa kwalo ngolimi olufundayo—noma ukubhale lapho kungelula ukukhuluma.

Leyo mininingwane emincane isichaza kangcono lesi sici kunegama laso. Lena akuyona idekhi yakho yesilulumagama ongayivula noma nini. Kungumsebenzi omfushane wokuzama ukukhumbula ngaphakathi kwezifundo ze-Duolingo. Ikhadi oliphendule kahle liyanyamalala. Uma uphendule kabi, ikhadi libonisa futhi lidlale impendulo, bese libuyela ngemuva kwenqwaba ukuze uzame futhi.

Kubafundi abaningi, lokho kwanele. Uma ulokhu ukhohlwa amagama ambalwa ngemva kwesifundo, idekhi yesibili ingasiza—kodwa ibe yalezo zindawo ezilokhu zikuhlupha kuphela. Ukukopisha wonke amagama ku-Duolingo kungamane kukwenzele umsebenzi owengeziwe.

## Asebenza kanjani amakhadi e-Duolingo

[Duolingo Flashcards (English)](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-flashcards/) ichaza lo mjikelezo:

1. Inqwaba inamakhadi amahlanu, ngalinye linegama ngolimi lwakho.
2. Usho ukuhunyushwa ngolimi olufundayo ngokuzwakalayo noma ukubhale.
3. Ikhadi oliphendule kahle liphenduka libe luhlaza bese liyanyamalala enqwabeni.
4. Ikhadi oliphendule kabi liyaphenduka, liveze ukuhunyushwa, likudlale ngokuzwakalayo, bese liya ngemuva kwenqwaba.
5. Izimpendulo ezintathu ezifanele zanele ukuze uphumelele kulo msebenzi.
6. Umzuliswano uphela ngesifinyezo salokho okuhambe kahle nalokho okungadinga ukuziqeqesha okwengeziwe.

I-Duolingo ithi i-Flashcards ivela ezifundweni. Umhlahlandlela wayo ka-2026 [Writing practice (English)](https://blog.duolingo.com/covering-all-the-bases-duolingos-approach-to-writing-skills/) nawo uyichaza njengomsebenzi wokukhumbula ngokukhuluma noma ngokubhala. Wona lowo mhlahlandlela ubala **Words** ngokwehlukana kuthebhu ethi Practice, lapho abafundi bengabuyela khona kusilulumagama sesifundo futhi baqondanise amagama nezincazelo zawo.

Ngakho uma ufuna amakhadi esilulumagama e-Duolingo, kunezinto ezimbili ezahlukene okufanele uzihlukanise:

- **Flashcards** ngumsebenzi wokukhumbula onamakhadi amahlanu ngaphakathi kwezifundo.
- **Words** yindawo ehlukile yokuziqeqeshela isilulumagama i-Duolingo eyichaza kuthebhu ethi Practice.

Ngamanye amazwi, i-Flashcards ne-Words akuwona amagama amabili esikrini esisodwa: bheka umsebenzi wamakhadi amahlanu ezifundweni, bese ubheka i-Words kuthebhu ethi Practice.

## Izinhloso zomsebenzi ose-app nezedekhi yesikhathi eside zihlukene

Amakhadi e-Duolingo akunika ithuba lokuzama ukukhumbula isilulumagama sesifundo ngaso leso sikhathi. Idekhi yakho inika izinto ozikhethile indawo yokubuyela kuzo ezinsukwini nasezinyangeni ezilandelayo.

| Umbuzo | Umsebenzi we-Duolingo wamakhadi amahlanu | Idekhi yesikhathi eside elawulwa umfundi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Itholakala kuphi? | Ngaphakathi kwesifundo se-Duolingo | Ohlelweni lwamakhadi okufunda olukhethayo |
| Yini eqala ukubuyekeza? | Isifundo siveza inqwaba yamakhadi amahlanu | Amakhadi afika esikhathini sokubuyekezwa ngokohlelo lwaleyo app |
| Uphendula ini? | Ukuhunyushwa ngolimi olufundayo, ngokukhuluma noma ngokubhala | Noma yimuphi umbuzo oqondile owubhalayo: igama, ibinzana, umehluko, noma ukulungiswa kwephutha |
| Kwenzekani ngemva kwephutha? | Impendulo iyavezwa futhi idlalwe; ikhadi libuyela ngemuva kwenqwaba | Ukala ukuthi kube lula kangakanani ukukhumbula noma uphinde ikhadi ngokwenqubo yokubuyekeza yohlelo |
| Isebenza kahle kuphi? | Ukuziqeqeshela ukukhumbula ngokushesha umongo wesifundo usacacile | Ukugcina ubuyekeza izinto ezikuhluphayo nezibalulekile kuwe ngokuhamba kwesikhathi |
| Ubani olawula okubhalwe ekhadini? | I-Duolingo ihlinzeka umsebenzi | Ungahlela, ususe, uhlukanise futhi ubhale kabusha ikhadi |

Enye ayithathi indawo yenye. Umzuliswano wamakhadi amahlanu ungaba yiyo yonke inqubo yakho. Idekhi ehlukile iba wusizo kuphela uma igcina okuthile obuyoqhubeka ukukukhohlwa ngaphandle kwayo.

## Lapho i-Duolingo iyodwa yanele khona

Cishe awudingi idekhi yesibili uma:

- ukhumbula amagama kahle ezifundweni ezilandelayo;
- ukulungiswa okukodwa kwanele ukuze uliqonde iphutha;
- ukuziqeqesha kwe-Words nendlela ejwayelekile yezifundo sekuvele kukunika amathuba anele okukhumbula;
- uhlangana nesilulumagama esifanayo ngokufunda, ezingxoxweni, noma ekilasini;
- ukunakekela olunye uhlu lokubuyekeza kungathatha isikhathi sokusebenzisa ulimi ngempela.

Ukungagcini nelilodwa ikhadi ngemva kweseshini enhle kungumphumela owamukelekayo.

Idekhi yakho iba wusizo kakhulu uma into efanayo ikuhlula kaningi, uma ukulungiswa kwephutha kuveza ukungaqondi okuthile, noma uma ibinzana libalulekile engxoxweni olindele ukuba nayo. Lena yindlela yokukhetha, hhayi ikhophi yokugcina sonke isifundo.

## Yini okufanele uyibuyekeze kamuva

Ngemva kwesifundo, bheka izimpawu kunokubheka izigaba zokuqukethwe. Ikhadi lifaneleka kakhulu uma okungenani okukodwa kwalokhu kuyiqiniso:

- **Usuhluleke kulo ngaphezu kokukodwa.** Uyayibona impendulo lapho usuyibonisiwe, kodwa awukwazi ukuyikhiqiza lapho ubuzwa.
- **Ukulungiswa kushintshe ukuqonda kwakho.** Usebenzise i-preposition, ubulili begama, ukuguqulwa kwesenzo, noma ukuhleleka kwamagama ngendlela engafanele, futhi manje wazi kahle ukuthi kungani.
- **Izakhi ezimbili ezifanayo zilokhu zikudida.** Ukuqhathanisa okuncane kungavimbela iphutha elifanayo ngokuzayo.
- **Ufuna ukusebenzisa ibinzana ngaphandle kohlelo lokusebenza.** Lilungele umlayezo, uhambo, ikilasi, noma ingxoxo oyikhathalelayo ngempela.
- **Umbuzo ungahlala uqondile.** Ungahlola into eyodwa ecacile ngaphandle kokukopisha yonke incazelo.

Yeka igama uma belilisha nje, lihlekisa, noma likudide isikhashana. Ukuhlangana negama akusho ukuthi usuzibophezele ekulibuyekezeni izinyanga eziningi.

## Inqubo yokukhetha ngemva kweseshini ngayinye

Le nqubo kufanele ithathe imizuzu embalwa, ingaguquli isifundo se-Duolingo sibe umsebenzi wokufaka idatha.

### 1. Qeda isifundo ngaphambi kokubhala amanothi

Hlala emsebenzini ngesikhathi uphendula ukuze ukubhala amanothi kungaphazamisi ukukhumbula. Hlunga izinto ezingaba amakhadi ngemva kwalokho.

Ekugcineni, sebenzisa isifinyezo nalokho okukhumbulayo ukuze ubone izinto ezikuhluphile. Uma kungekho okugqamayo, yima lapho.

### 2. Gcina iqoqo lincane

Uma kunezinto ezimbalwa ezilungisiwe ezifanele ukugcinwa, izinto ezi-3–8 sezivele ziyiqoqo eligcwele. Kuthathe njengobubanzi obusebenzayo, hhayi inani okufanele ulifinyelele noma umkhawulo wesayensi. Bala sonke isifundo noma iseshini yokufunda, hhayi inqwaba eyodwa yamakhadi amahlanu kuphela. Iseshini ejwayelekile ingaveza into eyodwa noma ingavezi lutho.

Bhala umongo owanele kuphela ukuze uqonde ukulungiswa kamuva:

```text
cita = appointment; forgot it again
depender de; used the wrong preposition
se me olvidaron las llaves = I forgot my keys
por/para: gift intended for Ana
```

Lokhu kubhala ngesandla kwenziwa ngenhloso. Akuqoqi idatha ngokuzenzakalela ku-Duolingo noma kukopishe sonke isifundo, futhi akudingi ukukhipha idatha noma i-API. Lo msebenzi omncane owengeziwe ukuphoqa ukuba wenze isinqumo esiwusizo: ingabe le nto ibaluleke ngokwanele ukuba ibe okunye okufanele ukubuyekeze?

### 3. Guqula inothi ngalinye libe umsebenzi owodwa wokukhumbula

Amanothi okulungiswa kwamaphutha awavamile ukuba amakhadi aselungele ukusetshenziswa. Bhala kabusha inothi ngalinye ukuze kube nempendulo ecacile elindelekile.

| Uphawu olusuka esifundweni | Ikhadi elibuthaka | Ikhadi elingcono | Elikuhlolayo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Uphinde wakhohlwa `la cita` | Ngaphambili: “Isilulumagama sesifundo” / Ngemuva: uhlu lwamagama ayishumi | Ngaphambili: `Tengo una ___ con el dentista.` / Ngemuva: `cita` | Igama elilodwa elilokhu likuhlupha, lisemongweni |
| Usebenzise i-preposition engafanele ngemva kuka-`depender` | Ngaphambili: “Chaza ama-preposition esi-Spanish” / Ngemuva: isigaba sohlelo lolimi | Ngaphambili: `Todo depende ___ resultado.` / Ngemuva: `del` — `Todo depende del resultado.` | I-preposition eyodwa nokuhlanganiswa kwayo ne-athikili, kusemongweni |
| Ufuna ukugcina indlela elungisiwe yokuthi “Ngikhohlwe okhiye bami” | Ngaphambili: yonke ingxoxo elungisiwe / Ngemuva: ukuhunyushwa kwayo | Ngaphambili: “Usebenzisa u-*se me*, yisho ukuthi: Ngikhohlwe okhiye bami.” / Ngemuva: `Se me olvidaron las llaves.` | Indlela eyodwa ewusizo yokwakha umusho |
| Udide u-`por` no-`para` | Ngaphambili: “por no-para” / Ngemuva: yonke imithetho | Ngaphambili: `Este regalo es ___ Ana.` / Ngemuva: `para` | Umehluko owodwa oqondile |

Ukuthi ikhadi libuza ngakuphi kuncike enhlosweni yakho. Uma inkinga kungukukhuluma, beka incazelo noma isimo ngaphambili bese ukhiqiza impendulo ngolimi olufundayo. Uma inkinga kungukufunda, bonisa ibinzana lolimi olufundayo bese ubuza incazelo yalo. Ungazigcina zombili izindlela entweni ebalulekile, kodwa ungaphindi yonke into ngokuzenzakalela.

Ukuze uthole ezinye izinhlobo zamakhadi, bheka [Language-learning flashcards (English)](/blog/how-to-use-flashcards-for-language-learning/).

### 4. Hlola ikhadi ngaphambi kokulihlelela ukubuyekezwa

Funda izinhlangothi zombili ngaphandle komongo wesifundo. Zibuze:

- Ingabe kunempendulo eyodwa ongayisekela?
- Ingabe ikhadi lihlola iphutha engilenze ngempela?
- Ingabe isibonelo sizwakala ngokwemvelo futhi siphelele ngokwanele ukuba siqondakale?
- Ingabe ngisazolikhathalela leli binzana ngemva kwenyanga?
- Ingabe ngilugcinile uphawu olusehlamvini, i-athikili, noma umniningwane wohlelo lolimi oshintsha impendulo?

Uma umbuzo uvumela ukuhunyushwa okuningana okufanele ngokulinganayo, engeza umongo noma uwenze uqonde kakhudlwana. “Yisho u-*still* ngesi-Spanish” akucacile ngokwanele; ukuhunyushwa okuhle kakhulu kuyashintsha ngokwencazelo. Umbuzo ophelele njengokuthi “She is still here” (“Usakhona lapha”) wenza okudingekayo kucace kakhudlwana.

### 5. Buyekeza idekhi encane, bese uqhubeka nokususa

Uma amakhadi esecacile, vumela uhlelo lwe-spaced repetition olukhethile luwahlelele ukubuyekezwa. Susa noma ubhale kabusha ikhadi uma ukubuyekeza kuveza ukuthi umbuzo awucacile, impendulo inkulu kakhulu, noma leyo nto ayisabalulekile.

Bheka uhlu lwamakhadi asesikhathini sokubuyekezwa kunokujaha inani eliphelele lamakhadi amasha. Uma ukwengeza amakhadi ngemva kwesifundo ngasinye kwenza ukubuyekeza kwakusasa kucasule, gcina ambalwa.

## I-FSRS ingena kuphi—futhi ayikwazi ukusiza kuphi

Kule nqubo, hlukanisa lezi zingxenye ezimbili. Incazelo esemthethweni ye-Flashcards ye-Duolingo ichaza umsebenzi wokuzama ukukhumbula nomjikelezo wokuphinda uzame. Kudekhi ehlukile esebenzisa **FSRS**, uhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda luqopha ukubuyekeza kwakho, bese isihleli sisebenzisa lowo mlando ukubala ukuthi ikhadi ngalinye eligciniwe kufanele libuye nini.

Lokho kuyasiza ngoba ukukhumbula akufani kuzo zonke izinto. Ungashesha ukukhumbula u-`la cita` kalula, kanti u-`depender de` aqhubeke ekudida. La magama awadingi ukubuya ngohlelo olulodwa lwezikhathi ezingaguquki.

I-FSRS ayikwazi ukuhlenga ikhadi elibi.

Ingahlela ngokunembile kakhulu ukuthi ubuyekezwe nini umbuzo othi “Chaza konke ukusetshenziswa kuka-por no-para”, kodwa umbuzo usazoba banzi kakhulu. Ingabuyisa ukuhunyushwa okungacacile kaningi, kodwa ukuphindaphinda ngeke kwenze umbuzo ucace. Ikhwalithi yekhadi iza kuqala; ukuhlela isikhathi kulandela ngemva kwalokho.

Mayelana nesihleli uqobo, funda [FSRS guide (English)](/blog/what-is-fsrs/). Mayelana nokwakha imibuzo, sebenzisa [Better flashcards (English)](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/).

## Ingabe udinga ukukhipha amakhadi e-Duolingo?

Akudingeki kule nqubo. Inhloso ukuhambisa izinto ezimbalwa ozikhethile, hhayi isizindalwazi sesilulumagama.

Qeda iseshini, khetha amaphutha aphindaphindayo, bese ufaka lezo zinto ngesandla ohlelweni lokubuyekeza osulusebenzisa. Lokhu kwenza inqubo inganciki kunoma iyiphi indlela yokukhipha idatha, futhi kugwema ukuthi ukufuna “ukukhipha amakhadi e-Duolingo” kube ukuzingela indlela yokuhambisa idatha eningi okungenzeka ungayidingi.

Uma ufuna ukuwafaka ku-Anki, dala amakhadi owakhethile ku-Anki. Inqubo **yokusuka ku-Duolingo kuya ku-Anki** akudingeki ibe nzima kunalokho. Uma i-Anki isivele inamadekhi akho okufunda ulimi, asikho isizathu sokushintsha uhlelo ngenxa yala makhadi amasha.

Uma ufuna idekhi entsha oyilawulayo kwenye indawo, imithetho efanayo yokukhetha amakhadi isasebenza. Ungahambisi amagama amahlanu esifundo ngenxa nje yokuthi bekunamakhadi amahlanu esikrinini. Hambisa kuphela izinto ezibonise ukuthi zidinga ukunakwa isikhathi eside.

## Impendulo esebenzisekayo

Amakhadi e-Duolingo ayimizuliswano emifushane yokukhumbula enamakhadi amahlanu: ubona igama ngolimi lwakho, ukhiqize ukuhunyushwa ngolimi olufundayo, bese uphinda uzame amakhadi akwehlulile ngaphakathi komsebenzi. Angena kahle ngaphakathi kwesifundo futhi angaba yikho konke ukubuyekeza isilulumagama okudingayo.

Dala idekhi yesibili kuphela ngezinto ezisasele zikuhlupha: amaphutha aphindaphindayo, ukulungiswa okushintshe ukuqonda kwakho, namabinzana ofuna ukuwasebenzisa kamuva ngempela. Bhala lezo zinto ngesandla, guqula ngayinye ibe umbuzo owodwa ocacile, bese uvumela i-FSRS ihlele ukuthi uzoyibuyekeza nini.

I-Duolingo igcina umsebenzi usondelene nesifundo. Idekhi yakho igcina inani elincane lezinto eziwusizo ezikuhluphayo litholakala ukuze uzibuyekeze isikhathi eside. Le ndlela yokwehlukanisa imisebenzi ilula ukuqhubeka nayo, futhi ukukhetha okumbalwa okubalulekile kuyigcina iwusizo.
