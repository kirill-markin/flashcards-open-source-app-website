## Sebenzisa isakhiwo se-TOEFL iBT esethulwa mhla zingama-21 kuJanuwari 2026

I-ETS yethula i-TOEFL iBT ebuyekeziwe emhlabeni wonke [mhla zingama-21 kuJanuwari 2026](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-updates-flyer-for-students.pdf). [Ikhasi layo lamanje lokuqukethwe kwesivivinyo](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/about/content.html) libala izinhlobo zemisebenzi esetshenziswa kuzo zonke izingxenye ezine. [Uhlelo lwesivivinyo lwango-2026](https://www.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-specifications-2026.pdf) luthi izingxenye zokufunda nokulalela zisebenzisa uhlelo oluzivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho ngezigaba ezimbili: indlela owenza ngayo emojulini yokuqala isiza ekukhetheni imojuli yesibili. Izingxenye zokubhala nokukhuluma ziqhubeka ngendlela emisiwe, ngaphandle kokuzivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho. I-ETS inikeza imininingwane eyengeziwe [esimemezelweni sayo sezinguquko](https://www.ets.org/newsroom/toefl-transformation-announcement.html).

Amaphuzu engxenye ngayinye nawesivivinyo sonke manje abikwa esikalini sika-1–6, ngezikhawu zohhafu wephuzu. I-ETS iphinde inikeze isilinganiso esiqhathanisekayo samaphuzu esivivinyo sonke esikalini sika-0–120 [kuze kube nguJanuwari 2028](https://www.ets.org/toefl/blog/understanding-toefl-ibt-score-displays.html). Hlola imfuneko yamanje yesikhungo ngasinye kunokuba ubambe ngekhanda indlela yokuguqula amaphuzu esencwadini endala yokulungiselela.

I-ETS ishicilela izikhathi eziyisisekelo ezilinganiselwayo futhi ixwayisa ngokuthi isikhathi nenani lemibuzo kungashintsha njengoba isivivinyo sizivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho. Lokhu kwenza ikhadi elithi `Ingxenye yokufunda ihlale inemibuzo engu-X ncamashi` lingathembeki. Amagama emisebenzi, izakhiwo zolimi namaphutha owaphindayo yizinto ezifaneleke kangcono ukuthi uzikhumbule.

## Yakha ama-flashcard esilulumagama se-TOEFL usebenzisa umongo nesimo segama

Ikhadi elinencazelo kuphela lingakwethulela igama. Amakhadi anomongo akhombisa ukuthi uyakwazi yini ukubona incazelo yalo, ukukhetha isimo esifanele nokulisebenzisa emshweni.

Incazelo:

- Ngaphambili: `allocate`
- Ngemuva: `to distribute for a purpose`

Incazelo ngokomongo:

- Ngaphambili: Emshweni othi `The university allocated additional rooms to first-year students`, lisho ukuthini igama elithi `allocated`?
- Ngemuva: Assigned or distributed for a particular purpose.

Isimo segama:

- Ngaphambili: Sebenzisa isimo segama elithi `allocate` ukuze uqedele umusho, bese usho ukuthi liyiluphi uhlobo lwegama: `The committee approved an ______ of funds to the library.`
- Ngemuva: `allocation` — ibizo eliza ngemva kuka-`an` nangaphambi kuka-`of`.

Ukusebenzisa igama:

- Ngaphambili: Sebenzisa isimo esifanele segama elithi `allocate` ukuze uqedele lo musho: `The department will ______ more funding to laboratory safety.`
- Ngemuva: `allocate`.

Akuvamile ukuthi udinge zonke lezi zinhlobo zamakhadi. Faka enye indlela yokubuza uma igama lilokhu likuhlula ekuzilolongeni, uma ulibona uliqonde kodwa ungakwazi ukulisebenzisa, noma uma amagama anomsuka ofanayo edala amaphutha aphindaphindayo. Gcina umusho umfushane ngokwanele ukuthi kudingeke ukhumbule impendulo kunokuba ubone nje isigaba osibambe ngekhanda.

Uma ufuna iqoqo eselilungile, [Ama-flashcard e-TOEFL iBT: izinhlobo zemisebenzi ezingu-12, isilulumagama namasu](/zu/catalog/packages/toefl-ibt-task-strategy-flashcards/) lihlanganisa zonke izinhlobo zemisebenzi yamanje ezingu-12 ngamakhadi angu-180 aphathelene nesilulumagama nezimo zamagama, izakhiwo zemisho, izinqumo emisebenzini nezinkomba zamasu ongazisebenzisa futhi. Lahlolwa ukuze kuqinisekiswe ukuthi lihambisana nesakhiwo sango-2026.

## Guqula izakhiwo zemisho zibe amakhadi amancane akwenza wakhe umusho

I-Build a Sentence ihlola ngokuqondile ukwakheka kwemisho. Izakhiwo ezifanayo zikusiza ugcine i-imeyili noma impendulo engxoxweni yezifundo icacile ngaphansi kwengcindezi yesikhathi.

Amakhadi awusizo akwenza wakhe isakhiwo ngokwakho:

- Ngaphambili: Hlela kabusha lezi zingxenye: `asked / whether / the deadline / Mia / had changed`
- Ngemuva: `Mia asked whether the deadline had changed.`

- Ngaphambili: Lungisa umbuzo ofakwe phakathi komusho: `Could you tell me where is the advising office?`
- Ngemuva: `Could you tell me where the advising office is?`

- Ngaphambili: Hlanganisa le mibono ngomusho ovuma iphuzu elilodwa kodwa wethule eliphikisayo: `Online classes are flexible. Some students need more direct interaction.`
- Ngemuva: `Although online classes are flexible, some students need more direct interaction.`

- Ngaphambili: Sebenzisa u-`Could you confirm whether ...?` ukuze ubuze ngesizotha nangokuqondile ukuthi umhlangano wokufundisana uqala ngo-9:00 ekuseni yini.
- Ngemuva: `Could you confirm whether the workshop begins at 9:00 a.m.?`

Gcina impendulo eyodwa ecacile, noma izimpendulo ezimbalwa kakhulu ezamukelekayo, ngemuva kwekhadi. I-imeyili ephelele inkulu kakhulu ukuba ibe sekhadini elilodwa futhi yenza kube lula kakhulu ukuzinika isilinganiso esingacacile.

Nge-Write an Email ne-Write for an Academic Discussion, sebenzisa imibuzo yohlu lokuhlola esikhundleni semibhalo yezimpendulo ephelele:

- Ngibhalela bani, futhi yiliphi iphimbo elifanele?
- Yiliphi iphuzu eliceliwe engingakakhulumi ngalo?
- Yisiphi isizathu noma isibonelo esiqondile esisekela umbono wami?
- Yiliphi iphutha lohlelo lolimi eliphindaphindayo ekubhaleni kwami okunqunyelwe isikhathi?

Le mibuzo iyaqhubeka nokuba wusizo lapho isihloko sishintsha. Izimpendulo eziphelele ezibanjwe ngekhanda zivame ukungasifaneli isimo.

## Yenza ikhadi ngalinye lamasu ligxile ephuzwini eliqondile

Amanye amaphutha aveza isikhala olwazini lolimi. Amanye akhombisa ukuthi ukhethe isinyathelo esingafanele phakathi komsebenzi. Guqula lawo ohlobo lwesibili abe amakhadi amasu kuphela uma isifundo sivela ephutheni eliqinisekisiwe futhi singasetshenziswa futhi.

Izibonelo:

- Ngaphambili: Ku-Read in Daily Life, yini okufanele uyibone ngaphambi kokuhlolisisa imininingwane emincane?
- Ngemuva: Inhloso yombhalo, izethameli zawo nesakhiwo sawo.

- Ngaphambili: Ku-Listen and Choose a Response, yini eyenza impendulo ibe yiphutha noma iphinda igama elisembuzweni?
- Ngemuva: Ayiphenduli ngendlela isikhulumi esiyidingayo, njengokuqinisekisa, ukulungisa, ukuchaza noma ukuphendula umbuzo.

- Ngaphambili: Ngesikhathi esidlule ngilalele inkulumo yezifundo, yini engayithatha ngephutha njengomqondo oyinhloko?
- Ngemuva: Ngakhetha isibonelo sokuqala esikhundleni sephuzu elalisekelwa yizibonelo.

- Ngaphambili: Iyiphi indlela ongayivumelanisa nesimo yokwelula impendulo ye-Take an Interview?
- Ngemuva: Phendula ngokuqondile, unikeze isizathu esisodwa, bese ufaka isibonelo esiqondile noma umphumela.

- Ngaphambili: Ngaphambi kokuthumela impendulo ye-Write an Email, yini okufanele ngiyiqhathanise nemiyalelo?
- Ngemuva: Wonke amaphuzu okuqukethwe aceliwe, izethameli nenhloso.

Ikhadi lenkulumo yezifundo lisebenzisa amazwi omfundi uqobo ngoba liqopha umkhuba awubonile kuye. Iseluleko esijwayelekile singaba nzima kakhulu ukusikhumbula nokusisebenzisa.

Sebenzisa uhlaka lwempendulo yomlomo ongakwazi ukuluvumelanisa nombuzo. I-ETS yeluleka ngokuthi ungasebenzisi izimpendulo ezibanjwe ngekhanda esiqondisweni sayo [samaphutha avamile ku-TOEFL iBT](https://www.ets.org/toefl/blog/common-toefl-ibt-mistakes-you-should-avoid.html). Inkomba emfushane yokwelula impendulo ingakusiza uyihlele. Inkulumo ephelele egcinwe ngekhanda ayikuqeqeshi ukukhuluma ungazilungiselelanga.

## Guqula amaphutha aqinisekisiwe ekuzilolongeni okusemthethweni abe amakhadi

Guqula iphutha lokuzilolonga libe yikhadi kuphela ngemva kokuba usuyazi ukuthi yini engahambanga kahle. Ukuqagela imbangela kungakufundisa iphutha elifanayo futhi.

Sebenzisa le nqubo:

1. Khetha izinto zokufunda [esikhungweni samanje sezinsiza zokulungiselela se-ETS](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/prepare.html) futhi uqinisekise ukuthi zihlanganisa isivivinyo esisetshenziswa kusukela mhla zingama-21 kuJanuwari 2026.
2. Qedela iqoqo elilodwa lemisebenzi ngaphansi kwezimo ezishiwo. Landela isikhathi esibekiwe futhi udlale isiqeshana ngasinye somsindo kanye kuphela, uma insiza ivumela lezo zimo.
3. Qopha igama lomsebenzi, impendulo yakho nendawo eqondile lapho ungaqinisekanga khona.
4. Qinisekisa umphumela usebenzisa izimpendulo ezisemthethweni, isiqondiso sokunikeza amaphuzu noma imiyalelo yomsebenzi ngaphambi kokubhala uhlaka lwekhadi.
5. Hlukanisa iphutha ngohlobo lwalo: isilulumagama, isakhiwo somusho, isinqumo emsebenzini, ukusebenza ekulaleleni noma ekukhulumeni, ukubhala noma ukuphatha isikhathi.
6. Yakha ikhadi kuphela uma ukukhumbula iphuzu elilodwa elifushane kungalungisa imbangela yephutha.
7. Phinde uhlole okufundile ngombuzo ohlukile osemthethweni ohambisana nesivivinyo samanje.

Nazi izindlela ezinhlanu ezivamile zokwenza lokho:

| Iphutha eliqinisekisiwe | Ikhadi eliwusizo | Ukuzilolonga okudingekayo ngemva kwalokho |

| Uqedele igama ngesijobelelo esingafanele ngoba umusho ubudinga ibizo | Umusho omfushane onomongo ocela ibizo elingekho nenkomba yohlelo lolimi | Esinye isigaba se-Complete the Words ongakaze usibone |
| Usebenzise ukuhleleka kwamagama kombuzo ngaphakathi komushwana ofakwe komunye umusho | Ikhadi lokulungisa noma lokuhlela kabusha leso sakhiwo somushwana | Iqoqo elisha le-Build a Sentence elinqunyelwe isikhathi |
| Ukhethe imininingwane yenkulumo yezifundo esikhundleni sephuzu layo eliyinhloko | Ikhadi lephethini yephutha lakho elehlukanisa ubufakazi nephuzu elisekelwa yibo | Enye inkulumo yezifundo edlalwa kanye kuphela |

Ungakopisheli umbuzo osemthethweni ophelele nencazelo yempendulo ekhadini elilodwa. Bhala isifundo esiqinisekisiwe ngamazwi akho. Shiya umbuzo ophelele ezintweni zokuzilolonga, ugcine eqoqweni lamakhadi into encane kuphela okufanele uyikhumbule.

## Hlola iqoqo elidala le-TOEFL ngaphambi kokulibuyekeza

Amaqoqo akhiwa ngaphambi komhla zingama-21 kuJanuwari 2026 angahlanganisa isiNgisi esisasebenza nemininingwane yesivivinyo engasasetshenziswa. Gcina ulimi oluwusizo, kodwa hlola wonke amakhadi izimpendulo zawo ezincike esakhiweni sesivivinyo.

Qala ngokusesha eqoqweni:

- amagama emisebenzi emidala afana ne-Integrated Speaking noma i-Integrated Writing
- izitatimende ezithi inani leziqeshana zemibhalo, izinkulumo zezifundo, izingxoxo, imisebenzi noma imibuzo aliguquki
- izikhathi ezindala zezingxenye zesivivinyo
- ukubhekiselwa emisebenzini emine yokukhuluma
- imithetho yamaphuzu engxenye esikalini sika-0–30 ethulwa njengendlela yamanje yokubonisa amaphuzu
- amathempulethi aphelele akhelwe uhlobo lwempendulo olungasasetshenziswa

Bese uhlukanisa imiphumela ngamaqembu amathathu:

1. **Gcina:** isilulumagama esingaguquki, uhlelo lolimi, amagama avame ukuhambisana nomehluko ojwayelekile olimini lwezifundo.
2. **Bhala kabusha:** ulimi oluwusizo olusafakwe esakhiweni somsebenzi omdala. Ikhadi elidala lokubhala okuhlanganisa imithombo, eligxile olimini lokuqhathanisa, lisengaguqulwa libe yikhadi elicacile lesakhiwo somusho.
3. **Susa noma ugcine endaweni yezinto ezingasasetshenziswa:** imiyalelo yemisebenzi, izikhathi, ukunikezwa kwamaphuzu namathempulethi achaza kuphela isakhiwo sangaphambi komhla zingama-21 kuJanuwari 2026.

Okulandelayo, qhathanisa amathegi emisebenzi asele namagama emisebenzi yamanje engu-12 ye-ETS. Nikeza ikhadi ngalinye eliphathelene nesakhiwo sesivivinyo umthombo wamanje osemthethweni we-ETS. Ungafaka futhi ithegi ethi `verified-2026` nosuku owalihlola ngalo njengemininingwane eyengeziwe. Izinsuku zibalulekile lapha ngoba i-ETS ingasibuyekeza isivivinyo. Isilulumagama esingaguquki asidingi ukuphathwa ngendlela efanayo.

## Sebenzisa i-spaced repetition ukuze ukhumbule, hhayi ukulingisa isivivinyo

I-spaced repetition, okuwukubuyekeza ngezikhawu, isiza ukuthi igama, isakhiwo somusho noma iphutha eliphindaphindayo kuhlale kukhumbuleka phakathi namasonto amaningana. Noma kunjalo, ikhadi elinombuzo ngaphambili nempendulo ngemuva alikwazi ukusebenza njengesivivinyo solimi esizivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho.

Ukuhlelwa okulula kwe-spaced repetition kwanele:

- iqoqo elilodwa elithi `TOEFL 2026`
- amathegi engxenye nohlobo lwamanje lomsebenzi
- ithegi yomthombo efana no-`ETS-practice-1`
- ithegi yephutha efana no-`vocabulary`, `word-order`, `main-idea`, `tone` noma `omitted-requirement`
- izilinganiso zokubuyekeza ezisekelwe kulokho okukhumbule ngempela

[Ucwaningo lwama-flashcard edijithali](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41301342/) lwango-2025 lunikeza ubufakazi obunomkhawulo obusekela ukuzama ukukhumbula impendulo. Eseshini elawulwayo, abantu abangu-108 abakhuluma isiShayina nesiNgisi bafunda amapheya amagama angu-60 esiSwahili nesiShayina. Ukuzilolongela ukukhumbula impendulo bese uthola imininingwane yokuthi impendulo yakho ilungile yini kwaholela ekukhumbuleni okungcono ngosizo lwezinkomba kunokuphinda ufunde, ezivivinyweni ezenziwa ngemva kwesikhawu esifushane. Ababhali bathi ucwaningo olwenziwe ngesikhathi esifushane kanje lubeka umkhawulo eziphethweni ezingafinyelelwa ngokufunda kwesikhathi eside. Lolu cwaningo alufakazeli ukukhuphuka kwamaphuzu e-TOEFL, ukukwazi ukusebenzisa okufundiwe emisebenzini yesivivinyo ephelele, noma izinzuzo phakathi namasonto okulungiselela.

I-FSRS ihlela ukuthi uwabuyekeze nini amakhadi okhetha ukuwagcina. Ayikwazi ukwahlulela ukuthi umthombo usahambisana nesikhathi yini, impendulo ekhulunyiwe iyezwakala yini, noma impendulo ephelele iyazifeza yini izidingo zomsebenzi. [Indlela yokufundela isivivinyo nge-FSRS ngo-2026](/blog/how-to-study-for-an-exam-with-fsrs/) ichaza ukuthi ungagcina kanjani umsebenzi wokubuyekeza ungabi ngaphezu kwamandla akho ngaphambi kosuku olumisiwe lwesivivinyo.

## Gcina iqoqo lamakhadi lincane kunesifundo sokulungiselela

I-TOEFL ihlola amakhono olimi abanzi. Uma uguqula sonke isifundo, incwadi yesilulumagama noma isivivinyo sokuzilolonga sibe amakhadi, uzakhela olunye uhlelo lokufunda okufanele uluqede.

Faka ikhadi uma okungenani okukodwa kwalokhu kuyiqiniso:

- into okufanele uyifunde ivele izikhathi ezingaphezu kwesisodwa
- uyibonile wayiqonda kodwa awukwazanga ukuyisebenzisa ngokwakho
- ulokhu udida izimo ezimbili zamagama ezicishe zifane
- isinqumo esifanayo emsebenzini sidale elinye iphutha
- iphutha lokubhala noma lokukhuluma seliphenduke umkhuba

Ungalenzi ikhadi uma iphutha lidalwe ukuchofoza okungalungile, impendulo idinga i-eseyi ephelele, noma umsebenzi omusha ungakufundisa lokho kangcono. Imibuzo ebanzi efana nokuthi `Ngingaphumelela kanjani engxenyeni yokulalela?` nayo ikhuthaza ukuzinika isilinganiso esingacacile.

Umjikelezo osebenzisekayo wamasonto onke uthi:

1. buyekeza amakhadi okufike isikhathi sawo
2. qedela ukuzilolonga okusemthethweni okuhambisana nesivivinyo samanje
3. qinisekisa amaphutha
4. faka amakhadi ambalwa agxile ezintweni ezincane
5. bhala kabusha noma ususe noma yiliphi ikhadi obekunzima ukulinikeza isilinganiso
6. buyela ekuzilolongeni ngemisebenzi exubile enqunyelwe isikhathi

Iqoqo kufanele lihambisane kakhulu nezidingo zakho njengoba uqhubeka nokulungiselela. Uhlu lokuqala lungasiza, kodwa amaphutha aqinisekisiwe ezivivinyweni zokuzilolonga akukhombisa ukuthi yini efanele ukubuyekezwa kaningi.

## Lokho ama-flashcard e-TOEFL angeke akuthathele indawo

Ama-flashcard ahlanganisa izingxenye ezincane ongazikhumbula. I-TOEFL yamanje iphinde ikudinge ukuthi ufunde, ulalele, ukhulume futhi ubhale ngaphansi kwemikhawulo yayo yesikhathi nezimo zemisebenzi.

Awakwazi ukuthatha indawo yalokhu:

- **Izingxenye zokufunda nokulalela ezizivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho.** Ukubuyekeza ngamakhadi anombuzo ngaphambili nempendulo ngemuva akulingisi iziqeshana zemibhalo ongazijwayele, izimpendulo ezintsha ongakhetha kuzo, noma indlela ukusebenza emojulini yokuqala okunquma ngayo imojuli yesibili.
- **Ukulalela umsindo odlalwa kanye kuphela.** Umbhalo walokho okushiwo awukwazi ukukuqeqesha ukubona incazelo, inhloso noma impendulo efanele enkulumweni oyizwe kanye ngejubane lemvelo.
- **Ukukhuluma ngaphansi kwezimo zamanje.** Amakhadi ombhalo angagcina ibinzana noma inkomba yempendulo. I-Listen and Repeat idinga ukuphinda ngokunembile nangendlela ezwakalayo. I-Take an Interview idinga ukwelula impendulo ngaleso sikhathi ngaphandle kokulungiselela.
- **Ukubhala okuphelele.** Amakhadi emisho angalungisa ukuhleleka kwamagama namaphutha ohlelo lolimi aphindaphindayo. Awakwazi ukukhombisa ukuthi i-imeyili ephelele ihlanganisa wonke amaphuzu aceliwe yini, noma ukuthi impendulo engxoxweni yezifundo ihlangene, ihambisana nesihloko futhi isekelwe kahle yini.
- **Ukuzilolonga okusemthethweni kwamanje.** Izinsiza ze-ETS zinikeza imiyalelo yamanje, izakhiwo zemisebenzi, izikhathi nokulindelwe ekunikezweni kwamaphuzu. Amaqoqo abanye abakhiqizi angaphelelwa yisikhathi ngaphandle kwesexwayiso.

Sebenzisa [izinsiza ezisemthethweni zokulungiselela i-TOEFL](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/prepare.html) kulo lonke uhlelo lwakho lokufunda. Ukuzilolonga okugcwele kuphinde kuveze izinkinga zejubane nokunaka ezingeke zivezwe uhlu lwesilulumagama olulinde ukubuyekezwa.

## I-Nibomo ingena kuphi

[I-Nibomo](/zu/) isiza engxenyeni yale nqubo yokugcina okufundile engqondweni. Uhlelo lokusebenza lwewebhu lusekela amakhadi anohlangothi lwangaphambili nolwangemuva, ingxoxo ye-AI esebenzisa idatha yendawo yokusebenza namafayela anamathiselwe, kuhlanganise namafayela ombhalo olula, kanye nokubuyekeza nge-FSRS. Izinketho zokubuyekeza yilezi: Phinda (Again), Kunzima (Hard), Kuhle (Good) nokuthi Kulula (Easy).

Nansi inqubo eyodwa ecophelelayo:

1. Bhala irekhodi elifushane lamaphutha ngemva kokuzilolonga okusemthethweni kwamanje.
2. Qinisekisa ukulungiswa ngakunye usebenzisa izinto zokufunda ze-ETS.
3. Faka irekhodi engxoxweni ye-AI, noma ulinamathisele njengefayela elisekelwayo, bese ucela izinhlaka ezimbalwa zamakhadi anohlangothi lwangaphambili nolwangemuva, ngalinye ligxile entweni eyodwa.
4. Qhathanisa uhlaka ngalunye nokulungiswa okuqinisekisiwe bese uluhlela ngokwakho.
5. Buyekeza amakhadi owagcinayo nge-FSRS. Linganisa impendulo oyikhiqizile, hhayi ukuthi ikhadi belibukeka lijwayeleke kangakanani.

Qhubeka usebenzisa izinsiza ze-ETS emsindweni, ekubhaleni nasekukhulumeni okunqunyelwe isikhathi, nasekuzilolongeni ngesivivinyo esizivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho. Sebenzisa uhlelo lokusebenza phakathi kwalawo maseshini ukuze ukhumbule isilulumagama, izakhiwo zemisho, izinkomba zemisebenzi namaphutha aphindaphindayo.

Imisebenzi umkhiqizo oyisekelayo njengamanje ichazwe emakhasini athi [Izici](/zu/features/) nokuthi [Ukuqalisa](/zu/docs/getting-started/).

## Qala ngeqoqo lakho elilandelayo lemisebenzi esemthethweni yokuzilolonga

Vula iqoqo elilodwa lemisebenzi yamanje ye-ETS. Ngemva kokuliqeda ngaphansi kwezimo ezishiwo, qinisekisa iphutha ngalinye bese ugcina kuphela isilulumagama, isakhiwo somusho noma isinqumo esingangena ekhadini elicacile elinohlangothi lwangaphambili nolwangemuva.

Izinkinga zokulalela, ukukhuluma, ukufunda okuzivumelanisa nokusebenza kwakho nokubhala okuphelele zibuyisele ekuzilolongeni okulingisa izimo zangempela. Hlola noma yiliphi iqoqo elidala ngokuqhathanisa namagama emisebenzi yamanje engu-12, bese uvumela i-FSRS ihlele izikhathi zamakhadi asele.

Yilona iqhaza eliwusizo lama-flashcard e-TOEFL ngo-2026: usizo oluncane lokukhumbula, olwakhiwe ngesivivinyo ozosibhala ngempela.
