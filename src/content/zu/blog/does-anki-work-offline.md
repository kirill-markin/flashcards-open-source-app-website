## Ukubuyekeza nokuhlela ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kugcinwa kuleyo divayisi kuqala

Uma uphendula amakhadi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, i-Anki iqopha lokho kubuyekeza eqoqweni elikuleyo divayisi. Uhlelo lokuhlela ukuthi amakhadi abuye nini luqhubeka ngokwesimo esigcinwe lapho. Amanothi amasha nezinguquko ezivamile nazo zigcinwa kudivayisi. Lezi zinguquko aziveli kwenye idivayisi uze uphinde uxhumeke futhi uvumelanise.

Ukuvumelanisa nge-AnkiWeb akuphoqelekile uma ufunda kudivayisi eyodwa kuphela. Lokhu kudlulisa izinguquko zeqoqo phakathi kwamadivayisi. [Incwadi yemiyalelo yokuvumelanisa ye-Anki](https://docs.ankiweb.net/syncing.html) ithi, ezimweni ezivamile, ukubuyekezwa kwamakhadi nokuhlelwa kwamanothi okwenziwe ezindaweni eziningi kungahlanganiswa. Uma ikhadi elifanayo libuyekezwe ezindaweni ezimbili, zombili izimpendulo zihlala emlandweni wokubuyekezwa kwalo, bese kusetshenziswa isimo esivela empendulweni yakamuva.

Le ndlela inciphisa ukungqubuzana kokuvumelanisa ongakugwema:

1. Vumelanisa idivayisi ngaphambi kokushiya indawo enoxhumano oluthembekile.
2. Buyekeza amakhadi, engeza amanothi noma ulungise umbhalo ovamile wamakhadi ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi.
3. Phinde uxhumeke futhi uvumelanise leyo divayisi ngaphambi kokuqhubeka kwenye.
4. Vumela enye idivayisi iqede ukuvumelanisa kwayo ngaphambi kokwenza ezinye izinguquko kuyo.

Izinguquko esakhiweni seqoqo zidinga ukuqapha okwengeziwe. Ukwengeza inkambu, ukususa isifanekiso sekhadi, ukushintsha izinhlobo zamanothi nemisebenzi efana naleyo kungadinga ukuvumelanisa ohlangothini olulodwa esikhundleni sokuhlanganisa. Ukuvumelanisa ohlangothini olulodwa kukucela ukuba ukhethe ukugcina iqoqo elikudivayisi noma eliku-AnkiWeb; iqoqo elikhethile lingathatha indawo yelinye, kususwe izinguquko ezikulelo elinye.

Ngakho qhubeka nokubuyekeza okuvamile nokuhlela amanothi ohambweni, kodwa kuhle ukuhlehlisa izinguquko eziyinkimbinkimbi zezinhlobo zamanothi nezifanekiso uma amaqoqo akumadivayisi amaningana eshintshwa ngokwehlukana ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Uma i-Anki ikucela ukuba ulayishe noma ulande, yima uthole ukuthi yiliphi iqoqo eliqukethe umsebenzi odinga ukuwugcina ngaphambi kokukhetha ukuthi ukudlulisa kuya ngakuphi.

## Imidiya iba kudivayisi kuphela ngemva kokufika kuyo

I-Anki igcina umsindo nezithombe ngokwehlukana nedatha yeqoqo. Kukhompyutha, [imibhalo yemiyalelo yemidiya](https://docs.ankiweb.net/media.html) ichaza ukuthi amafayela axhunywe enothini noma afakwe kulo ngokukopisha nokunamathisela akopishelwa kufolda ethi `collection.media` egcinwe kukhompyutha. Uma ifayela lemidiya selikuleyo folda, ikhadi alidingi i-inthanethi ukuze lilivule.

Ukungalungiseleli kahle kungadala izinkinga. Ukuvumelanisa iqoqo nokuvumelanisa imidiya kuyimisebenzi ehlukene, ngakho umsindo nezithombe kungase kusadluliselwa ngisho nangemva kokuba amakhadi esevele ekhona. [Umhlahlandlela wokuvumelanisa we-AnkiMobile](https://docs.ankimobile.net/syncing.html) uxwayisa ngokuthi imidiya ingase ingabi khona kuze kuphele ukuvumelanisa kokuqala ngokuphelele. Uhlu oluphelele lwamasethi amakhadi alufakazeli ukuthi iqoqo eligcwele izithombe noma umsindo selilungele ukusetshenziswa.

Ngaphambi kokunqamula uxhumano lwe-inthanethi:

- vumelanisa idivayisi owengeze kuyo imidiya;
- linda ukuvumelanisa imidiya kuyo kuqede;
- vumelanisa idivayisi ozohamba nayo, ulinde nakuyo;
- vula amakhadi asebenzisa uhlobo ngalunye lwezithombe nomsindo oludingayo;
- sebenzisa **Check Media** lapho itholakala khona ukuze uthole amanothi abhekisela kumafayela angekho.

Lokhu kuhlola kokugcina kubalulekile kumasethi amakhadi abelwe umphakathi. Ngezinye izikhathi umbhali wesethi akazange afake isithombe okubhekiselwe kuso, ngakho ukuvumelanisa ngokuphindaphindiwe ngeke kusilande.

Ukuba nemidiya kudivayisi akusho ukuthi wonke amakhadi anakho konke akudingayo. Isifanekiso sekhadi singabhekisela esithombeni, kuskripthi, kufonti noma kwesinye isisetshenziswa esikuwebhu. Izichazamazwi eziku-inthanethi, ukulanda amasethi amakhadi abelwe umphakathi nezingezo ezisebenzisa ama-API akude kusadinga uxhumano. Ukuguqula umbhalo ube inkulumo kuya ngezwi nangesistimu oyisebenzisayo: izwi lesistimu elifakiwe lingase lisebenze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, kodwa izwi elihlinzekwa yisevisi eku-inthanethi ngeke. Hlola isici osidingayo ngqo, esikhundleni sokucabanga ukuthi zonke izici ze-TTS noma zonke izingezo zisebenza ngendlela efanayo.

## Ukuvumelanisa i-Anki kusebenza kanjani uma uphinde uxhumeka

Ukuvumelanisa i-Anki ngemva kokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kunezigaba ezimbili: ukwenza umsebenzi kudivayisi manje, bese uvumelanisa ngenethiwekhi kamuva.

Uma uxhumano selubuyile, vumelanisa idivayisi enomsebenzi owenziwe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Linda ukuvumelanisa iqoqo nokuvumelanisa imidiya kuqede. Bese uvumelanisa idivayisi elandelayo ngaphambi kokubuyekeza amakhadi noma ukuhlela kuyo. Lokhu kulandelana kwenza kube lula ukubona isimo sakamuva uma i-Anki ikucela ukuba uxazulule ukungqubuzana.

Hlola umphumela; ukuphela kokunyakaza kwenkomba yokuvumelanisa esikrinini akwanele ukuqinisekisa ukuthi konke kudlulisiwe:

- thola inothi olengeze ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi;
- qinisekisa ukuthi inkambu oyihlelile inombhalo omusha;
- bheka umlando wokubuyekezwa noma isimo sokuthi ikhadi oliphendulile kufanele libuyekezwe nini;
- vula okungenani isithombe esisodwa noma ifayela lomsindo elisanda kwengezwa kudivayisi yesibili.

Uma uhlele inothi elifanayo kumadivayisi amabili, funda umbhalo ogcine usenothini esikhundleni sokucabanga ukuthi ukuhlanganisa kugcine amagama owafunayo. Uma kuvela inkinobho ebomvu yokuvumelanisa noma inketho yokulayisha noma ukulanda konke, ungavele uchofoze ngoba ukujwayele. Ukulanda konke kususa izinguquko zeqoqo elikudivayisi kufakwe elilandiwe; ukulayisha konke kususa iqoqo eliku-AnkiWeb kufakwe elilayishiwe ngaphambi kokuba amanye amadivayisi alilande.

## Uma i-inthanethi ingatholakali njalo, dlulisa iqoqo njengefayela

I-Anki isengadlulisa iqoqo phakathi kwamadivayisi ngaphandle kokufinyelela njalo ku-AnkiWeb, kodwa lokhu kungukudlulisela umsebenzi kwenye idivayisi, akukhona ukuhlanganisa umsebenzi owenziwe kumadivayisi amaningi.

[Umhlahlandlela wokudlulisa iqoqo we-AnkiMobile](https://docs.ankimobile.net/collection-transfer.html) usebenzisa ifayela elithi `collection.colpkg` eliqukethe wonke amasethi amakhadi nolwazi lokuhlela ukubuyekezwa. Uthekelisa iqoqo lamanje, udlulise ifayela nge-AirDrop noma ngokwabelana ngamafayela, bese ulingenisa kwenye idivayisi. [Incwadi yemiyalelo ye-AnkiDroid](https://docs.ankidroid.org/manual.html) ichaza indlela efanayo nge-USB yokudlulisa iqoqo phakathi kwe-Android nekhompyutha.

Ukungenisa ifayela leqoqo eliphelele kususa iqoqo eselikhona kudivayisi okuyiswa kuyo kufakwe elingenisiwe. Akukwazi ukuhlanganisa amaqoqo amabili ashintshwe ngokwehlukana ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Khetha idivayisi eyodwa eneqoqo lamanje ozosebenzisa lona njengomthombo: thekelisa kuyo, ngenisa kudivayisi elandelayo, wenze izinguquko zakho lapho, bese udlulisela iqoqo elibuyekeziwe emuva ngaphambi kokuqhubeka kudivayisi yokuqala.

Lokhu kuyasiza emsebenzini wasensimini, emikhunjini, ezindaweni ezikude noma kumanethiwekhi anemikhawulo lapho ukudlulisa amafayela ngezikhathi ezithile kungenzeka khona kodwa ukuvumelanisa efwini njalo kungenzeki. Ohambweni oluvamile ngendiza noma lokuya emsebenzini, kulula ukuqedela ukuvumelanisa nge-AnkiWeb ngaphambi kokuhamba.

## Ukuvumelanisa akuyona ikhophi eyisipele ye-Anki

Ukuvumelanisa kugcina amadivayisi esesimweni esifanayo. Ngakho ukususa okuthile ngephutha noma ushintsho ongafuni ukulugcina kungadlulela kuwo wonke amadivayisi avumelanisiwe.

Izinhlelo ze-Anki ezifakiwe zigcina amakhophi ayisipele kudivayisi, kodwa imidiya idinga ukunakwa ngokwehlukana. Ngokwesibonelo, [umhlahlandlela wezilungiselelo ze-AnkiMobile](https://docs.ankimobile.net/preferences.html) uthi amakhophi ayo ayisipele enziwa ngokuzenzakalela ahlanganisa amakhadi nezibalo, kodwa awahlanganisi umsindo noma izithombe. Ukuthekelisa iqoqo eliphelele kuhlanganise nemidiya kufeza umsebenzi ohlukile kokubili ekuvumelaniseni nasemlandweni wamakhophi ayisipele enziwa ngokuzenzakalela.

Uma kungaba nzima ukwakha kabusha isethi yamakhadi, thekelisa iqoqo eliphelele kuhlanganise nemidiya ngezikhathi ezithile, bese uligcina kwenye indawo ngaphandle kwedivayisi oyisebenzisa nsuku zonke. [Umhlahlandlela obanzi wamakhophi ayisipele amakhadi okufunda](/blog/how-to-back-up-flashcards/) uchaza ukuthi ungayihlanganisa kanjani leyo khophi yokubuyisela nombhalo ongadluliselwa kwezinye izinhlelo namafayela omthombo okuqala.

## Ukuzilolonga kwemizuzu eyishumi ngemodi yendiza

Kwenze lokhu kuyo kanye ilaptop, ifoni noma ithebhulethi ozohamba nayo. Ukuphumelela kokuhlola kukhompyutha akusho lutho ngesimo sefolda yemidiya yefoni yakho.

1. Usaxhumeke ku-inthanethi, vula uhlelo lwe-Anki olufakiwe bese uvumelanisa. Uma kuyidivayisi entsha, qedela ukulanda kokuqala kweqoqo.
2. Linda ukuvumelanisa imidiya kuqede. Ungayeki lapho kuvela amagama wamasethi amakhadi kuphela.
3. Vula wonke amasethi amakhadi owadingayo. Zama amakhadi anezithombe, umsindo, amafonti enziwe ngokukhethekile nanoma yimiphi imisebenzi ekhethekile yezifanekiso othembele kuyo.
4. Vula imodi yendiza noma uvale konke ukuxhumana kwenethiwekhi ngenye indlela.
5. Vala i-Anki ngokuphelele, uyivule futhi, bese uqala isethi yamakhadi oyidingayo. Lokhu kuveza indlela yokusebenza ebisebenza kuphela ngoba isikrini besivele sivuliwe.
6. Buyekeza amakhadi ambalwa. Engeza inothi elilodwa lokuhlola elibhalwe ngokucacile ukuthi elokuhlola, bese wenza ushintsho olulodwa lombhalo olungeke ludale inkinga.
7. Vala uhlelo uphinde ulivule usangaxhumekile ku-inthanethi. Qinisekisa ukuthi ukubuyekezwa, inothi elisha, ushintsho nemidiya ekudivayisi kusakhona.
8. Zama noma yisiphi isichazamazwi, izwi lokuguqula umbhalo ube inkulumo noma isengezo ohlela ukusisebenzisa. Bhala ukuthi yiziphi izingxenye ezidinga inethiwekhi.
9. Phinde uxhumeke bese uvumelanisa le divayisi. Linda izigaba zeqoqo nemidiya ziqede.
10. Vumelanisa idivayisi yesibili, bese uqinisekisa ukuthi inothi lokuhlola, ushintsho, isimo sokubuyekezwa nemidiya kukhona kuyo ngaphambi kokususa okuqukethwe kokuhlola.

Ungasebenzisi lokhu kuzilolonga ukwakha kabusha izinhlobo zamanothi kumadivayisi amabili. Inhloso ukuqinisekisa ukuthi indlela ozosebenza ngayo ohambweni iyasebenza: iqoqo elifanele likudivayisi, imidiya ebalulekile iyavuleka, umsebenzi owenziwe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi uhlala ukhona ngemva kokuvala nokuvula uhlelo, bese ukuvumelanisa kamuva kuwudlulisela kwenye idivayisi.
