## I-Claude isiyilungele le ndlela yokusebenza

Lolu sesho selubaluleke kakhulu ngoba i-Claude isikusekela kangcono ukufunda kunakuqala.

I-Anthropic yethula **i-Claude for Education** ngo-**2 Ephreli 2025**, kuhlanganise ne-**Learning Mode** ngaphakathi kwama-Projects. Imibhalo yayo yamanje yosizo ibaluleke ngokufanayo. Ikhasi losizo le-Anthropic lama-Projects, langomhla ka-**16 Mashi 2026**, lithi ama-Projects ayatholakala kubo bonke abasebenzisi, futhi abasebenzisi bama-akhawunti amahhala bangakha ama-Projects afinyelela kwamahlanu. Ikhasi losizo lokulayisha amafayela, langomhla ka-**22 Ephreli 2026**, lithi i-Claude yamukela izinhlobo zamafayela ezifanele ukufunda, kuhlanganise **ne-PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, HTML, ODT, RTF, EPUB, JSON ne-XLSX**.

Lokho kwenza ukuqala **ngamakhadi okufunda e-Claude** kuhleleke kakhulu kunendlela yakudala yokuthi “faka konke engxoxweni eyodwa bese wethemba ukuthi kuzosebenza”.

Umbiko we-Anthropic ngezemfundo ukhombisa nokuthi le ndlela isisetshenziswa kakhulu. Umbiko uthi abafundi basebenzise i-Claude ikakhulukazi ukwakha nokuthuthukisa okuqukethwe kwezemfundo ezingxoxweni ezihlobene nemfundo ezingu-**39.3%** zalezo ezahlaziywa. Ngakho umbuzo othakazelisayo awusewona ukuthi abantu bayayisebenzisa yini i-Claude ezintweni zokufunda. Kusobala ukuthi bayayisebenzisa. Umbuzo ongcono ngowokuthi lezo zinto ungaziguqula kanjani zibe amakhadi afanele ukubuyekezwa kamuva.

## Qala nge-Claude Project eyodwa ngesifundo, isivivinyo noma isihloko

Lona umkhuba wokuqala engingawugcina ngoba uthuthukisa cishe konke okulandelayo.

Imibhalo yamanje ye-Claude yama-Projects iwachaza njengezindawo zokusebenza ezizimele, ezinomlando wazo wezingxoxo nemithombo yazo yolwazi. Yilokho kanye okudingayo lapho ulungisa amakhadi okufunda.

Ungakhi indawo eyodwa enkulu yokusebenza ku-Claude oyibiza ngokuthi `school`.

Yakha i-Project eyodwa yalokhu:

- isifundo esisodwa
- isivivinyo esisodwa sesitifiketi
- iyunithi eyodwa ozama ukuyiqeda kuleli sonto
- iqoqo lezihloko ezihlobene ngempela

Lokho kugcina amakhadi okuqala ecacile ngoba i-Claude ayizami ukuhlanganisa izinto ezingahlobene ezivela ezifundweni ezine empendulweni eyodwa. Kwenza nokuwalungisa kungabi isicefe kangako ngoba uyazi ukuthi iqoqo ngalinye bekufanele lihlanganise ini.

Uma usebenzisa i-Claude njengomfundisi nangezinye izindlela ngaphandle kokwenza amakhadi kuphela, isihloko esithi [Indlela yokusebenzisa i-Claude ukufunda ngo-2026](/zu/blog/how-to-use-claude-for-studying/) sizokusiza kakhulu. Lesi sihloko sigxile kokuncane: indlela yokusuka kokukhiqizwa yi-Claude uye emakhadini osazowabona ewusizo lapho uwabuyekeza.

## Setha imiyalelo yephrojekthi kanye ukuze iqoqo ngalinye liqale kahle

Lena enye yezinzuzo eziwusizo kakhulu eziqondene ne-Claude.

Imihlahlandlela yamanje ye-Anthropic yama-Projects ithi ungabeka **imiyalelo yephrojekthi** ukuze i-Claude ilandele izidingo ezifanayo kuzo zonke izingxoxo ezikuleyo Project. Emakhadini okufunda, lokho kukusindisa ekuphindeni imithetho efanayo yokuhlela nokususa njalo lapho ulayisha ingxenye entsha.

Ngingagcina umyalelo wephrojekthi umfushane futhi uqondile:

```text
Uma ngicela amakhadi okufunda, sebenzisa ifomethi elula yengaphambili nengemuva. Ikhadi ngalinye malibe nomqondo owodwa, iqiniso elilodwa, umehluko owodwa noma isinyathelo esisodwa senqubo. Ingaphambili malicace futhi likwazi ukuphendulwa. Ingemuva malibe lifushane. Yeqa okuphindaphindiwe, imibuzo engacacile nanoma yini edinga umongo womthombo wokuqala ongafakiwe ekhadini.
```

Bese ugcina imiyalelo eqondene nomsebenzi engxoxweni uqobo lapho ulayisha iqoqo elisha.

Lokho kwehlukaniswa kusebenza kahle ngoba umyalelo wephrojekthi ubeka imithetho ejwayelekile, kanti umyalo osengxoxweni ubhekana nesifundo, isahluko noma iqoqo lemibuzo elikhona ngaleso sikhathi.

## Layisha amaqoqo amancane kunalawo ocabanga ukuthi uyawadinga

Yilapho izindlela eziningi **zokuguqula okukhiqizwe yi-Claude kube amakhadi okufunda** ziqala ukuhluleka khona.

Abantu babona ukuthi bangalayisha amafayela bese becabanga ukuthi bangafaka wonke amaphepha esifundo esicelweni esisodwa. Ngokuvamile lokho kukhiqiza amakhadi amabi kakhulu.

Imibhalo yokulayisha ye-Anthropic uqobo ikhuthaza indlela elula lapho imibhalo imikhulu: yihlukanise ibe izingxenye ezincane. Nami ngingenza lokho kanye.

Imithombo engcono ifana nalena:

- amanothi esifundo esisodwa
- ingxenye eyodwa yombhalo ozowufunda
- iqoqo elilodwa lemibuzo yokuzilolonga elinezilungiso
- ingxenye encane yesahluko
- isiqeshana esisodwa esihleleke kahle somhlahlandlela wokufunda

Imithombo emibi kakhulu ifana nalena:

- incwadi yokufunda yonke
- izifundo eziyishumi nambili ngesikhathi esisodwa
- ifolda yesemester ongakayihleli kahle
- inqwaba yemibhalo ethathwe kokushiwo ngomlomo ongafuni ngempela ukuyibuyekeza ikhadi ngekhadi

Amaqoqo amancane akuphoqa ukuba ubuze ngokwethembeka: yini ekule ngxenye okufanele ngikwazi ukuyikhumbula kamuva ngaphandle kokuphinde ngivule umthombo?

Lowo mbuzo ugcina iqoqo lamakhadi lincane futhi lingcono.

## Sebenzisa umyalo olula, imikhawulo yenze umsebenzi

Awudingi umyalo omude onehaba.

Ngingasebenzisa into efana nalena engxoxweni uqobo:

```text
Guqula lo mthombo ube amakhadi okufunda alula anengaphambili nengemuva.

Imithetho:
- Iqiniso elilodwa, umehluko owodwa noma isinyathelo esisodwa senqubo ekhadini ngalinye
- Ingaphambili malicace futhi likwazi ukuphendulwa
- Ingemuva malibe lifushane futhi liqonde ngqo
- Ungaqambi amaqiniso
- Yeqa okuphindaphindiwe nemibuzo engacacile
- Yeqa amakhadi adinga umongo wombhalo wokuqala ongafakiwe ekhadini
- Susa ngaphandle kokunqikaza amakhadi angafanele ukubuyekezwa isikhathi eside
```

Uma umthombo uyiqoqo lemibuzo elinezilungiso, engeza umusho owodwa:

```text
Beka phambili amaphutha engingase ngiwaphinde, izinto ezingase ziphinde zingidide, nomehluko engingase ngiphinde ngingawuboni.
```

Kwanele lokho.

Imikhawulo ewusizo ilula:

- into eyodwa okufanele ikhunjulwe ekhadini ngalinye
- izimpendulo ezimfushane
- awekho amaqiniso aqanjiwe
- akukho mibuzo ebanzi ecela isifinyezo engaphambili
- akukho khadi elidinga ukuthi umbhalo womthombo wonke uhlale uvuliwe eceleni kwalo

I-Claude ivamise ukuphendula kahle uma imingcele icacile. Akuvamile ukuba inkinga kube ukungabi nawo umyalo “omlingo”. Inkinga iwukuthi abantu bacela okuningi kakhulu ngesikhathi esisodwa.

## Susa amakhadi asazwakala sengathi abhalwe yi-Claude

Yilapho ikhwalithi ithuthuka kakhulu khona.

Amakhadi enziwe yi-Claude avame ukuba namaphutha ongawabona kalula:

- ingaphambili lizwakala libhalwe kahle kodwa awukwazi ukulinika impendulo ecacile
- ingemuva linamaqiniso amathathu ngoba isigaba besinamaqiniso amathathu
- ikhadi ligcina imisho engaqondile efana nokuthi “izinto ezisemqoka okufanele zicatshangelwe” noma “izici ezibalulekile”
- amagama acace ngokwanele ukuba uwathande, kodwa ayacasula lapho usubuyekeza

Lawo ngingawasusa ngokushesha.

Ingaphambili lekhadi lokufunda akufanele lizwakale njengomusho wokuqala womhlahlandlela wokufunda. Kufanele libe umbuzo ongawuphendula noma uhluleke ukuwuphendula.

Gcina ikhadi kuphela uma:

- ungakwazi ukuliphendula ngaphandle kokuphinde uvule umthombo
- ingemuva lifushane ngokwanele ukuba uzihlole ngokushesha
- lihlola umqondo owodwa, hhayi iqoqo lemibono
- usazoba nesifiso sokukhumbula elikuqukethe ngemva kwesonto

Uma kungenjalo, lisuse.

Lowo umehluko omkhulu phakathi kwesikhathi sokusebenza nge-Claude esizwakala sikhiqiza okuningi neqoqo lamakhadi elihlala liwusizo.

## Ikhadi elilodwa le-Claude elibuthakathaka, nenguqulo engingayigcina

Ake sithi i-Claude ifunda indinyana nge-glycolysis bese ikunikeza lokhu:

- Ingaphambili: Yiziphi izici nemiphumela esemqoka ye-glycolysis?
  Ingemuva: I-glycolysis iyindlela yokuguqulwa kwamakhemikhali emzimbeni eyenzeka ku-cytoplasm, eguqula i-glucose ibe yi-pyruvate, ekhiqiza i-ATP ne-NADH, futhi engadingi umoya-mpilo.

Lokho akulona ikhadi elilodwa. Kuyisigaba esizenza ikhadi.

Inguqulo engcono ivamise ukuba amakhadi ambalwa amancane:

- Ingaphambili: Yenzeka kuphi i-glycolysis?
  Ingemuva: Ku-cytoplasm.
- Ingaphambili: Iyiphi i-molecule ehlakazwa yi-glycolysis?
  Ingemuva: I-glucose.
- Ingaphambili: Ingabe i-glycolysis idinga umoya-mpilo?
  Ingemuva: Cha.

Mhlawumbe ungagcina ikhadi elilodwa elengeziwe lemikhiqizo ephumayo uma lowo mehluko ubalulekile esifundweni sakho.

Inhloso akukhona ukulondoloza impendulo ye-Claude ebhalwe kamnandi. Inhloso ukugcina izinto okufanele uzikhumbule ezifihlwe kuyo.

## Indlela yokusebenza engingayiphinda

Indlela enhle **yokusebenzisa i-Claude ukwenza amakhadi okufunda** imfushane ngokwanele ukuba uqhubeke uyisebenzisa ngisho sekuphele injabulo yokuqala.

Nansi inguqulo engingayigcina:

1. Yakha i-Claude Project eyodwa yesifundo, isivivinyo noma isihloko esisodwa.
2. Engeza umyalelo omfushane wephrojekthi onemithetho yamakhadi anengaphambili nengemuva.
3. Layisha iqoqo elilodwa elincane: isifundo, ingxenye yombhalo ozowufunda noma imibuzo yokuzilolonga elungisiwe.
4. Cela i-Claude ukuba yenze amakhadi okuqala alula anengaphambili nengemuva.
5. Susa noma ubhale kabusha amakhadi abuthakathaka ngokushesha.
6. Cela i-Claude ukuba igcine kuphela amakhadi owahlolile futhi wawavuma ngesixhumi se-MCP se-Nibomo. Buka kuqala indawo yokusebenza amakhadi azogcinwa kuyo nesicelo sokuwagcina, usivume, bese uhlola amakhadi agciniwe ku-Nibomo ngaphambi kokuwabuyekeza nge-FSRS. Ungawakopisha nangesandla.
7. Buyekeza iqoqo lokugcina nge-FSRS.

Leyo yindlela yokusebenza engakholakala kakhulu kunokwenza sengathi i-Claude uqobo iyindawo okufanele kugcinwe kuyo inkumbulo.

Uma iqoqo lakho elenziwe yi-AI selizwakala ligcwele ngokweqile, yeka ukwamukela izinhlaka ezintsha, ulungise ezikhona ngosizo lwesihloko esithi [Indlela yokulungisa amakhadi okufunda enziwe yi-AI ngo-2026 (ngesiNgisi)](/blog/how-to-fix-ai-flashcards/).
