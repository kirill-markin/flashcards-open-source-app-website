## I-Anki: inketho yamahhala esizinzile yekhompyutha ne-Android

I-Anki iyindawo ephephile yokuqala uma okubalulekile kuwe kuwukubuyekeza amakhadi isikhathi eside, kunemidlalo efana neye-Quizlet. [Isayithi elisemthethweni le-Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) linikeza izinguqulo zamahhala ze-Windows, macOS ne-Linux, ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala nge-AnkiWeb, futhi likukhomba ohlelweni lwamahhala lwe-AnkiDroid olwakhiwe umphakathi. Uhlelo olusemthethweni lwe-AnkiMobile lwe-iOS luyakhokhelwa, okuyisizathu esiyinhloko sokuthi uma kuthiwa “i-Anki imahhala” kudingeke kuchazwe ukuthi kusetshenziswa yiphi idivayisi.

Okuqukethwe okuyisisekelo kwe-Quizlet kulula ukukudlulisa. I-Anki ingakwazi [ukungenisa izinkambu zombhalo ongenakho ukufometha](https://docs.ankiweb.net/importing/text-files.html) ezihlukaniswe ngokhefana, ama-semicolon noma amathebhu. Khipha isethi evunyelwe ku-Quizlet, qondanisa amakholomu etemu nencazelo, bese uhlola ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa, i-HTML, okuphindiwe nombhalo ongekho ngezinhlamvu zesiLatini ngaphambi kokungenisa okusele. Izithombe nomlando wokufunda i-Quizlet engakukhiphanga ngeke kuphinde kuvele ku-Anki.

Indlela yokukhipha okuqukethwe ingelinye lamandla e-Anki. [Amathuluzi ayo okukhipha](https://docs.ankiweb.net/exporting.html) angakha umbhalo ongenakho ukufometha, izinqwaba ngazinye ze-`.apkg` noma iqoqo le-`.colpkg`. Izinketho zamaphakheji zingahlanganisa imidiya nemininingwane yokuhlela izikhathi zokubuyekeza. Lokho akuqinisekisi ukuthi olunye uhlelo luzoqonda zonke izifanekiso noma izinkambu zokuhlela izikhathi, kodwa ungagcina ikhophi eyisipele enemininingwane ngefomethi ye-Anki kanye nekhophi yombhalo elula.

I-Anki iyindlela engcono yamahhala esikhundleni se-Quizlet kulolu hlu kubasebenzisi bekhompyutha noma be-Android abakhathalela ukufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nesistimu yamakhadi esizinzile. Ayikufaneli kangako uma ufuna indlela yokusebenzisa ejwayelekile yasekilasini, izindlela eziningana eziqondisiwe noma uhlelo olusemthethweni lwe-iPhone ngo-$0. [Ukuqhathaniswa okubanzi kwezinye izindlela ze-Anki](/zu/blog/best-anki-alternatives/) kuyasiza uma ukugcina imininingwane iphelele ngesikhathi sokuthutha kubaluleke ngaphezu kokusuka ku-Quizlet uqobo.

Uma ufunda kukhompyutha bese usebenzisa ifoni lapho usohambweni, ukuvumelanisa okuzenzakalelayo akusona isengezo esincane—kushintsha izindleko zendlela yakho yokufunda.

Embhalweni ovunyelwe ukukhishwa, guqula amatemu nezincazelo kube yi-CSV noma i-Markdown, bese usebenzisa [amafomethi abhalwe yi-Mochi okungenisa](https://mochi.cards/docs/import-and-export/importing/). Kusazodingeka wakhe kabusha izithombe ngoba i-Quizlet ayizikhiphi.

Izinketho ze-Mochi zokukhipha okuqukethwe zicace kakhulu. Ifomethi yayo ye-`.mochi` ihlanganisa amakhadi, izifanekiso, okunamathiselwe, amathegi, imethadatha, umlando wokubuyekeza, ukuhleleka nesakhiwo sezinqwaba. I-Markdown ne-CSV kulula ukufunda kwezinye izinhlelo, kodwa [okukhishwa ngazo kushiya enye imethadatha](https://mochi.cards/docs/import-and-export/exporting/), kuhlanganise nomlando wokubuyekeza. Lokhu kuhlukanisa kahle: gcina ikhophi eyisipele ngefomethi yohlelo ukuze imininingwane iphelele, kanye nekhophi ye-Markdown noma ye-CSV ukuze kube lula ukuyifunda.

Khetha i-Mochi uma ukufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ne-akhawunti, kanye nokudluliseleka kwe-Markdown, kubaluleke ngaphezu kokuvumelanisa okuzenzakalelayo noma izabelo ezinkulu ze-AI. [Umhlahlandlela wezinye izindlela ze-Mochi](/blog/mochi-alternative/) uchaza kabanzi ngokukhetha lolu hlelo.

## I-Nibomo: ikhodi evulekile nokusebenza ngama-ejenti, kodwa isese-beta

I-Nibomo igxile ezidingweni ezithile kulolu hlu. Uhlelo olusingathiwe [lumahhala ngesikhathi se-beta](/zu/pricing/), luhlanganisa i-AI nokuvumelanisa phakathi kwewebhu, i-iOS ne-Android, futhi alunamkhawulo wamakhadi, amafayela noma isitoreji esiphelele osuselwa ohlelweni lokukhokha. Imikhawulo evamile yezicelo, amafayela nokudlulisa isasebenza. [Ikhasi lezici](/zu/features/) lichaza ukufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ukubuyekeza nge-FSRS nokufinyelela endaweni efanayo yokusebenza nge-MCP ne-Agent API.

Isithembiso sesikhathi eside sincane kunalokho okunikezwa yi-beta. Ukwakha nokubuyekeza amakhadi okuyisisekelo kuzohlala kumahhala. Ukusebenzisa i-AI kakhulu kungase kamuva kudinge ukhiye wakho womhlinzeki noma inketho ekhokhelwayo, futhi asikho isithembiso sokuthi zonke izici ezisingathiwe zizohlala zimahhala unomphela.

Ukuthutha usuka ku-Quizlet nakho kuyindawo ebuthaka kakhulu lapha. I-Nibomo ayinalo ithuluzi eliqondile lokungenisa okuvela ku-Quizlet, i-CSV, i-TSV, i-Anki noma umbhalo ojwayelekile. Umbhalo we-Quizlet ovunyelwe ukukhishwa ungaba umthombo wokwakha kabusha ngesandla noma wemibhalo yokuqala ye-AI oyihlolile, kodwa lokho akukhona ukungenisa okugcina yonke imininingwane. [Indlela yokukhipha okuvela ku-Quizlet](/zu/blog/how-to-export-quizlet-sets-and-turn-them-into-fsrs-flashcards/) ichaza ukulungisa okuqukethwe ngaphandle kokwenza sengathi imidiya nesimo sokufunda okungekho kungabuyiswa.

Iphakheji ye-`flashcards.zip` yohlelo idlulisa amakhadi, amathegi nemidiya ehlobene phakathi kwezindawo zokusebenza ze-Nibomo. Ayidlulisi umlando wokubuyekeza, isimo sohlelo lwe-FSRS lokuhlela izikhathi, izilungiselelo, izakhiwo eziphelele zezinqwaba noma idatha ye-akhawunti. Ukuzisingathela kukunika isofthiwe enelayisensi ye-MIT nokulawula ukufakwa kwayo, kodwa indlela ebhaliwe yokuyisebenzisa njengesevisi esetshenziswa ngempela isebenzisa i-AWS, futhi isenezindleko zengqalasizinda, abahlinzeki, ukunakekelwa, amakhophi ayisipele nomuntu oyiphathayo. [Umhlahlandlela wokuzisingathela](/docs/self-hosting/) uwuchaza ngokucacile lowo mngcele.

Khetha i-Nibomo uma ikhodi evulekile, ukusebenzisa i-FSRS ngokucacile noma ukufinyelela ngama-ejenti e-AI kuyisizathu sokushiya i-Quizlet. Khetha okunye uma okubaluleke kakhulu kuwukudlulisa ngokuqondile, amafomethi okungenisa asezinzile noma uhlelo olusingathiwe lwamahhala oluhlanganisa konke unomphela. [Ukuqhathaniswa okuhlukile kwe-Quizlet nohlelo olunekhodi evulekile](/blog/quizlet-alternative/) kungena kujule nge-Quizlet ne-Nibomo; lo mhlahlandlela wezinhlelo ezinhlanu ugxile emingceleni yalokho okutholakala mahhala.

## Yenza lokhu kuhlola kokuthutha ngaphambi kokudlulisa konke

Ukungenisa okuphumelele akufakazeli ukuthi uhlelo olusha luyakufanela. Sebenzisa isethi eyodwa emele ezinye zakho, bese uhlola yonke indlela yokungenisa nokukhipha ngesikhathi kuselula ukubuyela ku-Quizlet.

1. Khetha isethi enamakhadi angu-30–50 ongumdali wayo. Faka umbhalo ongekho ngezinhlamvu zesiLatini noma onezimpawu phezu kwezinhlamvu, izincazelo ezinde, ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa, amatemu abukeka ephindiwe kanye nekhadi elilodwa okungenani elisebenzisa isithombe uma uwasebenzisa.
2. Yikhiphe kuwebhusayithi ye-Quizlet. Bhala inani lamakhadi bese ugcina umbhalo wokuqala ungashintshiwe. Qaphela ukuthi yiziphi izithombe ezingekho ngaphambi kokusola uhlelo olusha.
3. Dlulisela isethi efanayo ohlelweni oluthandayo kakhulu. Sebenzisa indlela eqondile uma ikhona; uma ingekho, yenza ukuguqulwa kombhalo okuncane kakhulu okubhalwe emiyalweni. Ungalungisi wonke amasethi okwamanje.
4. Hlola amakhadi ayishumi anzima, hhayi ayishumi alula. Qinisekisa izinhlangothi zombili, ukuhlukaniswa kwemigqa, indlela izinhlamvu ezigcinwe ngayo (character encoding), okuphindiwe, ukuhleleka, amathegi noma amafolda nanoma yisiphi isithombe osakhe kabusha ngesandla.
5. Funda uxhumeke ku-inthanethi, bese uphinda iseshini emfushane ungaxhumekile. Xhuma futhi kudivayisi yesibili uma ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala kuyingxenye yohlelo lwakho, bese uqinisekisa ukuthi izinguquko nenqubekela phambili yokubuyekeza kufika kanye—hhayi kabili noma kungaphelele.
6. Khipha okuqukethwe ohlelweni olusha. Vula ifayela ngaphandle kwalolo hlelo bese uphendula imibuzo emithathu: Uyakwazi ukufunda umbhalo wamakhadi? Ingabe imidiya nokuhleleka kukhona? Ingabe umlando wokubuyekeza ufakiwe, ushiyiwe noma uvaleleke kufomethi yalolo hlelo kuphela?
7. Gcina isethi yokuqala ye-Quizlet nombhalo okhishiwe kuze kube yilapho indlela entsha yokufunda isisebenze okungenani ezikhathini ezimbalwa zokufunda kwangempela.

Lokhu kuhlola kuveza izimanga ezibizayo: isandiso se-Chrome esiyeka ukusebenza, ukuthenga uhlelo lwe-iOS obucabanga ukuthi akudingeki, izithombe ezingakaze ziphume ku-Quizlet, uhlelo lwamahhala olungenakho ukuvumelanisa noma ifayela elikhishiwe elilungele ukuphrinta kodwa elingalungele ukuthutha.

## Iyiphi indlela yamahhala esikhundleni se-Quizlet okufanele uyikhethe?

Kubasebenzisi abaningi bekhompyutha ne-Android, **i-Anki** iyinketho eqine kakhulu yesikhathi eside ngo-$0. Ihlanganisa ukufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ukuvumelanisa kwamahhala, amafomethi okungenisa nokukhipha asezinzile, futhi ayinaso isikhathi sokuphela kwe-beta. Okumele ukwamukele indlela yokusebenza edinga ulwazi lobuchwepheshe olwengeziwe nohlelo olusemthethweni lwe-iOS olukhokhelwayo.

Kubafundi abafuna uhlelo lwamahhala olusondele kakhulu ezindleleni ezahlukene zokufunda ze-Quizlet, **i-Knowt** iyona efanele ukuzanywa kuqala. Indlela yayo ebhaliwe yokungenisa okuvela ku-Quizlet ilula kunezindlela zokuguqula ezidingwa ezinye izinhlelo, nakuba ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nokukhipha okuqukethwe kunganiki isiqiniseko esifanayo.

Khetha **i-RemNote** uma ukuhlanganisa amanothi avumelanisiwe namakhadi kususa inkinga yangempela endleleni ofunda ngayo. Khetha **i-Mochi** uma ukufunda nge-Markdown kudivayisi kuphela, ngaphandle kwe-akhawunti, kwanele. Zama **i-Nibomo** uma okudingayo kuyikhodi evulekile nokusebenza ngama-ejenti, wamukele nemingcele ye-beta esingathiwe neyokuthutha.

Ungathuthi ngoba ikhasi elilodwa lamanani lithi “mahhala”. Thutha uma usuwahlolile amadivayisi owasebenzisayo, indlela ekugcina ufunda, isethi eyodwa yangempela ye-Quizlet nefayela obungalidinga uma usufuna ukuphuma.
