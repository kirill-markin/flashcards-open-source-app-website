Ngomhla ziyi-18 ku-Agasti 2026, imibhalo esemthethweni ye-Quizlet ayichazi i-API yomphakathi onjiniyela abangazibhalisela yona noma ingosi yomphakathi yonjiniyela. Okwamanje, unjiniyela ozimele akanayo indlela esemthethweni yokubhalisa uhlelo lokusebenza, athole ukhiye we-API ye-Quizlet, bese esebenzisa ama-endpoint achazwe emibhalweni ukuze afunde noma abhale idatha yamakhadi okufunda.

Lokhu kuchaza esikuthole emibhalweni ye-Quizlet etholakala emphakathini; akusho ukuthi siyakwazi okwenzeka ezinhlelweni zayo zangaphakathi. I-Quizlet iyaxhumana neminye imikhiqizo nezinhlelo zabalingani bayo. Uhlelo lwayo oluku-ChatGPT nesengezo se-Google Classroom yizibonelo ezimbili zamanje. Nokho, akukho kulokhu okuvula i-API ye-Quizlet yonjiniyela engasetshenziswa ezinye izinhlelo ngemisebenzi ehlukahlukene.

**Amaqiniso ahlolwe:** mhla ziyi-18 ku-Agasti 2026.

Ezinye izici ezisemthethweni zingabonakala zihlobene nokusebenza kwe-API. Nokho, ngasinye senzelwe umsebenzi othile kuphela:

Nakhu okwehlukanisa lezi zindlela: ukukopisha umbhalo wamakhadi akho kanye kuwumsebenzi wokukhipha idatha. Ukubonisa i-Quizlet kwelinye ikhasi kuwumsebenzi wokushumeka. Ukuxhumana nomkhiqizo othile kusebenza kuphela ngaphakathi kwendlela yokusebenzisa lowo mkhiqizo. Isofthiwe edala, ifunde futhi ihlele amakhadi ngokuphindaphindiwe idinga i-API yokufunda nokubhala enemibhalo echaza ukusetshenziswa kwayo.

## Ukukhipha idatha, ukushumeka nokufinyelela kwabalingani akuwona ama-API omphakathi

I-API yomphakathi inikeza onjiniyela bangaphandle incazelo ecacile yalokho abangathembela kukho: imibhalo yokuyisebenzisa, ukuqinisekisa ukufinyelela, imisebenzi esekelwayo, imithetho yokusetshenziswa nendlela yokuthola imininingwane yokufinyelela. Okwamanje, akukho kulokho i-Quizlet ekunikeza umphakathi okuhlanganisa zonke lezi zinto ukuze unjiniyela aziqalele ngokwakhe.

**Ukukhipha idatha** ku-Quizlet kuwukuyithutha ngesandla. Umdali wesethi angasebenzisa iwebhusayithi ukuhlela amagama nezincazelo zayo, akhethe **Kopisha umbhalo (Copy text)**, bese enamathisela umphumela kwenye indawo. I-Quizlet ithi izithombe azikwazi ukukhishwa, idatha yamasethi akopishiwe ayikwazi ukukhishwa, futhi lesi sici sitholakala kuwebhusayithi kuphela. Lokhu kuyasiza uma uthutha idatha kanye nje, ngokucophelela. Akunikezi isofthiwe indlela yokugcina idatha yezinhlelo ezimbili ivumelanisiwe.

**Ukushumeka** kuwukubonisa okuqukethwe, hhayi ukufinyelela idatha. I-Quizlet ikuvumela ukuthi ukopishe i-HTML yesethi yomphakathi kumodi yokufanisa (Match), yokufunda (Learn), yokuhlola (Test), yamakhadi okufunda (Flashcards) noma yokupela (Spell). Umsebenzi oshumekiwe ugcina uphawu lwe-Quizlet, futhi abafundi basebenzisa isixhumi esibonakalayo se-Quizlet. Uhlelo lwakho aluwatholi amakhadi aleyo sethi njengamarekhodi edatha olungawahlela.

**Ukuxhumana nomkhiqizo othile** kulandela indlela okuvunyelwene ngayo yokusebenzisa leyo mikhiqizo. I-Quizlet ingasebenza ne-ChatGPT noma ne-Google Classroom ngaphandle kokunikeza bonke onjiniyela indlela efanayo yokuxhumana nayo. Ukwethulwa kwalokhu kufakazela ukuthi ukuxhumana naleyo mikhiqizo kukhona; akufakazeli ukuthi kukhona i-API yomphakathi ye-Quizlet evulekele ukusetshenziswa okuvamile.

Ngenxa yalokho, isofthiwe endala eyenza kube lula ukuthumela izicelo ze-API (wrapper) ayisho ukuthi kukhona i-API ye-Quizlet esekelwayo. Nesicelo osibona kumathuluzi onjiniyela esipheqululini asisho lokho. Okushodayo yimibhalo yomphakathi nemithetho ezinzile onjiniyela abangathembela kuyo.

## Khetha indlela ehambisana nomsebenzi

### Ukuze ugcine ikhophi eyisipele noma uthuthe idatha kanye, yikhiphe ngewebhusayithi

Sebenzisa indlela esemthethweni ye-Quizlet yokukhipha idatha yesethi oyidalile. Njengoba le ndlela iphela ngokuthi **Kopisha umbhalo (Copy text)**, gcina ikhophi yokuqala oyinamathiselayo ingashintshiwe ngaphambi kokulungisa izihlukanisi noma ukunquma ukuthi ingxenye ngayinye yombhalo iya kuyiphi inkambu. Ugcina amagama nezincazelo; awulandi iphakheji yeqoqo lamakhadi ongayisebenzisa ukubuyisela konke. Izithombe nomlando wokufunda kusala ku-Quizlet.

Uhlu lwezinyathelo ezisebenzayo luku-[Indlela yokukhipha amasethi e-Quizlet ngo-2026](/zu/blog/how-to-export-quizlet-sets-and-turn-them-into-fsrs-flashcards/). Luchaza ikhophi yokuqala nekhophi osebenzela kuyo, i-UTF-8, ama-tab, izincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi, nomehluko phakathi kokuthutha okuqukethwe amakhadi nokuthutha idatha enquma ukuthi abuyekezwa nini.

Ukukhipha idatha kulungele ukuyithutha kube kanye. Akulungele ukudala amakhadi nsuku zonke, ukuvumelanisa idatha noma ukuyihlela ngokuphindaphindiwe ngesofthiwe.

### Uma ufuna ukubonisa isethi, sebenzisa indlela esemthethweni yokushumeka

Uma ufuna abafundi bafunde isethi yomphakathi ye-Quizlet besekusayithi lekilasi noma ekhasini le-LMS, sebenzisa ikhodi yokushumeka i-Quizlet ekunikeza yona kuwebhusayithi yayo. Khetha umsebenzi, ukhethe **Kopisha i-HTML (Copy HTML)**, bese ufaka umphumela ekhasini. Abafundi bathola umsebenzi we-Quizlet abangawusebenzisa; isayithi eliwuphethe alitholi idatha yamakhadi eluhlaza.

Ngokuvamile, yilokhu kuphela okudingwa uthisha. Ukukubiza nge-API kwenza isidingo sizwakale siyinkimbinkimbi kunalokho esiyikho.

### Uma usebenzisa i-ChatGPT noma i-Google Classroom, sebenzisa ukuxhumana okwenzelwe lowo mkhiqizo

Esimemezelweni sayo se-ChatGPT sangomhla ziyi-10 ku-Mashi 2026, i-Quizlet ichaza lezi zinyathelo: xhuma uhlelo lwe-Quizlet, qala umyalo nge-`@Quizlet`, ubuke isethi ekhiqiziwe kuqala ku-ChatGPT, bese uyivula ku-Quizlet ukuze uyivumelanise nezidingo zakho futhi ufunde. Lena yindlela esekelwayo yokudala isethi ye-Quizlet ngaleyo ngxoxo. Ayinikezi i-bot, iskripthi noma iwebhusayithi yakho imininingwane yokufinyelela i-API ye-Quizlet ongayisebenzisa futhi.

Nesimemezelo se-Quizlet se-Google Classroom sangomhla zingama-30 ku-Juni 2026 sichaza umsebenzi othile. Isengezo sivumela othisha ukuthi bathole futhi babele abafundi imisebenzi, okuhlanganisa imibuzo yokuzilolonga, amakhadi okufunda nemidlalo, bese belandelela ukubamba iqhaza nenqubekelaphambili ngaphakathi kwe-Classroom. I-Quizlet ithi lokhu kudinga i-Google Workspace for Education Plus; othisha bangase badinge ukuthi umphathi wabo we-IT abanike imvume noma abenze bakwazi ukuthola isengezo.

Uma enye yalezi zindlela isivele ihambisana nenhloso yakho, yisebenzise. Uma udinga uhlelo olwakhelwe izidingo zakho, akukho kulokhu kuxhumana okunikeza ukufinyelela okuvulekele bonke onjiniyela.

### Ukuze wenze imisebenzi ephindaphindwayo ngokuzenzakalela, khetha indlela yokufunda nokubhala echazwe emibhalweni

Ukwenza imisebenzi ngokuzenzakalela njalo kusho ukuthi isofthiwe yakho kufanele ikwazi ukwenza umsebenzi ofanayo ngokwethembeka izikhathi ezingaphezu kwesisodwa: ukudala amakhadi ngamanothi, ukuveza uhlu lwamaqoqo amakhadi, ukubuyekeza izimpendulo noma ukuphatha indawo yokusebenza ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Ukukopisha umbhalo ozowunamathisela kwenye indawo akukunikezi indlela yokwenza yonke le misebenzi ngokwethembeka.

Indlela ephephile iwukusebenzisa uhlelo lwamakhadi okufunda olushicilela ngokucacile ukuthi isofthiwe yangaphandle iqinisekisa kanjani ukufinyelela nokuthi yimiphi imisebenzi yokufunda nokubhala oluyisekelayo. Lokhu kungasho ukukhetha enye indlela ye-API ye-Quizlet ukuze wenze imisebenzi ngokuzenzakalela, bese uqhubeka nokusebenzisa i-Quizlet emisebenzini yokufunda esekelwa umkhiqizo wayo womphakathi.

## Ongakwenza nge-API ye-Nibomo njengenye indlela

I-Nibomo ishicilela izindlela ezimbili zokufinyelela isethi efanayo yedatha enomkhawulo, eqondene nomsebenzisi ngamunye:

- [I-Agent API yangaphandle](/zu/docs/api/) iqala ku-`GET https://api.nibomo.com/v1/`. Impendulo yayo yokuqala yokwethula isevisi iqondisa i-ejenti ekungeneni ngekhodi esetshenziswa kanye ethunyelwa nge-imeyili (OTP), ekudaleni ukhiye we-API nasekukhetheni indawo yokusebenza. Ukufunda idatha kusebenzisa indlela yemibuzo efana ne-SQL; ukubhala kusebenzisa indlela ehlukile yokwenza imiyalo.
- [Iseva ye-MCP ekude](/zu/docs/mcp-connector/) itholakala ku-`https://mcp.nibomo.com/mcp`. Izinhlelo ezixhuma nge-MCP zithola amathuluzi ayisishiyagalombili: `list_workspaces`, `sql_query`, `sql_execute`, `get_guide`, kanye namathuluzi okubuyekeza amakhadi athi `next_review_card`, `reveal_answer` no-`submit_review`.

I-`get_usage_limits` ibonisa kuphela iphakheji ye-akhawunti, imikhawulo nokusetshenziswa kwe-AI kwenyanga yamanje, ngaphandle kokushintsha lutho; ayifundi futhi ayishintshi amakhadi.

Zombili lezi zindlela zisebenza kuphela ngaphakathi kwendawo yokusebenza ekhethiwe. Izinsiza ezishicilelwe zithi `workspace`, `cards`, `decks` no-`review_events`, futhi imiphumela inomkhawulo wemigqa eyi-100 ngesitatimende ngasinye. Indlela efana ne-SQL isekela ingxenye ethile yolimi kuphela; ayinikezi ukufinyelela okuqondile ku-PostgreSQL. Ayikho i-schema ye-OpenAPI, ngakho izindlela zokusebenza ezincike ezinhlelweni zokuxhuma ezikhiqizwa nge-OpenAPI zizodinga enye indlela yokuxhumana.

Lokhu kungasiza unjiniyela noma i-ejenti ye-AI ukuba isebenze ngokuzenzakalela ngamakhadi okufunda engumnikazi wawo. Akukwazi ukufunda i-URL ye-Quizlet, ukugcina ikhophi evumelanisiwe ye-akhawunti ye-Quizlet noma ukusebenza njengohlelo oluxhuma ku-Quizlet ngendlela engachazwe emibhalweni esemthethweni. Alikho ithuluzi lokungenisa idatha ye-Quizlet ngokuzenzakalela. Uma uthutha idatha, qala ukhiphe amagama nezincazelo zesethi yakho, uhlole umbhalo, bese uwufaka ezinkambini ezifanele zamakhadi ohlelweni olusha. Uhlelo olwamukelayo luzakhela isimo salo sokufunda; umlando wokufunda ku-Quizlet awuthuthelwa kulo.

Ukuze ubone umehluko wemikhiqizo ongaphezu kokufinyelela nge-API, funda [ukuqhathaniswa nenye indlela ye-Quizlet enekhodi yomthombo evulekile](/blog/quizlet-alternative/).

## Izicelo zangaphakathi zesiphequluli aziyona indlela enqamulelayo ephephile

Isixhumi esibonakalayo sewebhu se-Quizlet sithumela izicelo zenethiwekhi, njengoba kwenza zonke izinhlelo zokusebenza zewebhu zesimanje. Ukuthola esinye salezo zicelo akusiguquli sibe i-endpoint esekelwayo yohlelo lwakho.

Ama-endpoint angaphakathi asetshenziswa isiphequluli angancika kumakhukhi eseshini, amafomethi angaphakathi, izilawuli zokuvimbela ukuhlukumeza isevisi, nasezimweni zokusebenza ezincike endleleni isixhumi esibonakalayo samanje esakhiwe ngayo. Angashintsha ngaphandle kokumenyezelwa kwezinguqulo emphakathini noma imiyalelo yokuthuthela enguqulweni entsha. Kanti [Imigomo Yesevisi ye-Quizlet](https://quizlet.com/tos), eyagcina ukubuyekezwa mhla zingama-28 ku-Meyi 2026, inqabela ukuvuna idatha emakhasini (scraping) nokunye ukukhipha idatha ngokuzenzakalela, kanye nokusebenzisa isevisi ngokuzenzakalela ngaphandle kwemvume.

Ukwakhela phezu kwalokho akuzinzile futhi kuyingozi ngisho nangeskripthi somuntu siqu; ingozi inkulu nakakhulu uma wakha umkhiqizo. Lapha ngeke nginikeze ama-endpoint aqageliwe noma izinyathelo zokuthola ukuthi i-Quizlet isebenza kanjani ngaphakathi ngokuyihlaziya (reverse engineering).

Uma udinga ukuthutha isethi yakho kanye, sebenzisa ukukhipha idatha. Shumeka isethi yomphakathi lapho abafundi beyidinga kwelinye ikhasi. Sebenzisa ukuxhumana ne-ChatGPT noma ne-Google Classroom kuleyo misebenzi ethile okwenzelwe yona. Uma ufuna ukufunda nokubhala ngokuphindaphindiwe, khetha isofthiwe echaza emibhalweni yayo indlela yokwenza imisebenzi ngokuzenzakalela—noma uqhubeke nokwenza ingxenye ye-Quizlet ngesandla kuze kube yilapho i-Quizlet isishicilela leyo ndlela.

## Ungabona kanjani uma isimo sishintsha

I-Quizlet ingase yethule uhlelo lonjiniyela ngemva kosuku okuhlolwe ngalo amaqiniso alesi sihloko. Okufanele ukubheke yingosi esemthethweni yonjiniyela noma imibhalo echaza ukuthi ubani ongabhalisa, ukuqinisekisa ukufinyelela kusebenza kanjani, yimiphi imisebenzi yamakhadi esekelwayo nokuthi yimiphi imithetho yokusetshenziswa esebenzayo.

Enye isofthiwe yomuntu wesithathu eyenza kube lula ukuthumela izicelo (wrapper) ngeke ishintshe impendulo. Nobunye ubambiswano obusha nomkhiqizo othile ngeke buyishintshe. Kuze kube yilapho i-Quizlet ishicilela indlela onjiniyela abangazibhalisela ngayo ukufinyelela, qaphela izimangalo mayelana ne-API ye-Quizlet yamanje futhi ukhethe indlela esekelwayo ehambisana nomsebenzi wangempela.
