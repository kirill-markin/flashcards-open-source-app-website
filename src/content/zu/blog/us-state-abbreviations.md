Isifinyezo sesifundazwe sihlukile ku-ZIP Code. Ekhelini, ikhodi enezinhlamvu ezimbili ikhomba isifundazwe; i-ZIP Code yingxenye enezinombolo elandela leyo khodi. Uma ifomu linezinkambu ezihlukene zesifundazwe ne-ZIP Code, faka ngakunye enkambini yako.

I-Washington, DC isebenzisa u-**DC**, kanti i-Puerto Rico isebenzisa u-**PR**. Zinezifinyezo zeposi, kodwa akukho nokukodwa kwazo okuyingxenye yethebula lezifundazwe ezingu-50. Isithasiselo se-USPS sihlanganisa nezindawo ezingaphansi kwe-US nezinye izindawo ezithunyelelwa iposi; sebenzisa [uhlu oluphelele lwe-USPS](https://pe.usps.com/text/pub28/28apb.htm) uma kuyilokho okudingayo.

## Qala ngamakhodi owadidanisayo

Uhlu oluhlelwe ngokulandelana kwezinhlamvu zamagama luwusizo ekutholeni impendulo, kodwa imigqa eseduze nayo ingakunikeza izinkomba. Qala ngokuqhathanisa amagama owadidanisayo, bese uwaxuba nezifundazwe ezingahlobene nawo ukuze uhlole ukuthi uyakwazi yini ukukhumbula impendulo ngayinye ngaphandle kosizo.

![Okhiye abacishe bafane behlelwa ezingxenyeni ezihlukene zethreyi elincane](/blog/us-state-abbreviations.png)

### Alaska, Alabama, Arkansas, Arizona

Gcina lezi ezine ndawonye lapho uqala ukuziqhathanisa:

- **Alaska → AK:** qaphela u-K egameni elithi Alaska.
- **Alabama → AL:** lapha izinhlamvu ezimbili zokuqala ziyasebenza.
- **Arkansas → AR:** nalapha, izinhlamvu ezimbili zokuqala.
- **Arizona → AZ:** qaphela u-Z egameni elithi Arizona.

Sebenzisa lokhu okubonayo njengezinkomba zokukhumbula. Uma usulibhekile leli qembu, livale bese uphendula umbuzo othi “U-AK usho siphi isifundazwe?” Bese uzama ukuthi “Ithini ikhodi ye-Arkansas?” Hlola nemibuzo ephambene: ukusuka ku-Alaska uye ku-AK, nokusuka ku-AR uye ku-Arkansas. Ungathola ukuthi olunye uhlangothi lulula kunolunye.

### Izifundazwe eziyisishiyagalombili eziqala ngo-M

I-Michigan, i-Minnesota, i-Mississippi ne-Missouri zonke ziqala ngo-“Mi.” Yi-Michigan kuphela esebenzisa u-MI. Funda igama nekhodi yalo ndawonye kunokuzama ukufinyeza wonke amagama ngomthetho owodwa.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Michigan | MI | Isebenzisa u-MI osekuqaleni |
| Minnesota | MN | Uhlamvu lwesibili lwekhodi ngu-N |
| Mississippi | MS | Uhlamvu lwesibili lwekhodi ngu-S |
| Missouri | MO | Uhlamvu lwesibili lwekhodi ngu-O |
| Massachusetts | MA | Isebenzisa u-MA osekuqaleni |
| Maryland | MD | Iqala ngo-M iphele ngo-D |
| Maine | ME | Iqala ngo-M iphele ngo-E |

Uma uphendula uthi “Missouri” lapho ubona u-MS, bhala phansi lokho kudideka ngokuqondile: **MS = Mississippi; MO = Missouri**. Ukubuyekeza la magama namakhodi awo kukunikeza umehluko othile ongazilolongela wona. Ukuphinda ufunde wonke amagama ayisishiyagalombili kungafihla ukuthi yiliphi elikubangele inkinga.

### Iowa, Idaho, Illinois, Indiana

Ikhodi ye-Idaho ngu-ID, eye-Illinois ngu-IL, kanti eye-Indiana ngu-IN. Eye-Iowa ngu-**IA**, esebenzisa uhlamvu lokuqala nolokugcina lwegama elithi Iowa. Zilolonge ngo-IA yodwa uma ulokhu uqagela u-IO, bese uyibuyisela eqenjini uyixube namanye amakhodi.

### Isifundazwe sase-Washington ne-Washington, DC

**U-WA usho isifundazwe sase-Washington. U-DC usho i-District of Columbia.** Bhala ukuthi “isifundazwe sase-Washington” ngaphambili kwamakhadi akho ukuze umbuzo ucace. Ikhadi elithi kuphela “Isifinyezo se-Washington?” lingaqondwa ngezindlela ezahlukene kungadingekile.

Izinhloko-dolobha zezifundazwe nazo zidinga imibuzo yazo, njengokuthi “Ithini inhloko-dolobha ye-Minnesota?” Gcina lawo makhadi ehlukene nemibuzo yezifinyezo ukuze impendulo ngayinye ikutshele ukuthi yiliphi iqiniso olikhumbulile. ## Imibuzo emifushane yezifinyezo zezifundazwe

Beka ithebula lezimpendulo lapho ungeke ulibone khona. Bhala zonke izimpendulo ezingu-12 ngaphambi kokuskrola uye ezimpendulweni. Azikho izimpendulo ongakhetha kuzo ezizokusiza: nikeza ikhodi enezinhlamvu ezimbili noma igama eliphelele lesifundazwe njengoba kuceliwe.

1. Ithini ikhodi ye-Alaska?
2. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-MO?
3. Ithini ikhodi ye-Iowa?
4. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-MD?
5. Ithini ikhodi ye-Arkansas?
6. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-MN?
7. Ithini ikhodi yesifundazwe sase-Washington?
8. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-ME?
9. Ithini ikhodi ye-Mississippi?
10. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-AZ?
11. Ithini ikhodi ye-Michigan?
12. Yisiphi isifundazwe esisebenzisa u-AL?

Ngaphambi kokuhlola, maka noma iyiphi impendulo oyiqagele noma okudingeke ucabangisise ngayo isikhashana. Gcina lezo zimpawu ngisho noma izimpendulo zigcina zilungile; azokusiza ukhethe lokho ozokuzilolongela ngokulandelayo.

### Izimpendulo

Zinike iphuzu elilodwa ngempendulo ngayinye enembile, kwangu-12 esewonke. Bese ubheka amaphutha: ingabe udidanise izifundazwe ezimbili, waqamba ikhodi efana no-IO, noma wazi igama nekhodi yalo ohlangothini olulodwa kuphela? Lokho kukutshela ukuthi yini okufanele uzilolongele yona. Ngisho noma uthole angu-12 kwangu-12, uhlolwe kule mibuzo embalwa kuphela; ukuhlola okuphelele kudinga zonke izifundazwe ezingu-50, kuhlanganise nezinhlangothi zombili uma zombili zidingeka emsebenzini wakho.

Buyela kulawo makhadi kwesinye isikhathi sokufunda ukuze uhlole ukuthi izimpendulo zisafika yini engqondweni.
