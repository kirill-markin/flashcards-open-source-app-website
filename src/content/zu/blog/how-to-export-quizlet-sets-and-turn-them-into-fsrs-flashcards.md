Uma inkinobho ethi **Export** ingaveli ku-Quizlet, qala ngokuhlola izinto ezimbili: ingabe nguwe owakha isethi yokuqala, futhi usebenzisa iwebhusayithi ye-Quizlet yini? I-Quizlet ivumela kuphela umuntu owakha isethi yokuqala ukuthi ayikhiphe, futhi lokhu kusebenza kuwebhusayithi kuphela. Isethi ekopishiwe ayikwazi ukukhishwa, noma ikhophi isivela emtapweni wakho.

Uma uvunyelwe ukuyikhipha, inqubo iyashesha: vula isethi kuwebhusayithi, khetha **More → Export**, hlela amagama nezincazelo, bese ukhetha **Copy text**. I-Quizlet ayilandi ifayela leqoqo lamakhadi. Ifaka umbhalo ebhodini lokukopisha (clipboard), ngaphandle kwezithombe.

**Amaqiniso ahlolwe:** ngo-30 Agasti 2026, ngokubheka [imiyalelo esemthethweni ye-Quizlet yokukhipha amasethi](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034345672-Exporting-your-sets).

Uma nguwe owakha isethi yokuqala kodwa inkinobho ye-Quizlet Export ingakaveli, qinisekisa ukuthi ungene nge-akhawunti engumnikazi wayo nokuthi uvule isethi yokuqala, hhayi ikhophi. Ikhasi losizo le-Quizlet alichazi enye indlela yokukhipha isethi. Kuleso simo, xhumana ne-Quizlet Support kunokwethemba ithuluzi lokulanda elithi lingadlula lo mkhawulo.

## Ungawakhipha kanjani ama-flashcard kuwebhusayithi ye-Quizlet

Ngesethi owazakhela yona:

1. Ngena ku-akhawunti yakho kuwebhusayithi ye-Quizlet.
2. Khetha **Your library** (umtapo wakho).
3. Khetha **Flashcard sets** (amasethi ama-flashcard).
4. Vula isethi ofuna ukuyikhipha.
5. Vula imenyu ethi **More**.
6. Khetha **Export**.
7. Khetha indlela yokuhlela amagama nezincazelo.
8. Khetha **Copy text** (kopisha umbhalo).
9. Namathisela umphumela kusihleli sombhalo ongenakho ukufometha (plain text).

Leyo yinqubo yamanje ephelele. Ayikho inkinobho yokugcina ethi “download”.

Emakhadini ajwayelekile anengaphambili nengemuva, ngincoma ukuthi ufake i-tab phakathi kwegama nencazelo, bese ufaka ikhadi ngalinye emugqeni omusha. Ukukhetha lezi zihlukanisi kuyiseluleko esisebenzayo, akuyona imfuneko ye-Quizlet. Ama-tab avame ukuba lula ukuwahlola kunokhefana ngoba okhefana bavela njalo ezincazelweni ezijwayelekile.

Ifayela elihleleke kahle elinamakhadi amabili lingabukeka kanje, line-tab eyodwa ehlukanisa igama nencazelo emugqeni ngamunye:

```text
photosynthesis	Process plants use to convert light energy into chemical energy
mitosis	Cell division that produces two genetically identical daughter cells
```

## Yini egcinwa lapho ukhipha isethi ye-Quizlet?

I-Quizlet ichaza lo msebenzi ngokuqondile: ikhipha **amagama nezincazelo** kusethi umuntu azakhele yona. Iphinde isho ukuthi izithombe azikwazi ukukhishwa.

Thembela kulokho okuvela embhalweni owunamathiselile. Uma umbhalo, isihlukanisi noma ukuhlukana kwemigqa kuvela lapho, ungakugcina kufayela. Ikhasi losizo le-Quizlet alithembisi ukudlulisa amafolda, izindlela zokufunda, ukufometha, izinga lokwazi okufundwayo, umlando wokubuyekeza amakhadi noma ishejuli yawo.

Ngakho lokhu kuyikhophi yombhalo wamakhadi, akusona isipele ongabuyisela ngaso isimo se-Quizlet. Lo mehluko uyasebenza nalapho ubusesha ukuthi “ukulanda ama-flashcard e-Quizlet”: i-Quizlet ikopishela umbhalo ebhodini lokukopisha, bese wena uzenzela ifayela.

## Gcina ikhophi yokuqala nge-UTF-8 ngaphambi kokulungisa noma yini

Yenza kube lula ukubuyela kokwangempela:

1. Namathisela umbhalo owukhiphile kusihleli sombhalo ongenakho ukufometha.
2. Wugcine nge-UTF-8, ngokwesibonelo uthi `biology-quizlet-raw.txt`.
3. Yenza enye ikhophi ethi `biology-quizlet-working.txt`.
4. Ungalithinti ifayela lokuqala; hlela ikhophi osebenza kuyo kuphela.

Ngaphambi kokungenisa ifayela osebenza kulo, lihlole isethi ye-Quizlet isavuliwe:

- Vula inketho yokubonisa izikhala ukuze uqinisekise ukuthi ama-tab nokuhlukana kwemigqa kuvela kuphi.
- Sesha isihlukanisi osikhethile ngaphakathi kwamagama nezincazelo. I-tab eyengeziwe ingakha inkambu yesithathu obungayilindele.
- Bheka izincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi; zingase zithathwe njengamakhadi engeziwe.
- Qhathanisa isiqalo, ingxenye ephakathi nesiphetho sefayela nalokho okuku-Quizlet, kuhlanganise nezincazelo ezinde kakhulu.
- Vala ifayela uphinde ulivule, bese uhlola izinhlamvu ezinezimpawu, imibhalo engasebenzisi izinhlamvu zesiLatini, izimpawu kanye nezimpawu zokuloba ezigobile.
- Maka izinkambu ezingenalutho, okuphindaphindiwe nemigqa engahleleki ngendlela elindelekile. Ungamane ukuqagele ukulungiswa kwazo ngaphandle kokuziveza lezi zinkinga.

Qhathanisa inani lemigqa nenani lamakhadi kuphela uma ikhadi ngalinye lisemugqeni owodwa ncamashi. Le ndlela emfushane ayisebenzi ngezincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi.

Gcina ifayela lokuqala noma ikhophi osebenza kuyo isibukeka ilungile. Uma ukungenisa kususa inkambu endaweni yayo noma kushiya okuthile ngaphandle, usazoba nomthombo ongashintshiwe ongawuqhathanisa nawo.

## Ukusuka ku-Quizlet kuya ku-CSV: lungisa ifayela ngendlela efanele

Ukushintsha isandiso segama lefayela sisuke ku-`.txt` siye ku-`.csv` akuguquli ifomethi yalo. I-CSV idinga izihlukanisi ezisetshenziswa ngendlela efanayo nezimpawu zokucaphuna ezifakwe kahle lapho kukhona okhefana, izimpawu zokucaphuna noma umbhalo onemigqa eminingi.

Ukuze ulungise ifayela le-CSV elivela ku-Quizlet ngokuphepha:

1. Ungasishintshi isipele sombhalo wokuqala.
2. Vula ikhophi osebenza kuyo ku-spreadsheet bese ukhetha sona kanye isihlukanisi osisebenzise ku-Quizlet.
3. Qinisekisa ukuthi irekhodi ngalinye elilindelekile linamakholomu amabili: igama nencazelo.
4. Hlola izimpawu zokucaphuna, izihlukanisi nezincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi.
5. Likhiphe njenge-CSV ene-UTF-8.
6. Phinda uvule i-CSV ukuze uyibuke kuqala kabusha ngaphambi kokuyisebenzisa kwenye indawo.

I-spreadsheet ingafaka izimpawu zokucaphuna ezidingwa yi-CSV. Ukushintsha wonke ama-tab abe okhefana akukwazi ukukwenza lokhu ngokuthembekile.

## Faka umbhalo ku-Nibomo njengohlaka ozoluhlola

[I-Nibomo](/zu/features/) ayinalo ithuluzi lokungenisa amakhadi ngokuqondile esuka ku-Quizlet. Ku-Nibomo eku-inthanethi, unamathisela ifayela le-TXT noma le-CSV bese i-AI isiza ukwakha uhlaka lwamakhadi. Le ndlela ayiqinisekisi ukuthi konke kudluliswa ngaphandle kokulahleka kolwazi.

1. Gcina ifayela lokuqala le-Quizlet ngaphandle kwe-app.
2. Namathisela i-TXT oyilungisile noma i-CSV elungiswe ngendlela efanele engxoxweni ye-Nibomo AI.
3. Tshela umsizi ukuthi ifayela lisebenzisa siphi isihlukanisi futhi linaziphi izinkambu.
4. Cela isibonelo esincane sokubuka kuqala, usho ngokucacile ukuthi angawagcini amakhadi okwamanje.
5. Qhathanisa inani lamakhadi ahlongozwayo, izingaphambili nezingemuva zawo nefayela osebenza kulo.
6. Gcina kuphela amakhadi osuwahlolile.

[Umhlahlandlela Wokuqalisa](/docs/getting-started/) uchaza ingxoxo ye-AI esebenzisa idatha yendawo yokusebenza namafayela anamathiselwe. Ungasebenzisa umyalo oqinisekisa ukuthi kuqala kuhlolwe amakhadi, njengokuthi:

> Funda leli fayela njengamapheya egama nencazelo ahlukaniswe ngama-tab. Ungawagcini amakhadi okwamanje. Yakha uhlaka lwesampula elincane, ugcine amagama njengoba enjalo emthonjeni kanye nezinhlamvu ezingezona ezesiLatini. Bhala ngokwehlukana imigqa engahleleki kahle, engenalutho noma engaqondakali, kunokuqagela ukuthi isho ukuthini.

Lokhu kungasiza uma amakhadi amadala nawo edinga ukulungiswa. Akufakazeli ukuthi wonke umugqa udluliswe kahle. Sebenzisa uhlu lokuhlola oluthi [Ungawalungisa kanjani ama-flashcard e-AI](/blog/how-to-fix-ai-flashcards/), bese uzama iqoqo elincane lamakhadi ngaphambi kokwakha asele.

Amakhadi akhiwe ku-Nibomo aqala ngomlando wokufunda omusha. Umbhalo we-Quizlet awunayo imininingwane yokubuyekeza amakhadi noma isimo seshejuli esidingekayo ukuze uqhubeke nohlelo lwakho lwangaphambili lokubuyekeza amakhadi.

> **Ukubeka obala:** Ngithuthukisa i-Nibomo. Le nqubo ichaza imikhawulo yomkhiqizo njengamanje; ayisho ukuthi uhambisana ngokuphelele ne-Quizlet.

## Ukusuka ku-Quizlet kuya ku-Anki: sebenzisa ithuluzi le-Anki lokungenisa umbhalo

I-Anki isekela amafayela ombhalo ongenakho ukufometha ane-UTF-8, lapho izinkambu zihlukaniswe ngokhefana, ama-semicolon noma ama-tab, njengoba kuchaziwe [emiyalelweni yayo esemthethweni yokungenisa umbhalo](https://docs.ankiweb.net/importing/text-files.html).

Ngekhophi osebenza kuyo ehlukaniswe ngama-tab:

1. Vula inqubo yokungenisa ye-Anki bese ukhetha ifayela lombhalo le-UTF-8.
2. Efasiteleni lokubuka kuqala, qinisekisa ukuthi i-Anki ibone i-tab; shintsha inketho yesihlukanisi uma ingayibonanga.
3. Khetha uhlobo lwenothi neqoqo lamakhadi ozofaka kulo.
4. Qondanisa inkambu yokuqala nengaphambili neyesibili nengemuva.
5. Hlola ukubuka kuqala ukuze uthole izinkambu ezingenalutho, ezisukile endaweni yazo noma ezengeziwe.
6. Ngaphambi kokungenisa, hlola isilungiselelo esilawula ukuphathwa kwamanothi aphindaphindiwe nokubuyekezwa kwalawo asevele ekhona.

I-Anki inquma inani lezinkambu elililindele ngomugqa wokuqala ongewona owamazwana. Kumarekhodi alandelayo, izinkambu ezingekho zisala zingenalutho kanti ezengeziwe azingeniswa. Yingakho ukubuka kuqala kubalulekile, ikakhulukazi lapho isihlukanisi esisodwa esingafanele singashintsha ukuhleleka komugqa.

Izincazelo ezinemigqa eminingi nazo zidinga indlela ekhethwe ngokucacile. I-Anki isekela izinkambu ezifakwe ezimpawini zokucaphuna ezithatha imigqa eminingi. Ingaphinde ithathe u-`<br>` njengokuqala komugqa omusha uma **Allow HTML in fields** (vumela i-HTML ezinkambini) ivuliwe. Sebenzisa indlela eyodwa bese uhlola isampula elikopishiwe ngaphambi kokungenisa ifayela lonke.

Ngokuzenzakalela, i-Anki ingafanisa inothi eselikhona lohlobo olufanayo ngenkambu yalo yokuqala, bese ibuyekeza ezinye izinkambu. Izinketho zokungenisa zingaziba okuphindaphindiwe noma zikufake njengamanothi amasha. Ukubuyekeza inothi le-Anki eselikhona kungagcina ishejuli yalo ye-Anki, kodwa ayikho ishejuli ye-Quizlet edluliswa ngefayela lombhalo.

## Gcina isethi yokuqala kuze kusebenze iqoqo elisha lamakhadi

Indlela esemthethweni ye-Quizlet iphelela ku-**Copy text** kuwebhusayithi, kumasethi umuntu azakhele wona. Izikripthi zokuqoqa idatha emakhasini (scraping), amakheli e-API angasese aqageliwe namathuluzi okulanda ezinkampani zangaphandle kuyizinqubo ezihlukile ezingasekelwe. Aziguquli isethi ekopishiwe noma oyibuka kuphela ibe isethi ekhishwe ngokusemthethweni.

Uma udinga ukwenza lokhu ngokuzenzakalela njalo kunokukopisha kanye, [isimo samanje se-Quizlet API](/blog/quizlet-api/) sichaza imingcele yalokho okusekelwayo. Uma isethi kungeyona eyakho, cela ifayela lomthombo kumuntu owayakha noma uzakhele iqoqo elincane lamakhadi ngamanothi akho. [Ungawenza kanjani ama-flashcard angcono](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/) kungakusiza uthuthukise lawo owakha kabusha kunokukopisha wonke umugqa omdala.

Ungayisusi isethi yokuqala ye-Quizlet kuze kube yilapho:

- ifayela lokuqala le-UTF-8 ligcinwe endaweni enesipele;
- ifayela osebenza kulo livuleka kabusha linezinhlamvu nezihlukanisi ezilindelekile;
- izinkambu ezinemigqa eminingi, ezingenalutho, okuphindaphindiwe nemigqa engahleleki ngendlela elindelekile sekuhloliwe;
- noma yiluphi ulwazi olubalulekile olwalusezithombeni selwakhiwe kabusha ngomthombo ovunyelwe ukuwusebenzisa; futhi
- ukuhlola okuncane kukhiqize izingaphambili nezingemuva ezifanele endaweni ofaka kuyo amakhadi.

Gcina ifayela lombhalo wokuqala nangemva kwalokho. Yilo mthombo ozimele okulula ukuwuqhathanisa nokufakiwe uma ukungenisa kwakamuva kubukeka kungalungile. Uma usanquma ukuthi uzowafaka kuphi amakhadi, [ukuqhathaniswa kwezinye izindlela esikhundleni se-Quizlet](/blog/quizlet-alternative/) kuchaza izinzuzo nobuthakathaka bazo kabanzi.
