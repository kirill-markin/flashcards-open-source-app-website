Abantu ababili bangaba nezinhlolokhono zokuthola ubuzwe ngosuku olulodwa ekuseni ngo-2026 kodwa benze ukuhlolwa kwe-civics okuhlukene, okuhlola ulwazi ngohulumeni, umlando namalungelo nezibopho zezakhamuzi. Uma omunye efake i-Form N-400 ngo-October 19, 2025, omunye wayifaka ngo-October 20, bafunda amaqoqo asemthethweni emibuzo ahlukene.

Ikhadi lingaba nempendulo efanele lapho ulenza, kodwa ibe ingasafanele ngosuku lwenhlolokhono. Cishe uMthethosisekelo awushintshanga ngobusuku obubodwa. Ukhetho, ukuqokwa komuntu esikhundleni, ukuguqulwa komthetho, noma ukuthuthela kwesinye isifunda sokumelwa ku-Congress kungenzeka kushintshe impendulo.

Ngakho bengingeke ngifundele ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kokuthola ubuzwe kuka-2025 njengenqwaba eyodwa yezimpendulo ezingu-128. Yakha isisekelo solwazi olungavami ukushintsha ngohulumeni nomlando. Gcina izimpendulo eziphathelene nezikhulu ezisezikhundleni manje, nesifundazwe sakho noma nesifunda sakho sokumelwa eqenjini elincane elinamathegi ukuze ukwazi ukuphinde uzihlole.

Usuku lokufaka isicelo lunquma ukuthi wenza luphi uhlobo lokuhlolwa. Usuku lwenhlolokhono lunquma ukuthi ezinye izimpendulo zisafanele yini ngaleso sikhathi.

## Qala ngosuku owafaka ngalo i-Form N-400

Akukho “ukuhlolwa kwe-civics ka-2026” okusemthethweni. Umuntu oya enhlolokhonweni ngo-2026 angase enze uhlobo luka-2008 noma **ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kokuthola ubuzwe kuka-2025**.

I-USCIS ikhetha uhlobo lokuhlolwa ngokosuku owafaka ngalo i-Form N-400:

| Usuku lokufaka i-Form N-400 | Uhlobo lokuhlolwa kwe-civics |
| --- | --- |
| Ngaphambi kuka-October 20, 2025 | Ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kokuthola ubuzwe kuka-2008 |
| Ngo-October 20, 2025 noma ngemva kwalolo suku | Ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kokuthola ubuzwe kuka-2025 |

I-USCIS ishicilela lo mnqamulajuqu [ekhasini layo elithi Check for Test Updates, elibhalwe ngesiNgisi](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/check-for-test-updates). Qinisekisa usuku owafaka ngalo isicelo ngaphambi kokulanda imibuzo, ukuthenga izinto zokufunda noma ukwenza amakhadi. Inhlolokhono yango-2026 ayimshintsheli ekuhlolweni kuka-2025 umuntu owafaka isicelo ngaphambi komnqamulajuqu.

Ohlotsheni olujwayelekile luka-2025, iqoqo elisemthethweni liqukethe **imibuzo engu-128**. Ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kwenziwa ngomlomo. Isikhulu se-USCIS sibuza imibuzo efika kwengu-20, futhi kufanele uphendule okungenani engu-12 ngendlela efanele. I-USCIS ichaza le mithetho ekuqaleni kwe-[PDF esemthethweni ethi 128 Civics Questions and Answers, ngesiNgisi](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf).

Ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kuyingxenye eyodwa yenhlolokhono yokuthola ubuzwe. Isikhulu sibuza nangesicelo sakho nangomlando wakho. Ngaphandle kwalapho kusebenza khona ukukhululwa okuthile, ukuhlolwa kokuthola ubuzwe kuhlanganisa ukukhuluma, ukufunda nokubhala isiNgisi. I-USCIS ichaza inqubo ephelele [ekubukezweni kwayo kwenhlolokhono yokuthola ubuzwe, ngesiNgisi](https://my.uscis.gov/citizenship/what_to_expect). Amakhadi okufunda angakusiza ukhumbule izimpendulo ze-civics; awakwazi ukunquma ukuthi uyafaneleka yini noma ukulungiselela udaba lwesicelo sakho.

## Hlukanisa isethi yamakhadi ngezimpendulo ezizinzile nezamanje

Iningi leqoqo elisemthethweni liphathelene nolwazi olungavami ukushintsha: izimiso zoMthethosisekelo, izinhlaka zikahulumeni, amalungelo nezibopho, ukuma kwezwe, umlando, izimpawu namaholide. Ingxenye encane incike ekutheni ubani osesikhundleni manje noma ukuthi uhlala kuphi.

Bengingasebenzisa isethi eyodwa ebizwa ngokuthi `USCIS 2025 civics` enamathegi amabili ayinhloko:

1. `stable` yezimpendulo ezingavami ukushintsha ngemva kokhetho noma kokuthutha
2. `current-answer` yezimpendulo eziphathelene nomuntu osesikhundleni, ukhetho, ukuqokwa, ukuguqulwa komthetho, isifundazwe noma isifunda sokumelwa

Engeza ithegi ecacisa kabanzi lapho isiza:

- `current:federal`
- `current:state`
- `current:district`
- `65-20`

Lokhu kuhlukanisa indlela yokunakekela amakhadi; akusho ukuthi ikhadi eline-`stable` alisoze lashintsha. I-USCIS ihlala ingumthombo othembekile wokuthi impendulo efanele iyiphi. Amathegi akusiza uthole amakhadi okungenzeka kakhulu ukuthi adinge ukuphinde ahlolwe.

Ngaphandle kwalokhu kuhlukanisa, igama lomuntu osesikhundleni, elingase lishintshe, lingabonakala lihlala njalo njengenani lezichibiyelo zoMthethosisekelo. Ithegi ethi `current-answer` yenza lowo mehluko ubonakale.

Ukuze uzijwayeze ngokwehlukana imibuzo yomlando ongavami ukushintsha, [isethi yamakhadi angu-92 omongameli base-U.S. ngokulandelana, amakhadi ayo abhalwe ngesiNgisi](/zu/catalog/packages/us-presidents-in-order-flashcards/) ikuqeqesha ukukhumbula igama likamongameli uma unikezwe inombolo yakhe ngokulandelana, nokukhumbula inombolo uma unikezwe igama. Ayihlanganisi iqoqo lemibuzo le-USCIS futhi ayibuyekezi izimpendulo eziphathelene nabantu abasezikhundleni njengamanje, ngakho yigcine ngaphandle kwesethi elungiselela inhlolokhono ngokuqondile.

## Yakhela emibhalweni emibili esemthethweni ye-USCIS

Qala ngale mibhalo emibili esemthethweni ebhalwe ngesiNgisi:

- [iqoqo lezimpendulo lemibuzo engu-128](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf), elinikeza imibuzo nezimpendulo ezishicilelwe yi-USCIS
- [i-USCIS 2025 Civics Test Study Guide](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/brochures/USCIS-2025-Civics-Test-Study-Guide.pdf), echaza ulwazi ngohulumeni nomlando olusekela lezo zimpendulo

Sebenzisa iqoqo lezimpendulo ukuze uhlole ngokuqondile lokho okufanele ukukhumbule. Sebenzisa umhlahlandlela wokufunda lapho impendulo izwakala njengomusho owubamba ngekhanda ngaphandle kokuwuqonda.

Ungaceli ithuluzi le-AI ukuthi liqambe isethi ephelele yamakhadi obuzwe lisusela olwazini olujwayelekile. Lingase lisebenzise iqoqo elidala lemibuzo engu-100, lifinyeze impendulo ngokweqile, linikeze igama lomuntu osesikhundleni ozoshintsha kamuva, noma lifake incazelo yomhlahlandlela wokufunda empendulweni. I-AI ingasiza ukuhlela okusalungiswa noma ukuthola amakhadi aphindaphindiwe. Qhathanisa ikhadi ngalinye nemibhalo yamanje ye-USCIS ngaphambi kokulifunda.

Embuzweni ngamunye osemthethweni owengezayo, gcina le mininingwane emithathu:

1. **Inani lezimpendulo ezicelwa umbuzo.** Umbuzo ocela izinto ezimbili akufanele uguqulwe ube yikhadi eliqeqesha eyodwa kuphela.
2. **Izimpendulo ezishicilelwa yi-USCIS.** Khetha impendulo emfushane esohlwini ongayisho ngokucacile kunokuba ubhale indlela yakho yokuhumusha umqondo.
3. **Ukuthi impendulo ingashintsha yini noma ihlukane ngokwendawo.** Faka ithegi bese ubhala usuku owahlola ngalo umthombo.

Ingemuva eliwusizo lekhadi elinempendulo engashintsha lingafaka lokhu:

```text
Answer: [current answer checked against USCIS]
Scope: federal / state / district
Last checked: 2026-07-24
Recheck: immediately before interview
```

Leli nothi lenziwe laba lula ngamabomu. Likutshela ukuthi impendulo ihlolwe nini nokuthi kufanele uyihlole nini futhi.

## Zijwayeze ngendlela ukuhlolwa okwenziwa ngayo: ngomlomo

Isikhulu sibuza imibuzo ye-civics ngokuzwakalayo. Isethi eqeqesha ukubona nokukhomba impendulo kuphela ishiya ingxenye yomsebenzi.

Gcina umbuzo osemthethweni ngaphambili kwekhadi. Wufunde kanye, ususe amehlo esikrinini, bese usho impendulo ngaphambi kokuveza ingemuva. Uma ucabanga ukuthi “Bengikwazi lokho” ngemva kokubona impendulo kuphela, bala lelo khadi njengeliphuthiwe.

Imikhuba emithathu emincane yenza isethi ibe wusizo kakhulu:

- Susa izinkomba zezinketho eziningi. Ukuhlolwa kwe-civics kwangempela kwenziwa ngomlomo.
- Zijwayeze ukusho amagama abantu namagama ongawajwayele ngokuzwakalayo, ungagcini ngokuwacabanga.
- Beka impendulo ehlolwayo kuqala engemuva; faka incazelo ngezansi kwayo.

Ngezikhathi ezithile, cela umuntu ofunda naye akufundele imibuzo engu-10 noma engu-20 ngokulandelana okuxutshiwe. Ukuzirekhoda ngocingo kungaveza nokuma isikhathi ngaphambi kokuphendula ongakunaki lapho ubukeza buthule. I-Nibomo ayihloli indlela okhuluma ngayo futhi ayilingisi inhlolokhono ye-USCIS, ngakho lokhu kuhlala kuwukuzijwayeza ukukhuluma okujwayelekile usebenzisa amakhadi akho.

I-USCIS ikhuthaza abafake izicelo ukuba basebenzise izimpendulo eziseqoqweni layo elishicilelwe. Uzijwayeza ukunikeza impendulo ecacile embuzweni obuzwa ngomlomo, hhayi ukwethula inkulumo ngakho konke okwaziyo ngesihloko.

## Nikeza izimpendulo ezishintshayo uhlelo lwazo lokubuyekezwa

I-USCIS ithi ezinye izimpendulo ze-civics zingashintsha ngemva kokhetho lukahulumeni wobumbano noma lwesifundazwe, ukuqokwa kwamajaji, ukuguqulwa kwemithetho noma ezinye izinguquko. Impendulo eyamukelekayo yileyo ehambisana nomuntu osesikhundleni noma nendawo ohlala kuyo **ngesikhathi senhlolokhono yokuthola ubuzwe**, hhayi eyayifanele ngesikhathi ufaka isicelo noma wenza ikhadi.

[Ikhasi le-USCIS lezinguquko zokuhlolwa, ngesiNgisi](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/find-study-materials-and-resources/check-for-test-updates) libala izimpendulo okungenzeka zishintshile kuzo zombili izinhlobo zokuhlolwa. Liphinde liqondise abafake izicelo emithonjeni esemthethweni yezimpendulo eziphathelene nezifundazwe nezifunda zokumelwa ku-Congress.

Lesi sihloko asiwabali ngamabomu amagama abantu abaphethe izikhundla zikaMongameli, iPhini likaMongameli, uSomlomo, iJaji Eliyinhloko, umbusi wesifundazwe, isenetha noma omele isifunda njengamanje. Labo bantu bangashintsha. Faka impendulo efanele ekhadini lakho, uyifake ithegi yokuthi idinga ukuqinisekiswa, bese usebenzisa imithombo enikezwa yi-USCIS.

Landela lolu hlelo lokuhlola:

1. Hlola amakhadi anezimpendulo ezingashintsha lapho uwakha okokuqala.
2. Waphinde uwahlole lapho i-USCIS ikubekela usuku lwenhlolokhono.
3. Phinda uhlole lonke iqembu le-`current-answer` ngaphambi nje kwenhlolokhono.
4. Buyekeza ingemuva nosuku lwe-`Last checked` kunokuba uthembele kulokho okukhumbula ezindabeni.

Uma uthutha, hlola iqembu lezimpendulo zesifundazwe nesifunda sokumelwa ngisho noma kungabanga nokhetho. Indawo yakho entsha ingase ishintshe impendulo efanele.

Ukuhlukanisa la makhadi kwenza ukuhlola kokugcina kube kufushane. Akudingeki useshe yonke isethi yomlando njalo lapho kushintsha umuntu osesikhundleni.

## Gcina amakhadi olwazi oluzinzile emafushane

Iqoqo elisemthethweni likukhombisa ukuthi kufanele ufunde ini. Umhlahlandlela wokufunda ukunikeza incazelo esekelayo. Awukho kule mibhalo onquma ukuthi kufanele kube nencazelo engakanani engemuva kwekhadi lakho.

Beka impendulo ehlolwayo kuqala, bese wengeza kuphela imininingwane eyanele yokuyiqonda. Kuba nzima ukuzihlulela uma ingemuva linepharagrafu, ezinye izimpendulo ezine, iqhinga lokukhumbula nesifundo somlando. Beka ulwazi olusekelayo kwelinye ikhadi uma ufuna ukulugcina enkumbulweni.

Imithetho yokuhlela eku-[How to Make Better Flashcards, isihloko esibhalwe ngesiNgisi](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/) isebenza kahle nalapha:

- into eyodwa ecacile okufanele uyikhumbule
- impendulo eqondile ngaphambi kwencazelo
- imininingwane eyanele ukuze uqonde ikhadi kamuva
- kungabikho izinkomba zempendulo ezifihlwe ngaphambili

Ungawenzi wonke amakhadi angu-128 ngobusuku obubodwa ngoba nje uhlu olusemthethweni lunezinto ezingu-128. Qala ngeqoqo elincane ongakwazi ukubhekana nalo. Engeza amanye inqobo nje uma usakwazi ukuthola isikhathi phakathi nesonto sokubuyekeza amakhadi afikelwe isikhathi; ikhadi ngalinye elisha liletha ukubuyekezwa kwesikhathi esizayo.

## Umjikelezo wokufunda kusukela ekufakeni isicelo kuze kufike inhlolokhono

Le ndlela yezinyathelo eziyisikhombisa igcina umthombo osemthethweni, ukukhumbula ngomlomo nezimpendulo ezishintshayo ohlelweni olulodwa.

### 1. Qinisekisa uhlobo lokuhlolwa

Qhathanisa usuku owafaka ngalo i-Form N-400 nomnqamulajuqu we-USCIS. Faka u-`2008` noma u-`2025` egameni lesethi ukuze amaqoqo emibuzo angaxubani ungaboni.

### 2. Hlukanisa iqoqo elisemthethweni

Landa imibuzo nezimpendulo ezifanele ze-USCIS. Maka into ngayinye ngokuthi `stable` noma `current-answer`, bese wengeza ukuthi iphathelene nohulumeni wobumbano, isifundazwe noma isifunda sokumelwa lapho lokho kusiza.

### 3. Engeza iqoqo elincane

Yakha amakhadi anombuzo ngaphambili nempendulo ngemuva usebenzisa imibhalo esemthethweni. Gcina impendulo esohlwini lwe-USCIS kuqala nanoma iyiphi incazelo ngezansi kwayo. Hlola ikhadi ngalinye ngaphambi kokulifaka ekubuyekezeni.

### 4. Buyekeza amakhadi afikelwe isikhathi bese uphendula ngomlomo

Hlola amakhadi afikelwe isikhathi sokubuyekezwa nsuku zonke uma ukwazi, kunokuba ubuyekeze wonke amakhadi nsuku zonke. I-FSRS ihlela ukubuyekezwa kwesikhathi esizayo isebenzisa umlando wakho wokukhumbula, kodwa ayikwazi ukuqinisekisa ukuthi impendulo isahambisana nesikhathi samanje yini.

### 5. Zijwayeze ukuphendula amaqoqo emibuzo ngomlomo

Kanye noma kabili ngesonto, cela omunye umuntu afunde iqoqo elixutshiwe ngaphandle kokukubonisa isikrini. Ziqeqeshele ukukhumbula izimpendulo ngaphandle kwezinkomba ozibonayo. Imiphumela yalezi zikhathi zokuzijwayeza ayibikezeli umphumela wokuhlolwa okusemthethweni.

### 6. Sebenzisa amaphutha ukuqondisa ukufunda okwengeziwe

Lapho uphuthelwa ikhadi, thola inkinga: ukukhumbula, ukuphimisa, ukuqonda incazelo, noma impendulo engasafanele. Funda ingxenye ehambisana nalokho kumhlahlandlela wokufunda we-USCIS uma umqondo ngokwawo ungacacile. Qhubeka nokuzijwayeza ngomlomo ukuze ukwazi ukuphendula ngaphandle kwezinkomba ezibonakalayo zekhadi.

### 7. Hlola iqembu le-current-answer

Sebenzisa ikhasi le-USCIS lezinguquko lapho sekubekwe usuku lwenhlolokhono yakho, uphinde ulisebenzise ngaphambi nje kwayo. Faka izimpendulo ezintsha esikhundleni salezo ezingasafanele kunokushiya zombili izinhlobo engemuva.

## Uma ufaneleka ku-65/20, funda imibuzo enenkanyezi

Abafake izicelo bathola ukucatshangelwa okukhethekile kwe-65/20 uma, **ngesikhathi befaka i-Form N-400**, beneminyaka **engu-65 noma ngaphezulu** futhi sebengabahlali baphakade abasemthethweni **iminyaka engu-20 noma ngaphezulu**. Ekuhlolweni kuka-2025, abafanelekayo bangafunda **imibuzo engu-20 ephawulwe ngenkanyezi** eqoqweni elisemthethweni futhi benze ukuhlolwa kwe-civics ngolimi abalukhethayo. Isikhulu sibuza engu-10 kuleyo mibuzo, futhi umfakisicelo kufanele aphendule okungenani engu-6 ngendlela efanele.

Le mininingwane yokuhlolwa ivela ku-[PDF esemthethweni yemibuzo nezimpendulo zango-2025, ngesiNgisi](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf). Funda [ikhasi lamanje le-USCIS elithi Exceptions and Accommodations, ngesiNgisi](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/exceptions-and-accommodations) ukuze uqinisekise ukuthi lo mthetho usebenza kanjani, kanye nokunye ukukhululwa ezimfunekweni zesiNgisi, ukukhululwa ngenxa yokukhubazeka, izimfuneko zabahumushi namalungiselelo akhethekile.

Uma i-65/20 isebenza kuwe, yakha isethi ehlukile noma isihlungi ngemibuzo esemthethweni engu-20 enenkanyezi. Ungafundi amakhadi angu-20 akhethwe nje noma kanjani. Izimpendulo ezishintshayo kuleli qoqo elinenkanyezi nazo zisadinga ukuhlolwa ngokwesikhathi senhlolokhono ngendlela efanayo.

## Ukuhlola kokugcina ngaphambi kwenhlolokhono

- Ngiqinisekisile uhlobo lokuhlolwa kwami ngokosuku engafaka ngalo i-Form N-400.
- Amakhadi ami avela eqoqweni elisemthethweni elifanele lemibuzo ye-USCIS.
- Ngiyakwazi ukuphendula ngomlomo ngaphandle kwezinkomba zezinketho eziningi.
- Amakhadi acela izimpendulo ezimbili noma ngaphezulu aqeqesha lonke inani eliceliwe.
- Izimpendulo zamanje eziphathelene nohulumeni wobumbano, isifundazwe nesifunda sokumelwa zinamathegi azo ahlukene.
- Ngizihlolile lezo zimpendulo ezingashintsha ngaphambi nje kwenhlolokhono.
- Uma kungenzeka ngifanelekele ukukhululwa noma amalungiselelo akhethekile, ngihlole imithetho ye-USCIS ngokuqondile.
- Ngilungiselela nezinye izingxenye zenhlolokhono yokuthola ubuzwe nanoma yikuphi ukuhlolwa kwesiNgisi okudingekayo, hhayi i-civics kuphela.

Isethi ewusizo yamakhadi okulungiselela ukuhlolwa kobuzwe base-U.S. igcina ulwazi lwe-civics olungavami ukushintsha lulula ukukhumbula, futhi yenza izimpendulo ezishintshayo zibe lula ukuzithola, ukuziqinisekisa nokuzishintsha. Qinisekisa uhlobo lokuhlolwa ngokosuku owafaka ngalo isicelo. Zijwayeze ukuphendula ngomlomo usebenzisa imibhalo esemthethweni ye-USCIS. Thatha usuku lwenhlolokhono njengomnqamulajuqu wokuqinisekisa yonke impendulo engashintsha.

Lesi sihloko sinikeza indlela yokufunda, hhayi iseluleko sezomthetho. I-USCIS iyisiphathimandla esinquma izinhlobo zokuhlolwa, izimpendulo ezamukelekayo, ukukhululwa, amalungiselelo akhethekile nezinguquko zezimpendulo zamanje.
