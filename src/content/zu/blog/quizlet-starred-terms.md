Ake sithi unamakhadi okufunda angu-80 esahlukweni esisodwa, kodwa ayisithupha akubambezela njalo. Ngaphambi kokuba uwaphinde wonke lawo ayisithupha, bheka ukuthi yini eyenza ngalinye libe nzima. Uma ungayikhumbuli impendulo, zijwayeze ukuyikhumbula. Uma umbuzo ungacacile, wulungise. Noma ungaliphinda izikhathi ezingu-20, umbuzo ongacacile usazokudida.

Yenza uhlu olufushane lwamakhadi ongakwazanga ukuwaphendula kahle. Qinisekisa ukuthi yilawo avelayo lapho uzilolonga, bese ubuyela kuso sonke isahluko. Nansi indlela yokwenza lokho ngezinkanyezi ze-Quizlet.

## Khetha amagama amakwe ngenkanyezi ku-Quizlet

Lezi zinyathelo ziqinisekiswe ngomhla ka-1 ku-Okthoba 2026 [emiyalelweni ye-Quizlet yokufunda amagama amakwe ngenkanyezi (ngesiNgisi)](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360031172312-Studying-most-missed-terms-with-stars).

1. Ngena ku-akhawunti yakho bese uvula isethi yakho yamakhadi.
2. Cindezela inkanyezi eseceleni kwegama ngalinye ofuna ukulifunda.
3. Khetha **Starred** ukuze ufunde okumakiwe, noma **Study X** eseceleni kuka-**Study all**, kuye ngokuthi iyiphi inkinobho evelayo.
4. Khetha umsebenzi wokuzilolonga ukuze uqale ukufunda amakhadi owakhethile.

Ukuze ususe inkanyezi, yicindezele futhi. Nguwe wedwa obona ukuthi umake maphi amagama.

Ikhasi losizo lichaza nendlela yokumaka amagama ngenkanyezi ku-**Flashcards**. Vula izilungiselelo (**Options**), uvale ukuhlukaniswa kwamakhadi (**Sorting**), bese ukhetha ukuwaqala kabusha (**Restart Flashcards**). Uma amakhadi owamake ngenkanyezi engaveli, hlola uhlu nomsebenzi wokuzilolonga ngendlela echazwe ngezansi.

Uma umake amagama kumasethi amaningana, imiyalelo ye-Quizlet ithi faka lawo masethi kufolda eyodwa, uvule umsebenzi wokuzilolonga, bese ukhetha **Options → Study starred** ukuze ufunde okumakiwe.

Izinyathelo ezingenhla zivela ekhasini losizo le-Quizlet. Ithebula nokuhlola okungezansi kuyiziphakamiso zokuthi ungakuhlela kanjani ukuzilolonga kwakho.

## Bheka ukuthi kungani ikhadi ngalinye likuhlupha

Ngaphambi kokuzilolonga ngohlu olufushane, zama ukuphendula amakhadi ambalwa ungabheki izimpendulo ezingemuva. Bhala impendulo, uyisho ngokuzwakalayo noma uzinqumele engqondweni ukuthi uzothini ngaphambi kokuyibheka. Bhala phansi ukuthi uphendule kanjani. Impendulo ingabukeka ijwayelekile uma usuyibona, ngisho noma ubungayikhumbuli ngesikhathi uzama ukuyisho.

Sebenzisa leli thebula lamakhadi ayisithupha njengesibonelo. Ake sithi amakhadi avela esethini yesifundo sesiNgisi enamagama okufanele uwafunde nezikhumbuzo zesifundo. Esikhundleni salezi zibonelo, bhala imibuzo esemakhadini akho nokuthi kwenzekeni lapho uyiphendula.

| Ikhadi nombuzo | Okwenzekile | Ongakwenza ngaphambi kokuphinda ufunde |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A: “I ___ your invitation: accept or except?” | Uthe “except”, okuyimpendulo engalungile. | Lifake ohlwini. Qaphela ukuthi elithi “accept” lisho ukuvuma ukwamukela, kanti elithi “except” lisho ukushiya okuthile ngaphandle. |
| B: “May I ___ your pen: borrow or lend?” | Uqagele wathi “borrow”, wangakwazi ukuchaza ukuthi ukhethe lelo gama ngani. | Lifake ohlwini. Zama ukukhumbula igama ngaphandle kokunikwa izinketho, bese uchaza ukuthi ubani othola ipeni. |
| C: “What is the plural of child?” | Ukhumbule kahle wathi “children” ungakayibheki impendulo. | Lishiye ngaphandle kulokhu. Buyela kulo lapho uphinda sonke isahluko. |
| D: “What does light mean?” | Umbuzo ubungacacile. Uphendule wathi “not heavy” (akusindi), kanti ngemuva ekhadini kubhalwe ukuthi “illumination” (ukukhanya). | Faka umusho ocacisa incazelo, njengokuthi “This bag is light.” (Lesi sikhwama asisindi.) Hlola ukuthi impendulo elungisiwe iyahambisana nomusho ngaphambi kokuzilolonga. |
| E: “Which word list is on Friday's quiz?” | Isikhumbuzo sesidala. Ngemuva ekhadini kubhalwe ukuthi Unit 3, kanti ephepheni uthisha alibuyekezile kubhalwe ukuthi Unit 4. | Bheka iphepha lakamuva likathisha bese ulungisa isikhumbuzo ngaphambi kokuhlela ukuthi uzophinda ini. |
| F: “What is the past participle of write?” | Elithi “written” lingelesifundo sezenzo esizofundwa kamuva. Leso sifundo asifakiwe esivivinyweni. | Lishiye ngaphandle kulokhu. Bhala phansi ukuthi lingelesiphi isifundo ozosifunda kamuva. |

Kulesi sibonelo, amakhadi A no-B yiwo ozozilolonga ngawo manje. Amakhadi D no-E asadinga ukulungiswa ngaphambi kokuba uzilolonge ngawo. Ikhadi C lingalinda uze uphinde isahluko ngoba uyikhumbulile impendulo yalo. Ikhadi F alingeni kulokho ozokufunda namuhla.

Zibhalele isikhumbuzo esifushane njengokuthi “Ku-D, faka umusho onegama elithi light” noma “Ku-E, hlola iphepha elibuyekeziwe likathisha.” Uma ungakwazi ukushintsha okubhalwe esethini, gcina imibuzo nezimpendulo ozilungisile emanothini akho bese uzilolonga ngawo. Ukuze ubhale imibuzo ecace kakhudlwana, bheka [indlela yokwenza amakhadi okufunda angcono (ngesiNgisi)](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/).

## Hlola ikhadi olifakile nelilodwa olishiyile ngaphandle

Ngaphambi kokuba uchithe isikhathi esiningi uzilolonga, bhala phansi izibonelo ezimbili ozozibona kalula. Ikhadi A kufanele libe sohlwini olufushane, kanti ikhadi C kufanele lisale ngaphandle. Bhala nohlu oluphelele olindele ukulubona. Kulokhu lunamakhadi amabili, u-A no-B.

Qhathanisa amakhadi owakhethile nohlu olubhalile ngaphambi kokuqala. Hlola inani lamakhadi, bese uqinisekisa ukuthi yiwo kanye obufuna ukuwakhetha. Inani lingafana noma ukhethe amakhadi angafanele.

Uma usuzilolonga, bheka ukuthi liyavela yini ikhadi A, elibuza ngesimemo. Qaphela nokuthi liyavela yini ikhadi C, elibuza ngobuningi begama elithi “child”. Uma kuvela ikhadi C, yima uhlole ukuthi ukhethe maphi amakhadi. Uma ikhadi A lingaveli zisuka, kungenzeka lisazovela. Uma ubona ikhadi A ngesikhathi ikhadi C lingakaveli, okwaziyo nje ukuthi u-A uvelile. Kusamele uhlole ukuthi wonke amakhadi obuwafuna afakiwe nokuthi ashiywe ngaphandle wonke obungawafuni.

Kulolu hlu lwamakhadi amabili, phawula ukuthi u-A uvelile yini nokuthi u-B uvelile yini. Uma uhlu lulude, qhathanisa ikhadi ngalinye obufuna ukuliphinda nalawo ozilolonge ngawo ngaphambi kokuthi uthi usuwaphinde wonke. Uma uzilolonge isikhashana nje, kungase kungakacaci ukuthi uwaphindile wonke yini.

Uma amagama owamake ngenkanyezi ku-Quizlet engaveli ngendlela obulindele ngayo, zibuze le mibuzo emibili.

- Ingabe amakhadi asohlwini yiwo obufuna ukuwakhetha?
- Ingabe umsebenzi wokuzilolonga owukhethile uyawaveza lawo makhadi?

Uma uhlu lungahambisani nalokho obukuhlosile, lungisa amakhadi owakhethile ngaphambi kokuqhubeka. Uma uhlu lulungile kodwa umsebenzi wokuzilolonga ungahambi ngendlela obulindele ngayo, bhala ukuthi iyiphi isethi, yimuphi umsebenzi nokuthi iyiphi idivayisi oyisebenzisayo. Faka nesibonelo esisodwa esicacile sekhadi elifakiwe noma elishiywe ngaphandle. Nika ithimba losizo leyo mininingwane. Ukuthi amakhadi avele ngendlela obungayilindele kukodwa akusho ukuthi usukhohlwe okwengeziwe.

## Hlela ukubuyela kuso sonke isahluko

Phendula imibuzo esemakhadini A no-B, uhlole iphutha ngalinye, bese uzama futhi ngemva kokwenza okunye. Ukuphinda ngaso leso sikhathi kukusiza ubone ukuthi uyakuqonda yini okulungisiwe. Ukuzama kamuva kukunika elinye ithuba lokuyikhumbula impendulo ungayibhekile.

Uma usukwazi ukuphendula umbuzo osekhadini ngaphandle kwezinkomba futhi uchaze iphuzu obuligejile, likhiphe ohlwini olufushane. Uma usaqagela, ligcine. Lo mbandela ukusiza unqume ukuthi usungayeka nini kulokhu. Ukuthi usayikhumbula yini impendulo ngemva kwesikhathi kuyobonakala lapho uzama futhi.

Ngaphambi kokuvala amanothi akho, hlela isikhathi sokuphinda sonke isahluko. Buyisela ikhadi C namakhadi osuwalungisile ekuzilolongeni, bese uhlola okunye okusesahlukweni ukuze ubone lapho usadinga khona ukuzilolonga. Gcina inothi elihlukile elisho ukuthi ikhadi F lingelesifundo esizayo, ukuze ungacabangi ukuthi usuyifundile leyo ngxenye.

Zibhalele isinyathelo esisodwa ozosithatha ngokulandelayo. “Kusasa, phendula imibuzo esemakhadini esahluko ngaphandle kwezinkomba. Yakha kabusha uhlu olufushane ngamakhadi ongakwazanga ukuwaphendula kahle.” Vuselela uhlu lwakho ngokwalokho okwazi ukukukhumbula.
