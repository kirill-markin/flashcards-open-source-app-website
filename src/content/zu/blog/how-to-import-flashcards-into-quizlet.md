Impendulo ethi “red, green, blue” ibukeka iyimpendulo evamile yekhadi lokufunda. Kodwa uma usebenzisa okhefana ukuhlukanisa igama nencazelo yalo, labo khefana benza kungacaci ukuthi umugqa kufanele uhlukaniswe kuphi. Izimpawu zokuloba ezimbalwa zingakwenza kudingeke ulungise uhlu lwamagama osuvele ululungisile.

Ukuze ungenise amakhadi okufunda ku-Quizlet usuka ku-Excel, Google Sheets noma umbhalo, qala ngamakholomu amabili: elinye elamagama, elinye elezincazelo. Kopisha idatha, uyinamathisele efomini lokungenisa elikuwebhusayithi, bese ukhetha amathebhu phakathi kwezinkambu nemigqa emisha phakathi kwamakhadi. Qala ngokuzama imigqa emine ukuze uhlole ipheya ngayinye ngaphambi kokudlulisa lonke uhlu.

## Ungalithola kuphi ifomu lokungenisa le-Quizlet

Kuwebhusayithi yayo, i-Quizlet ichaza ukungenisa kusetshenziswa okhefana, amathebhu noma amadeshi phakathi kwezinkambu, namasemikholoni noma imigqa emisha phakathi kwamakhadi. [Imiyalelo esemthethweni yokungenisa](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029977151-Creating-sets-by-importing-content) inikeza lezi zinyathelo:

1. Ngena ngemvume bese ukhetha **Create → Flashcard set** (Dala → Isethi yamakhadi okufunda).
2. Faka isihloko, ukhethe **Import** (Ngenisa), bese unamathisela umbhalo wakho.
3. Qondanisa izilungiselelo zezihlukanisi nombhalo owufakayo. Ezibonelweni ezilapha, sebenzisa **Tab** (Ithebhu) no-**New line** (Umugqa omusha).
4. Khetha **Import**, bese ukhetha izilimi zamagama nezincazelo.
5. Hlola amakhadi ngaphambi kokukhetha **Create** ukuze ugcine futhi ushicilele isethi.

Imiyalelo yahlolwa ngomhla ka-15 Septhemba 2026. Izibonelo ezingezansi zichaza indlela yokulungisa nokuhlola umbhalo; azichazi ukuhlola okwenziwe ngemva kokungena ngemvume.

## Qala ngebanga elincane lamaseli espredishithini

Ku-Excel noma ku-Google Sheets, beka igama nencazelo yalo emgqeni ngamunye. Nasi isivivinyo samakhadi amane:

| Igama | Incazelo |
| --- | --- |
| rojo | red |
| azul | blue |
| verde | green |
| colores primarios de la luz | red, green, blue |

Kopisha kuphela imigqa emine yedatha emakholomini amabili. Uma izihloko zisemgqeni woku-1, khetha **A2:B5**. Ungazifaki izihloko ebangeni olikopishayo.

Umphumela owulindele amakhadi amane, anamagama esiSpanishi nezincazelo zesiNgisi. Ikhadi lokugcina kufanele ligcine u-“colores primarios de la luz” ndawonye kolunye uhlangothi no-“red, green, blue” ndawonye kolunye. Okhefana bayingxenye yempendulo.

Uma leso sibonelo sesicacile, lungisa esakho ispredishithi. Sebenzisa ikhophi ukuze ugcine esokuqala ozosiqhathanisa naso. Susa imigqa engenalutho, ungawafaki amanothi noma amakholomu engeziwe kulokho okukhethayo. Gcwalisa noma yiliphi igama noma incazelo engekho ngaphambi kokukopisha.

Gcina amakholomu amabili ehlangene lapho uhlunga idatha. Ukuhlunga amagama kuphela kungawahlukanisa nezimpendulo zawo, ngakho ngisho noma inani lamakhadi angenisiwe lilungile, amapheya angase abe nephutha.

## Ifomethi yombhalo ongenakho ukufometha isebenzisa amathebhu angempela

Le migqa emine ibukeka kanje njengombhalo ohlukaniswe ngamathebhu. Isikhala ngasinye phakathi kwegama nencazelo yalo sinohlamvu olulodwa lwethebhu:

```text
rojo	red
azul	blue
verde	green
colores primarios de la luz	red, green, blue
```

Ithebhu iwuhlamvu oluthile. Izikhala eziningana zingabukeka zifana nayo, kodwa azisona isihlukanisi sethebhu. Ukuthayipha izinhlamvu ezithi `\t` nakho akuyifaki ithebhu. Uma kunzima ukwenza lokhu kusihleli sakho sombhalo, faka amapheya emakholomini amabili espredishithini bese ukopisha lawo maseli.

Ikhadi ngalinye kufanele libe emgqeni owodwa wangempela. Incazelo ende ingabonakala iqhubekela emgqeni wesibili ngenxa yokuthi iwindi lincane; lokho akufaki ukunqamuka komugqa embhalweni. Kodwa uma ufaka ukunqamuka komugqa ngaphakathi kweseli lespredishithi, kuba yingxenye yombhalo. Susa lokho kunqamuka okungaphakathi kwamaseli ngaphambi kokusebenzisa imigqa emisha njengezihlukanisi zamakhadi.

Susa nanoma yimaphi amathebhu angaphakathi kwegama noma kwencazelo. Kule fomethi, amathebhu kufanele abe phakathi kwezinkambu ezimbili kuphela.

## Uma uhlu lwakho luyifayela le-CSV

Vula i-CSV espredishithini ngaphambi kokulungisa okuzongeniswa. Irekhodi le-CSV elifana naleli linezinkambu ezimbili uma lifundwa ngokwemithetho yalo yezimpawu zokucaphuna:

```text
"colores primarios de la luz","red, green, blue"
```

Impendulo engaphakathi kwezimpawu zokucaphuna inokhefana. Inkambu yombhalo ehlukanisa okufakiwe ngokhefana ingase ingakwazi ukufunda ifomethi ye-CSV, ngakho ungathembeli ezimpawini zokucaphuna ukuze zigcine impendulo ihlangene lapho.

Ku-Excel noma ku-Google Sheets, qinisekisa ukuthi irekhodi ligcwalisa amaseli amabili kuphela. Bese ukopisha lawo maseli ukuze ufake idatha ehlukaniswe ngamathebhu. Uma impendulo isabalale yaya kwamanye amakholomu, qala ngokulungisa izilungiselelo zokungenisa i-CSV zespredishithi. Ukukopisha umugqa osuvele wonakele kudlulisela lelo phutha phambili.

Hlola namanani ispredishithi esingase siwahumushe kabusha. Uma u-`0012` eyikhodi okudingeka uyifunde, ukuyiguqula ibe ngu-`12` kushintsha ikhadi. Qhathanisa izihlonzi, izinsuku, izimpawu zokucaphuna namagama anezimpawu zokuphimisa nefayela lokuqala ngaphambi kokukopisha ibanga lamaseli.

## Uma amakhadi engahambisani nemigqa yakho

Sebenzisa isivivinyo ukuthola indawo yokuqala engahambisani. Shintsha into eyodwa ngesikhathi, bese uqhathanisa umphumela naleyo migqa emine yomthombo.

| Okubonakalayo | Inkinga engaba khona yesihlukanisi | Okufanele ukushintshe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impendulo ihlukana lapho kunokhefana khona | Kukhethwe ukhefana phakathi kwezinkambu | Lungisa amakholomu amabili bese ukhetha i-Tab |
| U-“well-being” uhlukana phakathi negama | Kukhethwe ideshi phakathi kwezinkambu | Gcina ikhifeni bese uhlukanisa izinkambu ngethebhu |
| Impendulo eyodwa iba amakhadi amaningana | Kukhona ukunqamuka kwemigqa ngaphakathi kombhalo womthombo | Beka impendulo emgqeni owodwa wangempela |
| U-“Term” no-“Definition” bavela njengekhadi | Kukopishwe umugqa wezihloko | Khetha imigqa yedatha kuphela |
| Zombili izinkambu zihlala ohlangothini olulodwa | Kusetshenziswe izikhala esikhundleni sethebhu | Kopisha ibanga lamaseli elinamakholomu amabili espredishithini |

Ngokwesibonelo, impendulo eno-“first stage” no-“second stage” emigqeni ehlukene ingaguqulwa ibe ngu-`first stage; second stage`. Lokho kugcina zombili izingxenye emgqeni owodwa. Gcina u-**New line** ekhethiwe phakathi kwamakhadi uma usebenzisa isemikholoni ngaphakathi kwempendulo.

Uma lezi zigaba ezimbili zifanele imibuzo ehlukene, bhala amapheya amabili aphelele. [Umhlahlandlela wokwenza amakhadi okufunda angcono (ngesiNgisi)](/blog/how-to-make-better-flashcards/) ungakusiza wenze leso sinqumo.

## Hlola umphumela ngaphambi kokushicilela

Hlola wonke amakhadi amane esivivinyo. Ohlwini lwakho oluphelele, qhathanisa amakhadi angenisiwe nomthombo osuwulungisile:

- Ingabe inani eliphelele lihambisana nenani lemigqa yedatha egcwele?
- Ingabe ipheya yokuqala neyokugcina ziyahambisana, kuhlanganise nezimpendulo zazo eziphelele?
- Ingabe izimpawu zokuloba, izimpawu zokuphimisa namakhodi kuhlezi kungashintshile?
- Ingabe kukhona izinhlangothi ezingenalutho, izihloko, amakhadi aphindaphindiwe noma izimpendulo ezihambisana namagama okungewona?
- Ingabe zonke izimpendulo olungise ukunqamuka kwemigqa yazo zisazwakala kahle?

Funda amapheya amaningana avamile kanye nalawo asolisayo. Ukufana kwamanani kuphela ngeke kukubonise impendulo engalungile exhunywe egameni elifanele. Uma uthola iphutha, lungisa umthombo ngaphambi kokuphinda ungenise. Gcina ifayela elilungisiwe ukuze ulisebenzise uma kudingeka ulungise futhi esikhathini esizayo.
