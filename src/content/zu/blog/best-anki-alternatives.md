## Impendulo emfushane ngaphambi kokuba uthuthe noma yini

Qhubeka ne-Anki njengokujwayelekile. Shintsha kuphela uma olunye uhlelo lususa inkinga ephindaphindayo enkulu ngokwanele ukuba ukuthutha kube nomvuzo.

Imikhiqizo emithathu yamukela amaphakheji e-Anki anedatha yokufunda, kodwa iyahlukahluka ngokuthi ithembisa ukugcina ini:

- **I-Mnemosyne** ibhala ngokungenisa okuphelele okuvela ku-Anki, okuhlanganisa izinhlobo zamakhadi ezenziwe ngokwezifiso nedatha yokufunda. Kulezi zinhlelo iyona esondelene kakhulu nendlela evamile yokusebenza kukhompyutha, ngedatha egcinwe endaweni nangomthombo ovulekile, nakuba ingenalo uhlelo olwenzelwe i-iOS ngqo.
- **I-Mochi** ingenisa amafayela e-`.apkg` kanye nomlando wokubuyekeza. Iguqula i-HTML ibe yi-Markdown, isuse i-CSS ne-JavaScript, bese isebenzisa u-Remembered noma u-Forgot esikhundleni sezinkinobho ezine zokubuyekeza ze-Anki.
- **I-RemNote** ingenisa amafayela e-`.apkg`, izinhlobo eziningi zamanothi nomlando wokubuyekeza. Umhlahlandlela wayo wamanje uphinde uthi amakhadi angenisiwe aya emgqeni ohlukile obizwa ngokuthi **Need to Learn**, ngakho “umlando wokubuyekeza ungenisiwe” akusho ukuthi “umugqa wanamuhla we-Anki ukopishwe njengoba unjalo.”

Ezine ezisele zidinga ukwakha kabusha okuqukethwe, kunokuthutha lonke iqoqo:

- **I-Quizlet** iwusizo kumasethi omphakathi, amakilasi, imidlalo nokuzijwayeza okuqondiswayo.
- **I-Brainscape** inika iqembu indlela elula yokubuyekeza ngokulinganisa ukuzethemba kusukela ku-1 kuya ku-5.
- **I-SuperMemo** isho ukuthuthela endleleni yayo evaliwe nakukhathalogi yezifundo zayo.
- **I-Nibomo** ikunikeza uhlelo lwewebhu nezinhlelo ezenzelwe amadivayisi ngqo ezinelayisensi ye-MIT, i-backend ongazisingathela yona, kanye nokufinyelela nge-API ne-MCP. Indlela yayo ye-TXT noma ye-CSV, edinga ukuhlolwa, ayisigcini isimo sakho sokufunda se-Anki.

Uma amakhadi akho encike ekubonisweni ngendlela ethile ngqo, ezingezeni noma emgqeni wamanje wokubuyekeza, ukuhlala ku-Anki akusho ukungakwazi ukunquma. Yiyo impendulo.

3. **Ngingakwazi ukuphinde ngihambe?** Khipha idatha ohlelweni olwamukelayo bese uhlola ukuthi ifomethi yokuyikhipha iquketheni ngempela.

Isingenisi singaphumelela embuzweni wokuqala kodwa sehluleke kweminye emibili.

## RemNote: faka amakhadi kumanothi axhumene

I-RemNote ishintsha lapho amakhadi evela khona. Esikhundleni sokunakekela iqoqo elihlukile eceleni kwamanothi ezinkulumo, udala amakhadi ngaphakathi kohlaka lwamanothi, idokhumenti noma umsebenzi owenziwa nge-PDF. Leso isizathu esizwakalayo sokushiya i-Anki uma ukukopisha izinto phakathi kohlelo lwamanothi nolwamakhadi okufunda sekuyiyona ngxenye edla isikhathi esiningi.

Indlela yayo yokuthutha ingenisa izinhlobo eziningi zedatha, kodwa kudingeka sicacise ukuthi kwenzekani emgqeni wamakhadi azobuyekezwa. [Umhlahlandlela wamanje we-RemNote wokungenisa kusuka ku-Anki](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6751471-importing-from-anki) uthi khipha i-`.apkg` enolwazi lwezikhathi zokubuyekeza, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe zamaqoqo nemidiya. Ingenisa umlando wokubuyekeza nezinhlobo eziningi zamanothi, kuhlanganise nezisisekelo, ama-cloze namakhadi ajwayelekile okufihla izingxenye zesithombe.

Umhlahlandlela ofanayo uthi amakhadi asanda kungeniswa aya emgqeni ohlukile we-**Need to Learn**. Ngamanye amazwi, i-RemNote iyawuthola umlando, kodwa imibhalo ayithembisi ukuthi umugqa wakho wamanje wamakhadi alindele ukubuyekezwa ku-Anki uzovela ungashintshile. I-CSS eningi nayo iyasuswa, i-JavaScript eyenziwe ngokwezifiso ayisekelwa, omunye umsindo owenziwa embhalweni ngesikhathi sokusetshenziswa awusebenzi, kanti ukungenisa amakhadi afihla izingxenye zezithombe kuncike emagameni athile alindelwe amanothi nezinkambu.

Zama iqoqo elimele amakhadi akho bese uhlola amakhadi nomugqa wokuqala. Ukungenisa okubukeka kucocekile kuyingxenye yokuhlola kuphela.

Izinhlelo ezifakiwe zedeskithophu nezeselula zisebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ngemva kokufakwa nokungena ngemvume. [Umhlahlandlela wokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](https://help.remnote.com/en/articles/6752029-offline-mode) ubeka umkhawulo obalulekile ngemidiya: eyedeskithophu igcina zonke izithombe nama-PDF esizinda solwazi, kodwa eyeselula igcina okwesikhashana ezinye zezithombe zakamuva kuphela. Uhlelo lwewebhu lungaqhubeka kuthebhu esivele ivuliwe, kodwa aluvuleki ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi uma beluvaliwe.

Sebenzisa i-RemNote uma amanothi axhumene ezuzisa ngokwanele ukuba ushintshe indlela iqoqo lakho elakhiwe ngayo. Gcina i-Anki uma izifanekiso zamakhadi nezengezo kuyisisekelo sendlela osebenza ngayo, hhayi umhlobiso nje.

## Mochi: i-Markdown egcinwe endaweni nokukhipha okuphelele ngefomethi yayo

I-Mochi iyindawo elula kakhudlwana yokuthuthela kuyo kulabo abafuna idatha egcinwe endaweni, amakhadi abhalwe nge-Markdown nezilawuli ezimbalwa esikrinini. Izinhlelo zayo ezifakiwe zisebenza kuzo zonke izinhlobo eziyinhloko zamadeskithophu namaselula futhi zingasetshenziswa [ngokuphelele ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, kungadingeki i-akhawunti](https://mochi.cards/docs/getting-started/download-and-install/). Ingxenye ekhokhelwayo ukuvumelanisa, okubiza u-$5 ngenyanga.

Isingenisi esiqondile se-Anki sithwala umlando wokubuyekeza, okwenza i-Mochi ibe phambili kakhulu kunezinketho ezamukela umbhalo kuphela. Indlela eguqula ngayo nayo ichazwe ngokucacile okungavamile: i-Mochi isusa i-CSS ne-JavaScript bese iguqula i-HTML ibe yi-Markdown. Lokho kuyasebenza uma incazelo iqukethwe umbhalo nezinto ezivamile ezinamathiselwe. Kodwa kuyisixwayiso uma incazelo ithwalwa isifanekiso.

I-Mochi manje inikeza izinhlelo ezimbili zokubeka izikhathi zokubuyekeza. I-algorithm yayo isalokhu iyona esetshenziswa ngokuzenzakalela, kanti [i-FSRS ingavulwa](https://mochi.cards/docs/reviewing/fsrs/) ngaphandle kokusula inqubekelaphambili esivele ikhona ku-Mochi. I-FSRS ibala isimo samakhadi ngomlando wokubuyekeza i-Mochi enawo. Izilinganiso zokubuyekeza ziseyizinketho ezimbili—Remembered noma Forgot—ngisho naku-FSRS, ngakho abasebenzisi be-Anki abathembele ku-Hard naku-Easy njengezimpawu ezihlukile kufanele balindele indlela ehlukile yokusebenza nsuku zonke.

Ukuphinde ukhiphe idatha kucace kakhulu kunasezinhlelweni eziningi ezivaliwe. [Ukukhipha ngefomethi yayo ye-`.mochi`](https://mochi.cards/docs/import-and-export/exporting/) kufaka amakhadi, izifanekiso, okunamathiselwe, amathegi, ukuhleleka kwamaqoqo nomlando wokubuyekeza. Amafayela e-Markdown nawe-CSV kulula ukuwahlola kwenye indawo, kodwa ashiya ngaphandle umlando wokubuyekeza nenye imethadatha.

Amakhosombe e-GitHub omphakathi e-Mochi aqukethe [okuhlanganisa nezinye izinhlelo namathuluzi ahlobene](https://github.com/mochi-cards/open-source), hhayi uhlelo oluyinhloko noma iseva yokuvumelanisa esekelwayo. Ikhethe ngenxa yokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nokuthutheka kwedatha, hhayi ukuze ulawule ikhodi yomthombo.

## Mnemosyne: indlela yedeskithophu enomthombo ovulekile

I-Mnemosyne isondelene kakhulu nesakhiwo esivamile “sohlelo nesizindalwazi esigcinwe endaweni.” Inguqulo yamanje isekela i-Windows, i-macOS ne-Linux, kanye nohlelo lwe-Android lokubuyekeza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi. Ikhasi lezici zayo lichaza amakhadi anokuqukethwe okuhlukahlukene, amathegi ahlelwe ngamazinga, ama-plugin, isikali sokubuyekeza esingu-0–5 nokungenisa okuphelele kwe-Anki okuhlanganisa izinhlobo zamakhadi ezenziwe ngokwezifiso nedatha yokufunda.

Lena indlela eqonde kakhulu lapha yokuthuthela emthonjeni ovulekile uma ufuna ukushiya i-Anki ngaphandle kokungena ohlelweni olukhulu lwamanothi noma kusevisi yefu. Iphinde ibe [neseva yokuvumelanisa eyakhelwe ngaphakathi](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/help/syncing) engasebenza kudeskithophu noma emshinini ongenaso isixhumi esibonakalayo, ihlanganise idatha yokufunda evela ezinhlelweni zamadivayisi amaningana.

Ukushiyeka kwayo nakho kuyingxenye yesinqumo. Uhlelo lwe-Android [alikwazi ukuhlela amakhadi](https://mnemosyne-proj.org/help/android-client). Abasebenzisi be-iOS kufanele babuyekeze ngesiphequluli bexhuma kuseva esebenza komunye umshini, futhi ikhasi elisemthethweni lezici zaleyo seva lixwayisa ngokuthi ayinazo izici zokuphepha. Ukuziqhubela iseva yokuvumelanisa kusho nokugcina iseva ifinyeleleka, ukumisa inethiwekhi nokugcina amakhophi ayisipele efolda yedatha.

Ilayisensi ichazwe kabanzi kunokuthi “GPL” nje: [ifayela lelayisensi lephrojekthi](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/master/LICENSE) likhomba imigomo ehlukene ngokwengxenye. [Ilayisensi yengxenye eyinhloko](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/master/mnemosyne/LICENSE) isebenzisa i-AGPL v3 enombandela owengeziwe ngegama nokubonga umsunguli, kanti [i-openSM2sync](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mnemosyne-proj/mnemosyne/master/openSM2sync/LICENSE) isebenzisa i-LGPL v3. Funda lawo mafayela uma uhlela ukushintsha noma ukusabalalisa kabusha isofthiwe.

## Quizlet: shintsha ngenxa yekilasi, hhayi ukugcina iqoqo linjengoba linjalo

I-Quizlet iphumelela komunye umsebenzi. Amasethi okufunda omphakathi, amakilasi othisha, ukwabelana, i-Match, i-Test, i-Learn nemisebenzi yamaqembu kulula ukukunikeza ikilasi kunephrofayela ye-Anki eyenziwe ngokwezifiso.

Ukuthutha kugcina embhalweni ongenafomethi kuphela. I-Quizlet ingaguqula imigqa enamathiselwe ibe amagama nezincazelo, kodwa ayifundi i-`.apkg` iphinde yakhe izinhlobo zamanothi, izifanekiso, izikhathi zokubuyekeza noma umlando wokubuyekeza. Abadali bangakwazi [ukukhipha amasethi abo njengombhalo okopishiwe](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034345672-Exporting-your-sets), ngaphandle kwezithombe; amasethi akopishwe kwabanye abasebenzisi awakwazi ukukhishwa. Lokhu ukuthutheka kokuqukethwe, hhayi kweqoqo lonke.

Indlela i-Quizlet ehlela ngayo izikhathi iyashintsha. Indlela yayo entsha ye-[Spaced Repetition](https://quizlet.com/features/spaced-repetition) ivuleka ngokuzenzakalela kuwebhu kumasethi anamagama okungenani angu-100, futhi isebenzisa izilinganiso ezithi Repeat, Hard, Okay no-Easy. I-Quizlet isabeka ukusekelwa kweselula njengokuzofika maduze. I-Learn isalokhu iyindlela ehlukile yokuzijwayeza ezivumelanisa nomfundi, enemikhawulo yokusetshenziswa encike ohlelweni olukhokhelwayo.

Ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi nakho kusho izinhlelo zeselula, hhayi iwebhusayithi. I-Quizlet igcina ngokuzenzakalela amasethi ayisishiyagalombili akamuva futhi ikuvumela ukulanda amanye; [i-Flashcards ne-Match zisebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](https://help.quizlet.com/hc/en-us/articles/360030565412-Studying-offline-with-Quizlet-mobile-apps), bese inqubekelaphambili ivumelaniswa lapho uphinda uxhuma.

Thuthela lapha uma inhloso kuwukusabalalisa izinto zokufunda nokwenza imisebenzi yasekilasini. Ukwakha kabusha uhlelo lwakho lwezikhathi osulusebenzise isikhathi eside ukuze nje uthole lezo zici ngokuvamile akuwufanele umzamo.

## Brainscape: izinqumo ezimbalwa ngezikhathi, kodwa ithutha okuncane

I-Brainscape icela ukuba ulinganise ukuzethemba kwakho kusukela ku-1 kuya ku-5, bese isebenzisa leso silinganiso ukubuyisa ngokushesha amakhadi ongakawakhumbuli kahle. Ifomula ephelele ayishicilelwe, kodwa indlela yokuyisebenzisa kulula ukuyifundisa ekilasini elihlanganyelayo.

Yamukela amafayela e-CSV, TXT, XLSX ne-ODS. Lokho kulungele amakhadi anengaphambili nengemuva, kodwa kusho ukuthi izifanekiso, izengezo, imithetho yemidiya nedatha yokufunda ye-Anki kusala emuva. Ukukhipha ikhophi yakho eyisipele kuyisici se-Pro futhi kukhiqiza amafayela angavulwa kuspredishithi aphinde angeniswe kamuva.

Isikhungo sosizo samanje se-Brainscape sithi iwebhusayithi nezinhlelo zayo zeselula manje zinezici ezifanayo eziyinhloko zokuthola, ukudala, ukwabelana nokufunda. Siphinde sichaze ukusebenzisa iselula ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ukuphinda uvumelanise mathupha nokufaka izinguquko emakilasini aselandiwe kudivayisi. Lokho kunikeza indlela esebenzisekayo yokuphinda uxhume, kodwa akuthembisi ukuthi yonke i-akhawunti ingumtapo ophelele ogcinwe endaweni.

Ukukhipha amaqoqo akho kusalokhu [kuyisici se-Pro](https://brainscape.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/115002383872-How-can-I-export-a-backup-of-my-flashcards). Awukho umthombo wekhodi okhishwe ngokusemthethweni noma indlela yokuzisingathela.

Kufanele ucabangele i-Brainscape uma i-Anki ibonakala idinga izilungiselelo eziningi kakhulu kubantu okumele bafunde. Akuyona indlela enhle yokuphuma kumuntu osesebenzisa lezo zilungiselelo kahle.

## SuperMemo: khetha indlela yayo, wamukele ukuqala phansi

Isevisi yamanje ye-SuperMemo.com iyinkundla yokufunda izilimi kuwebhu, ku-iOS naku-Android, eyakhelwe endleleni yokuhlela ye-SuperMemo evaliwe. Ihlukile kumkhiqizo omdala we-Windows okungenzeka ujwayelwe abantu asebenesikhathi eside besebenzisa i-SuperMemo.

Lesi yisinqumo sendlela yokufunda nekhathalogi, hhayi ukuthutha okushelelayo kusuka ku-Anki. I-SuperMemo isekela [ukudala amakhadi amaningi ngokunamathisela imigqa yemibuzo nezimpendulo ehlukaniswe ngezimpawu](https://www.supermemo.com/en/blog/how-to-create-flashcards-for-learning), ngomkhawulo wamakhadi angu-100 ekungeniseni ngakunye. Angitholanga isingenisi samanje esisemthethweni se-`.apkg` noma indlela yokuthi umsebenzisi akhiphe idatha kusevisi esingathiwe. Ngakho izifanekiso, izengezo, ukuhleleka kwemidiya nomlando wokubuyekeza akudluli kuleyo ndlela ebhaliwe.

Izifundo ezilandiwe zingafundwa ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi ezinhlelweni zeselula. [Umhlahlandlela wokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](https://www.supermemo.com/en/faq/can-i-use-supermemo-offline) ushiya ngaphandle izici ze-AI, ukwengeza ama-MemoCards, ukusesha, okuqoshiwe, izibalo nesihleli sezifundo, ngakho lungisa izinto zokufunda bese uvumelanisa ngaphambi kokunqamula uxhumano.

Khetha i-SuperMemo uma indlela yayo noma ikhathalogi yayo yezifundo ibaluleke ngokwanele kuwe ukuba uqale uhlelo olusha lwezikhathi. Uma okubalulekile kuwe kuwukugcina uhlelo osuvele unalo, yona ixazulula inkinga ehlukile.

## Nibomo: ukufinyelela ikhodi yazo zonke izingxenye, kodwa ingenisa okuncane kakhulu kusuka ku-Anki

I-Nibomo ivumela ukuba ulawule izingxenye eziningi zomkhiqizo. [Ikhosombe elinelayisensi ye-MIT](https://github.com/kirill-markin/flashcards-open-source-app) liqukethe uhlelo lwewebhu, izinhlelo ze-iOS ne-Android, i-backend, ukuvumelanisa lapho ubuya ekusebenzeni ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi, ingqalasizinda, [i-API yomphakathi](/docs/api/) kanye [neseva ye-MCP](/docs/mcp-connector/). Ukubuyekeza kusebenzisa [i-FSRS](/docs/architecture/#scheduling). Iwebhu, i-iOS ne-Android kubhala endaweni kuqala, kufake izinguquko emgqeni wokuthumela, bese kuvumelanisa lapho uxhumano lubuya.

Lokho akuyenzi ihambisane ne-Anki. I-Nibomo ayikwazi ukufunda i-`.apkg` noma i-`.colpkg`. [Ukuthutha nge-Anki TXT](/blog/migrate-from-anki-txt-export-open-source-flashcards/) okusekelwayo kuyindlela yokwakha umbhalo wokuqala ngosizo lwe-AI bese uhlolwa, emakhadini asekelwe kakhulu embhalweni. Ayigcini izifanekiso, izengezo, ukuhleleka kwamazinga amaqoqo, izinsuku ezimisiwe, izikhawu noma izehlakalo zokubuyekeza. Izikhombo zemidiya efayeleni le-TXT aziwona amafayela emidiya, ngakho amaqoqo asebenzisa imidiya eningi adinga ukwakhiwa kabusha nokuhlolwa ngokwehlukana.

Iphakheji ye-Nibomo ethi `flashcards.zip` nayo ayibanzi njengekhophi eyisipele. Ihambisa amakhadi, amathegi nemidiya ehlobene phakathi kwezindawo zokusebenza ze-Nibomo. Ayihambisi umlando wokubuyekeza, isimo se-FSRS, izakhiwo ezigcwele zamaqoqo, izilungiselelo zendawo yokusebenza noma ama-akhawunti.

Ingxenye eyinhloko esingathiwe [imahhala ngesikhathi se-beta](/zu/pricing/). Ukuzisingathela zonke izingxenye ukuze zisebenze kubasebenzisi akukhona ukufaka i-Docker ngomyalo owodwa: [umhlahlandlela wokuzisingathela](/docs/self-hosting/) usebenzisa i-AWS CDK futhi udinga izinsiza ze-AWS kanye ne-Cloudflare, i-Resend, i-Sentry, imininingwane eyimfihlo yokufinyelela, ukuthuthwa kwesizindalwazi, amakhophi ayisipele, ukubuyiselwa kwedatha nokuthuthukiselwa ezinguqulweni ezintsha. I-Docker Compose iyindlela yokuthuthukisa isofthiwe, hhayi indlela esekelwayo yokuyifaka ukuze isebenze kubasebenzisi.

Sebenzisa i-Nibomo uma ukufinyelela yonke ikhodi yomthombo ne-backend elawulwa umuntu oyisingathayo kuyisizathu sokushintsha, futhi amakhadi akho elula ngokwanele ukuba akhiwe kabusha ngokuphepha. [Vula uhlelo olusingathiwe](https://app.nibomo.com/) ukuze uzame iqoqo elincane lesikhashana. Gcina i-Anki—noma uzame i-RemNote, i-Mochi noma i-Mnemosyne—uma ukulondoloza isimo sokufunda ngokunembile kuyinto yokuqala.

## Yiluphi uhlelo olungena esikhundleni se-Anki olusebenza ku-iOS ngaphandle kwezimanga ezimbi zokuthutha?

“Uhlelo lwe-iOS olungena esikhundleni se-Anki” lungasho izinto ezimbili ezihlukene: uhlelo olwenzelwe i-iPhone ngqo, noma okungangena esikhundleni [sokuthenga i-AnkiMobile kanye ngo-$24.99](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ankimobile-flashcards/id373493387).

I-RemNote, i-Mochi, i-Quizlet, i-Brainscape, i-SuperMemo ne-Nibomo zonke zinezinhlelo ze-iOS. I-Mnemosyne ayinayo. Kodwa lokho akuwuphenduli umbuzo wokuthutha:

- **I-Mochi** igcina okuningi kunezinketho ze-iOS ezamukela umbhalo kuphela: ingenisa umlando wokubuyekeza we-`.apkg`, iguqulele ku-Markdown futhi isebenzise izilinganiso ezimbili.
- **I-RemNote** nayo ingenisa umlando wokubuyekeza we-`.apkg`, kodwa hlola umugqa wayo we-**Need to Learn** esikhundleni sokucabanga ukuthi uhlelo lwanamuhla lwe-Anki luzogcinwa.
- **I-Quizlet** ilungele ukusabalalisa izinto ekilasini, kodwa indlela yayo evela ku-Anki eyombhalo kuphela, kanti i-spaced repetition yayo entsha ayikafiki kuselula.
- **I-Nibomo** iyinketho enekhodi etholakalayo nohlelo olwenzelwe i-iOS ngqo, kodwa ukuthutha kwayo kusuka ku-Anki kubuyisela isimo sokufunda ekuqaleni.
- **I-Brainscape** ne-**SuperMemo** zifaneleka kuphela uma indlela yazo yokubuyekeza iwufanele umzamo wokwakha kabusha amakhadi nohlelo lwezikhathi.

Ngaphambi kokushiya i-AnkiMobile ngenxa yentengo, yiqhathanise nezindleko zokubhalisa namahora adingekayo ukulungisa ukuthutha. Ukuthenga uhlelo kanye kungashibha kunohlelo lwamahhala oluguqula iqoqo osulisebenzise isikhathi eside libe umsebenzi wezandla.

## Kunini lapho kuphephe khona ukugcina i-Anki

Ukuhlala kuyisinqumo, hhayi ukwehluleka ukukhetha into entsha. Gcina i-Anki uma okunye kwalokhu kuyiqiniso:

- iqoqo lakho lincike ezifanekisweni ezenziwe ngokwezifiso, ku-CSS, ku-JavaScript noma ezingezeni;
- i-Image Occlusion, umsindo noma enye imidiya ithwala incazelo ebalulekile;
- inothi elilodwa likhiqiza amakhadi abuzana ezinhlangothini eziningi okufanele ahlale exhumene;
- iminyaka yomlando wokubuyekeza nezinsuku zamanje ezimisiwe kunenani elikhulu kunesihleli esisha;
- uthembele endleleni yokusebenza kudeskithophu noma enhlanganisweni yamapulatifomu olunye uhlelo olungakwazi ukuyifinyelela;
- indlela yohlelo olusha yokusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi isebenza kuphela ohlotsheni lwedivayisi noma lwesixhumi ongeke ulusebenzise;
- uyawuthanda umqondo wokuzisingathela kodwa awufuni ukuqhuba, ukuvikela, ukwenza amakhophi ayisipele nokuthuthukisa iseva;
- olunye uhlelo alulungisi inkinga ephindaphindayo ngaphandle kokubukeka lucocekile.

I-Anki isanikeza izengezo eziningi ezithuthukiswe isikhathi eside, izifanekiso zamanothi namakhadi eziguquguqukayo, izilawuli zezikhathi zokubuyekeza ze-FSRS nezindlela zakudala, izinhlelo ezifakiwe ezigcina idatha endaweni namafomethi amaphakheji angathwala iqoqo. Awukho kule mikhiqizo engenhla olingisa konke lokho.

Ukuze uqhathanise ngokugxila emikhawulweni yobunikazi, funda [umhlahlandlela wezinhlelo zamakhadi okufunda ezinomthombo ovulekile](/blog/best-open-source-flashcard-apps-2026/). Uma ukusebenza ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kuyikona okunqumayo, [isiqhathaniso samakhadi okufunda ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi](/blog/best-offline-flashcards-app/) sihlukanisa izinhlelo ezifakiwe, okuqukethwe okugcinwe okwesikhashana namathebhu esiphequluli.

## Uhlu lokuhlola ukuthutha olusengaphetha ngokuthi “cha”

Ungaqali ngeqoqo lakho lonke. Hlela ukuhlola ongakwamukela ukuthi kwehluleke.

1. **Dala iphakheji yokubuyisela idatha.** Khipha i-`.colpkg` enemidiya, uyigcine ngaphandle kwephrofayela ye-Anki, bese ugcina ikhophi yesibili kwenye indawo.
2. **Khetha iqoqo eliyinkimbinkimbi.** Faka ama-cloze, izinkambu ezenziwe ngokwezifiso, amakhadi aya phambili naphendukezelwe, amaqoqo angaphakathi kwamanye, amathegi, izithombe, umsindo nomlando wokubuyekeza owanele ukuveza indlela uhlelo lwezikhathi oluguqulwa ngayo.
3. **Khipha ngefomethi uhlelo olwamukelayo oluyisekelayo ngempela.** Uma usebenzisa isingenisi esiqondile, sebenzisa i-`.apkg` enedatha yezikhathi zokubuyekeza, izilungiselelo ezigciniwe nemidiya. Sebenzisa u-Notes in Plain Text kuphela uma wamukela ukwakha kabusha okuqukethwe kuphela.
4. **Bhala isimo sokuqala ozosiqhathanisa naso.** Bhala inani lamanothi, inani lamakhadi, amagama amathegi namaqoqo, inani lamafayela emidiya, izinsuku ezimbalwa ezimisiwe nezikhawu, kanye nenani lamakhadi okulindeleke ukuthi likhiqizwe uhlobo ngalunye lwenothi.
5. **Ngenisa endaweni yesikhashana.** Ungabhali phezu kwephrofayela ewumthombo noma uhlanganise ukuhlola kokuqala nomtapo waphakade wohlelo olwamukelayo.
6. **Hlola okuqukethwe nesimo sokufunda ngokwehlukana.** Ingaphambili nengemuva okulungile akufakazeli ukuthi ama-cloze, imidiya, amakhadi aphuma enothini elilodwa, umlando wokubuyekeza noma usuku olulandelayo lokubuyekeza kulondolozekile.
7. **Vula uhlelo obeluvaliwe ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi kudivayisi ngayinye oyisebenzisayo.** Buyekeza, hlela, vala uhlelo ngokuphelele, uluvule kabusha ngaphandle koxhumano, bese uphinda uxhuma uhlole enye idivayisi.
8. **Hlola umugqa ngaphambi kokuthi izikhathi zokubuyekeza ziqale ukuhlukana.** Qhathanisa amakhadi okuqala asesifike isikhathi sokuwabuyekeza nezikhawu ezimbalwa ngaphambi kokulinganisa amakhadi afanayo owasebenzisa nsuku zonke kuzo zombili izinhlelo. Ngemva kokubuyekeza kokuqala kwenye indawo, izinhlelo ezimbili zezikhathi sezizimele.
9. **Hlola indlela yokuphuma ngaphambi kokuzibophezela.** Khipha idatha ohlelweni olwamukelayo bese uhlola lokho ongakuthola futhi uma ulushiya ngonyaka ozayo.
10. **Gcina i-Anki nekhophi eyisipele engakathintwa.** Ungasusi lutho kulokhu kuze kube uhlelo olusha seluphumelele ekusetshenzisweni okuvamile futhi usukwamukele ngokucophelela konke okulahlekile.

Uma uhlelo olwamukelayo lwamukela umbhalo kuphela, landela [indlela ephelele yokukhipha i-TXT ngokuphepha](/blog/migrate-from-anki-txt-export-open-source-flashcards/). Ihlukanisa i-`.colpkg` yokubuyisela idatha nefayela lomsebenzi elithuthekayo, ikwenze kucace ukuthi isimo sokufunda siqala phansi.

## Yenza isinqumo ngalolu hlelo

Qala ngalokho ongeke ukwazi ukukulahlekelwa:

1. Uma izifanekiso eziqondile, izengezo noma umugqa wamanje kubalulekile, gcina i-Anki ngaphandle uma i-`.apkg` emele iqoqo lakho ifakazela okuhlukile.
2. Uma amanothi namakhadi kufanele abe uhlelo olulodwa, zama i-RemNote. Hlola umugqa we-**Need to Learn**, hhayi amakhasi angenisiwe kuphela.
3. Uma i-Markdown egcinwe endaweni namafayela akhishiwe ongawahlola kubaluleke ngaphezu kwendlela i-Anki ebonisa ngayo amakhadi, zama i-Mochi.
4. Uma ufuna uhlelo lwedeskithophu olugxilile, olunomthombo ovulekile nokungenisa okuqondile kusuka ku-Anki, zama i-Mnemosyne—bese uqinisekisa ukuthi imikhawulo yayo kuselula iyahambisana nenqubo yakho.
5. Uma inkinga yangempela kuwukusabalalisa izinto ekilasini noma indlela elula yokubuyekeza ngokuhlanganyela, yakha kabusha isethi encane ku-Quizlet noma ku-Brainscape.
6. Uma ufuna indlela ye-SuperMemo ngokukhethekile, yamukela uhlelo lwezikhathi olusha. Uma ufuna ngokukhethekile umthombo wazo zonke izingxenye, ukuzisingathela nokufinyelela nge-API ne-MCP, yamukela ukwakha kabusha okuqukethwe ku-Nibomo okulahlekelwa enye idatha kanye nomsebenzi wokuyiqhuba.

Ukuze ubone ukuqhathaniswa kwesici ngasinye kwezindlela ezintathu ezihluke kakhulu, bheka [i-Anki ne-Quizlet ne-Nibomo](/blog/anki-vs-quizlet-vs-open-source-flashcards-app/).

Umthetho owusizo ulula: shintsha uma inzuzo icacile futhi ukuhlola kwangempela kukhombisile ukuthi ukulahleka kuyamukeleka. Uma iqoqo elimele amakhadi akho lingathutheleki kahle ohlelweni olusha, ukugcina i-Anki akukhona nje ukubalekela ushintsho. Kungumphumela wokuqhathanisa.
